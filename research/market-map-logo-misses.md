# Market-map logo misses

Empty. All 111 market-map companies now have a registered mark under
`public/images/logos/` and a `logo` id on the company row.

Licence was not used as a skip reason. Official marks without a named
reuse grant are registered as `unlicensed` (or `unknown`) with creator
and source URL. Do not invent a Commons licence.
