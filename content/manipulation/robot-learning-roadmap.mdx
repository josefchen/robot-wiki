## Stage 1: frames and calibration

Start with [kinematics](/classical/kinematics/): which frame a pose or tool motion is written in, and where joint limits apply. *Modern Robotics* supplies the vocabulary <Cite id="modern-robotics-2017" />, and Tedrake warns that sloppy notation "will inevitably lead to confusion and bugs" <Cite id="tedrake-manipulation-2026" />. A drifting camera transform or timestamp mismatch can make a good policy look random, so [calibration](/classical/calibration/) and [state estimation](/classical/state-estimation/) come next; Kalibr estimates sensor time offsets to better than one sample <Cite id="kalibr-2013" />.

## Stage 2: data as a system

Each step of a robot dataset pairs synchronized observations with commanded and achieved actions, robot state, task, episode end and origin, and RLDS stores such episodes without loss <Cite id="rlds-2021" />. Of public [datasets](/data-hardware/datasets/), Open X-Embodiment pools 22 robots from 21 institutions <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />, and DROID holds 76k trajectories from 13 institutions <Cite id="droid-2024" />. By November 2026 the Hugging Face Hub held almost 300 TB of open robot data in LeRobot format <Cite id="lerobot-v0-7-2026" />, and LeRobot spans the [robot-learning stack](/data-hardware/robot-learning-stack/) from motors to dataset streaming <Cite id="lerobot-paper-2026" />.

Record and replay ten episodes before scaling, checking timestamps, units and rotations. Past a threshold per environment or object, more demonstrations add little and variety matters more <Cite id="lin-data-scaling-laws-2024" />.

## Stage 3: an imitation baseline

[Behavior cloning](/manipulation/bc-foundations/) trains on the expert's states, then visits its own. DAgger showed why errors compound and why labels on the learner's own states fix this <Cite id="dagger-2011" />; HG-DAgger collects them by letting a person take over when the robot errs <Cite id="hg-dagger-2019" />. Start small, with one camera, task, action format and simple network, and debug alignment, normalization and reset variety before scaling. robomimic found results sensitive to design choices, demonstration quality and when training stops <Cite id="robomimic-2021" />. Then add structure over time: ACT predicts action chunks <Cite id="act-aloha-2023" />, and Diffusion Policy models the several valid ways to do a task <Cite id="diffusion-policy-2023" />.

## Stage 4: the action contract

Among [action spaces](/manipulation/action-spaces/), joint positions belong to one mechanism, while tool-pose changes mean the same on arms with compatible controllers. Rounding each action number into a token handles fast, dexterous data poorly <Cite id="pi0-fast-2025" />, and in contact-rich RL, variable stiffness in tool space improved sample efficiency, energy use and safety <Cite id="martin-martin-2019" />. The format fixes labels, controller, failure modes and which robots can share data, so change it separately from the model.

## Stage 5: pretrained models

A [pretrained VLA](/manipulation/foundation-models/) helps when language, new objects or many tasks matter. OpenVLA made the recipe reproducible <Cite id="openvla-2024" />, π0 pretrains on over 10,000 hours of robot data <Cite id="pi0-2024" />, and SmolVLA trains on one GPU yet performs comparably to VLAs ten times larger <Cite id="smolvla-2025" />. In TRI's blind, randomized trials, pretraining taught new tasks with a fraction of the data <Cite id="tri-lbm-2025" />. NVIDIA reports five demonstrations per task and a 30-minute fine-tune on one RTX 5090 lifting GR00T 2 from 40% to 95% success on 50 new tasks <Cite id="gr00t-2-2026" />.

## Stage 6: evaluation

Define success, failure, intervention, reset and timeout before the main run, and test familiar setups apart from ones with changed objects or lighting. Papers often omit run counts, starting conditions, success criteria or statistics <Cite id="kress-gazit-evaluation-2024" />, so report trial counts and uncertainty; STEP's sequential test needs up to 32% fewer trials <Cite id="optimal-stopping-2025" />. Measure latency: in simulation, 100 ms of delay nearly halved success on moving targets <Cite id="vla-lagbench-2026" />.

Error on recorded data is a diagnostic; robot success under controlled changes is the measure. LIBERO-Plus perturbs LIBERO <Cite id="libero-2023" /> tasks in seven ways, such as viewpoint, lighting and sensor noise <Cite id="libero-plus-2025" />. SIMPLER's simulated scores correlate with real performance <Cite id="simpler-2024" />, and RoboArena's double-blind head-to-head trials rank generalists better than centralized evaluation <Cite id="roboarena-2025" />.

## Stage 7: simulation and RL

Simulation tests controllers and lets reward be optimized safely; domain randomization varies the simulation until reality looks like one more variation <Cite id="tobin-2017" />. Isaac Lab targets large-scale robot learning <Cite id="isaac-lab-2025" />, and its 3.0 release separates tasks from physics engines <Cite id="isaac-lab-3-0-2026" />. The hardware gap still has to be measured.

<Image id="robocasa-kitchens-2024" />

Offline RL learns from logged data alone <Cite id="offline-rl-tutorial-2020" />. Online RL needs safe exploration, automatic resets and cheap evaluation, which SERL bundles with an off-policy learner <Cite id="serl-2024" />; without them, start with <Term id="imitation-learning">imitation learning</Term>. With them, real-arm [RL fine-tuning](/manipulation/rl-finetuning/) reached 100% success on precise tasks within 1 to 2.5 hours <Cite id="hil-serl-2024" />, and in simulation SimpleVLA-RL lifted OpenVLA-OFT, trained on one demonstration per task, from 48.9% to 96.9% average LIBERO success <Cite id="simplevla-rl-2025" />.

## A practical sequence

Build replayable data and a deterministic evaluation harness, then a behavior-cloning baseline. Add action chunks when the baseline needs them, a pretrained VLA for new objects and instructions, and RL once reward, coverage and resets are real. For deployment, [ROS 2](/classical/ros2-for-ml-engineers/) adds the production-grade features ROS 1 lacked <Cite id="ros2-science-robotics-2022" />.
