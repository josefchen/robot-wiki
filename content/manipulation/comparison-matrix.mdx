## Use the matrix as a constraint solver

Remove models whose weights or training data the project cannot use (Gemini Robotics On-Device offers trusted-tester SDK access <Cite id="gemini-robotics-on-device-2025" />), then models whose action representation, latency, observations or adaptation-data needs mismatch the robot, before comparing benchmark performance.

<Image id="roboarena-evaluation-2025" />

Compare survivors with one protocol, identical cameras, action horizon and resets; keep a behavior-cloning or action-chunking baseline. TRI's Large Behavior Model study used blind, randomized trials <Cite id="tri-lbm-2025" />; RoboArena ranked seven generalist policies more accurately than centralized evaluation through over 600 double-blind pairwise real-robot episodes <Cite id="roboarena-2025" />. The [foundation-model guide](/manipulation/foundation-models/) explains family priors, the [action-space guide](/manipulation/action-spaces/) output compatibility, and the [evaluation crisis](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis/) why protocols resist shared leaderboards.

## The eight axes

Action representation: continuous regression, discrete action tokens or generative heads, including diffusion or <Term id="flow-matching">flow matching</Term>. Supervision and runtime generation can differ: π0.5 trains with FAST tokens but generates continuous actions with flow matching <Cite id="pi05-2025" />.

Distinguish planned chunk length $H$, the <Term id="predicted-horizon">predicted horizon</Term>, from steps executed before re-planning, the <Term id="committed-horizon">committed horizon</Term>. Diffusion Policy's simulated CNN Push-T reference uses observation, prediction and execution horizons of 2, 16 and 8 <Cite id="diffusion-policy-2023" />; ACT's 100-step entry is its k=100 chunk-ablation reference <Cite id="act-aloha-2023" />; OFT+ predicts and executes 25 actions at 25 Hz on ALOHA <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />; π0.7 predicts 50 with five denoising steps, executing 15 or 25 <Cite id="pi07-2026" />. [Real-time chunking](/manipulation/realtime-execution/) generates the next chunk while the current one executes <Cite id="real-time-chunking-2025" />.

Control frequency: specify conditions. ACT's ALOHA runs at 50 Hz with <Term id="temporal-ensembling">temporal ensembling</Term> <Cite id="act-aloha-2023" />, RT-1 commands Everyday Robots' platform at 3 Hz <Cite id="rt1-2022" />, and RT-2's multi-TPU cloud service runs PaLI-X-55B at 1 to 3 Hz <Cite id="rt2-2023" />. OpenVLA reaches about 6 Hz on one RTX 4090; its Franka fine-tuning setups use 5 Hz and 15 Hz controllers <Cite id="openvla-2024" />.

Gemini Robotics turns about 250 ms end-to-end latency into effective 50 Hz control frequency with multi-action chunks <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2025" />, GR00T N1's 16-action chunk sampling takes 63.9 ms on an L40 <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" />, π0.6's card: 63 ms per chunk on one H100 <Cite id="pi06-model-card-2025" />; Generalist says GEN-1.5 produces 100 Hz action trajectories <Cite id="gen-1-5-2026" />.

<ChunkBuffer className="my-6" />

Backbone, conditioning and cross-embodiment: components, inputs and robot-body transfer scope. Mixed-embodiment training, one checkpoint across bodies and new-body fine-tuning are different evidence; CrossFormer gives the strongest evidence: identical weights control very different robots after 900K training trajectories from 20 embodiments <Cite id="crossformer-2024" />. Skild says S1 executes a task shown in one video <Cite id="skild-s1-2026" />: demonstration video conditioning.

Hierarchy: internal high-level stages versus separate systems. π0.5 predicts a semantic subtask before low-level actions <Cite id="pi05-2025" />; the N1.7 SONIC workflow's separate whole-body controller decodes VLA latent action tokens into joint commands <Cite id="isaac-gr00t-repo-2026" />. A separate high-level VLM gives π0 subtasks <Cite id="pi0-2024" />.

Weights separates download availability from license. openpi lists π0, π0-FAST and π0.5 checkpoints <Cite id="openpi-repo-2024" />; N1.7's README separates Apache 2.0 code and NVIDIA Open Model License weights <Cite id="isaac-gr00t-repo-2026" />; SmolVLA releases code, pretrained models and training data <Cite id="smolvla-2025" />.

## Row notes

RT-1's "limited" cross-embodiment training mixes Kuka IIWA and Everyday Robots data; evaluation uses Everyday Robots <Cite id="rt1-2022" />. ACT trains task-specific roughly 80M-parameter models from scratch <Cite id="act-aloha-2023" />; RT-2's action-only entry excludes its chain-of-thought variant <Cite id="rt2-2023" />.

<Image id="gemini-robotics-apollo-franka-2025" />

OpenVLA's 7B denotes its language model, beside a 600M visual encoder <Cite id="openvla-2024" />. OFT's "limited" means pretrained-model adaptation to separate tasks and robot setups <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />; GR00T N1.6 predicts state-relative action chunks for most embodiments <Cite id="gr00t-n1-6-2025" />. π*0.6's Recap post-training more than doubles throughput on some of the hardest tasks <Cite id="pistar06-2025" />.

Gemini Robotics 1.5's card discloses continuous numerical actions, omitting action-head family, chunk length, command rate or parameter count; one checkpoint spans ALOHA, bi-arm Franka and Apollo; separate GR-ER 1.5 orchestrators are optional <Cite id="gemini-robotics-15-2025" />. DeepMind's July 30, 2026 Gemini Robotics 2 announcement covers Apollo 2 and Franka Duo with one checkpoint but omits backbone, output parameterization, chunk length, control frequency or parameter count <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2-2026" />. Skild's row: its January 14, 2026 Series C announcement, without reproducible cross-embodiment evaluation or runtime details <Cite id="skild-series-c-2026" />.

## What the matrix can establish

ACT fixes CVAE latents to the prior mean, zero, to decode deterministically <Cite id="act-aloha-2023" />; π0's flow-matching expert generates continuous action chunks <Cite id="pi0-2024" />. Generalist says GEN-1's base trains on wearable human data without robot data and averages 99% success versus earlier models' 64%, with 1 hour of robot data per task <Cite id="generalist-gen1-2026" />. On March 16, 2026, NVIDIA previewed GR00T N2, based on DreamZero <Cite id="nvidia-gtc-gr00t-n2-2026" />.

## Helix 02 and GO-2 disclosure limits

Figure announced Helix 02's S1 joint targets at 200 Hz and S0 actuator commands at 1 kHz, but no output-head training loss, chunk horizon or weight-release terms <Cite id="helix-02-2026" />. Helix's first post disclosed a 7 to 9 Hz onboard VLM and 80M-parameter S1 at 200 Hz, trained end-to-end with standard regression loss <Cite id="helix-2025" />; Figure reports Index human-video pretraining raised Helix 2.5's zero-shot success in 30 unseen homes from 9% to 56% <Cite id="helix-2-5-2026" />. AgiBot describes lower-frequency System 2 planning and higher-frequency System 1 action following, without numerical rate, backbone, output encoding, chunk length or weight terms <Cite id="agibot-go2-2026" />; predecessor GO-1 uses latent action representations trained on AgiBot World's over 1 million trajectories across 217 tasks <Cite id="agibot-world-2025" />.
