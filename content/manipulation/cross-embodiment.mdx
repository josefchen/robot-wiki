<Term id="cross-embodiment">Cross-embodiment transfer</Term> is training one policy on data from bodies with different joint counts, action dimensions and sensors, or moving a policy to an unseen body. Open X-Embodiment pools data from 22 robot embodiments <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />, and human video adds a body whose actions are estimated from images <Cite id="egoscale-2026" />.

## Counting robot and human data

π0's 10,000 hours are its own data from 7 robot configurations and 68 tasks; by timesteps, the open-source OXE, DROID and Bridge data are 9.1% of the mixture <Cite id="pi0-2024" />. RT-X experiments used 9 of OXE's 22 embodiments <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />, and GR00T N1.7's rounded 20K pretraining hours are EgoScale human video <Cite id="isaac-gr00t-repo-2026" />.

Moritz Reuss wrote in October 2025 that "OXE is mostly low-quality data" and that imitation learning lacks good ways to measure [data quality](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck/) <Cite id="oxe-quality-critique-2026" />. Re-Mix's learned mixture weights beat uniform weights by 38% on average <Cite id="remix-2024" />, and Toyota Research Institute's controlled study of 89 policies found co-training with cross-embodiment robot data improves generalization to distribution shifts and unseen tasks <Cite id="lbm-cotraining-2026" />.

## Robot action interfaces

π0 zero-pads every action vector to the largest robot's 18 dimensions <Cite id="pi0-2024" />. Octo curates 25 <Term id="open-x-embodiment">OXE</Term> datasets with delta <Term id="end-effector">end-effector</Term> control and aligns grippers to one convention, adding lightweight encoders or heads for new spaces <Cite id="octo-2024" />, while CrossFormer trains one transformer on 900K trajectories from 20 embodiments without manually aligning observation or action spaces <Cite id="crossformer-2024" />.

HPT maps each embodiment's proprioception and vision to a few tokens for a shared trunk, scaling to 52 datasets <Cite id="hpt-2024" />. LAP writes low-level actions as natural language, and LAP-3B reports over 50% average zero-shot success on unseen robots, about twice the strongest prior VLAs <Cite id="lap-2026" />.

## Motion Transfer across robot embodiments

Gemini Robotics 1.5 trains on ALOHA, bi-arm Franka and Apptronik Apollo data plus Internet text, images and video, with Motion Transfer, an architecture and training recipe that aligns embodiments. On tasks demonstrated only on another robot it helps, less so for Apollo, in ablations run interleaved on one work cell; one checkpoint drives all three robots, though the long-horizon agent adds bi-arm Franka post-training, and the report specifies an orchestrator over the <Term id="vision-language-action-model">VLA</Term> but not the alignment representation <Cite id="gemini-robotics-15-2025" />. DeepMind says tasks shown only to ALOHA 2 "just work" on Apollo and bi-arm Franka, and vice versa <Cite id="gemini-robotics-15-blog-2025" />.

DeepMind says On-Device 2, natively multi-embodiment with Motion Transfer, adapts to new bi-arm robots in a few hours, typically from fewer than 200 examples <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2-2026" />.

## Relative end-effector actions for robots and humans

GR00T N1 embedded state and action with embodiment-aware encoders <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" />; N1.6 moved most embodiments to state-relative action chunks, which NVIDIA found smoother and more accurate than absolute actions <Cite id="gr00t-n1-6-2025" />. N1.7 shares relative end-effector deltas between human and robot embodiments and calls this a key factor in cross-embodiment performance <Cite id="isaac-gr00t-repo-2026" />.

<Image id="egoscale-human-to-robot-2026" />

EgoScale uses relative wrist motion and <Term id="retargeting">retargets</Term> 21 human hand keypoints to the 22-DoF Sharpa hand, with embodiment-conditioned adapters for <Term id="proprioception">proprioception</Term> and hand actions around a shared backbone and action expert <Cite id="egoscale-2026" />. Morphometric Imitation (September 2026) retargets reconstructed hand-object interactions across [hand morphologies](/frontier/dexterity/), then refines them with residual RL; on a Sharpa hand its policies reached 89.3% zero-shot success in 300 real trials on 30 objects <Cite id="morphometric-imitation-2026" />.

## Closing the visual embodiment gap

Mirage masks out an unseen target robot and inpaints the source robot in real time <Cite id="mirage-2024" />. RoVi-Aug synthesizes training images with other robots and viewpoints and deploys zero-shot on an unseen robot, and co-training with it raises success by up to 30% <Cite id="rovi-aug-2024" />.

## Latent actions as a shared interface

LAPA learns discrete latent actions between video frames, pretrains a VLA on them and fine-tunes on robot data; training only on human video still transfers positively <Cite id="lapa-2025" />. LAC-WM (Stanford and Meta FAIR, October 2026) conditions a robot world model on a latent action space shared across embodiments; picking action chunks for a VLA on five unseen LIBERO tasks, it raised average success from 82.4% with an explicit-action world model to 92.0% <Cite id="lac-wm-2026" />.

## Human-to-robot transfer recipes

Simar Kareer's EgoMimic treated human and robot data equally and found an extra hour of hand data worth more than one of robot data <Cite id="egomimic-2024" />. On December 16, 2025, Kareer and Physical Intelligence colleagues co-fine-tuned π0.5 on human data, with 3D hand positions as actions, and the most relevant robot data. PI reports about 2× performance on four scenarios present only in human data, without trial counts, and transfer that improves as robot pretraining diversifies <Cite id="pi-human-to-robot-2025" />.

<Image id="phantom-human-video-robot-2025" />

EgoScale's Ruijie Zheng and coauthors instead pretrain on human video, mid-train on aligned human-robot data and post-train on task robot data, and find adding aligned mid-training best overall <Cite id="egoscale-2026" />. EgoBridge aligns human and robot policy latents with optimal transport and reports a 44% absolute success gain over human-augmented cross-embodiment baselines <Cite id="egobridge-2025" />. EgoLAP (Princeton, Toyota Research Institute and Physical Intelligence, October 2026) routes human and robot trajectories through a shared chain of language actions and reached 80.1% mean real-world task progress on bimanual YAM robots, 2.3 times the alternative action representations <Cite id="egolap-2026" />.

Phantom needs no robot data: it inpaints the human arm, overlays a rendered robot and deploys zero-shot, with success up to 92% <Cite id="phantom-2025" />. Masquerade robotizes in-the-wild human video and fine-tunes on 50 [robot demonstrations](/data-hardware/teleop-rigs/) per task, beating baselines by 5 to 6 times <Cite id="masquerade-2026" />. Figure reports that Index human-video pretraining, all else fixed, raised Helix 2.5's zero-shot success in 30 unseen homes from 9% to 56% <Cite id="helix-2-5-2026" />, and Generalist's GEN-1 base model pretrains on over half a million hours from wearable devices on humans, with no robot data <Cite id="generalist-gen1-2026" />.

## Scaling the number of embodiments

Across about 1,000 procedurally generated locomotion bodies, adding embodiments generalizes more broadly than adding data on fixed ones <Cite id="embodiment-scaling-2025" />. Skild reports training one model on 100,000 simulated robots and testing zero-shot on robots excluded from training <Cite id="skild-omni-bodied-2025" />. DreamZero adapted from AgiBot G1 to a YAM bimanual robot with 55 trajectories, about 30 minutes of play data, though its authors describe the result only qualitatively <Cite id="dreamzero-2026" />.
