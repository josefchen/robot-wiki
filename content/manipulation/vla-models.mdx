A <Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action (VLA)</Term> model is a robot controller made by teaching a web-trained vision-language model to output movements. Its knowledge of objects, words and space carries over to robots, which have far less data of their own <Cite id="rt2-2023" />.

<Image id="gemini-robotics-aloha-dexterous-2025" />

## A VLA writes a move the way it writes a sentence

A running VLA reads the cameras and the instruction ("pick up the apple"), writes the next move, lets the robot carry it out and looks again. A move is a few numbers: how far to shift, turn and open the gripper. The classic design rounds each to one of 256 levels with its own token <Cite id="rt2-2023" />.

<ActionTokenization className="my-6" />

[Behavior cloning](/manipulation/bc-foundations/) and [Diffusion Policy](/manipulation/diffusion-policy/) learn the same loop from robot demonstrations alone. A VLA starts out knowing what an apple is.

## RT-1 turned actions into tokens

RT-1 (2022) learned hundreds of real manipulation tasks with one <Term id="transformer">transformer</Term>. Trained on about 130,000 demonstrations from 13 robots, it performed over 700 training instructions with 97% success, and 76% of unseen ones <Cite id="rt1-2022" />.

## RT-2 brought web knowledge to the robot

RT-2 (2023) <Term id="fine-tuning">fine-tuned</Term> web-trained <Term id="vision-language-model">vision-language models</Term> of up to 55 billion parameters to write actions as number text, keeping web data in the mix <Cite id="rt2-2023" />. In about 6,000 real trials it matched RT-1 on familiar tasks and roughly doubled success on unseen ones. With step-by-step reasoning it chose a rock as an improvised hammer <Cite id="rt2-2023" />.

Also: Gemini Robotics 1.5's reasoning between actions <Cite id="gemini-robotics-15-2025" />; VLA-0's plain-text actions, which beat same-data rivals on LIBERO <Cite id="vla-0-2025" />.

## Open X-Embodiment pooled 22 kinds of robot

<Term id="open-x-embodiment">Open X-Embodiment</Term> (OXE, 2023) converted 60 datasets from 34 labs into one format: over a million real episodes from 22 kinds of robot. RT-X is RT-1 and RT-2 retrained on data from nine of them <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />.

<Image id="open-x-embodiment-overview-2023" />

<Term id="cross-embodiment">Cross-embodiment</Term> data helped small datasets most: RT-1-X beat each lab's own model on four of five, with 50% higher mean success. For skills seen only in another arm's data, RT-2-X reached 75.8% against RT-2's 27.3% <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />.

The mix matters. Across 89 <Term id="co-training">co-trained</Term> policies, TRI found that vision-language and cross-robot data substantially improved generalization, while discrete action tokens brought no significant benefit <Cite id="lbm-cotraining-2026" />. Re-Mix's learned dataset weights beat an even mix by 38% on average <Cite id="remix-2024" />.

Also: X-VLA's per-source soft prompts <Cite id="x-vla-2025" />; the verdict that "OXE is mostly low-quality data" <Cite id="oxe-quality-critique-2026" />; Xiaomi-Robotics-1, 57.4% on RoboCasa365 from handheld-gripper data <Cite id="xiaomi-robotics-1-2026" /> <Cite id="xiaomi-robotics-1-repo-2026" />.

## Octo and OpenVLA opened the recipe

Octo, trained on 800,000 OXE trajectories, takes words or goal images and fine-tunes within hours on a consumer GPU <Cite id="octo-2024" />.

<Image id="octo-evaluation-setups-2024" />

OpenVLA, trained on 970,000 demonstrations, beat RT-2-X by 16.5 points with seven times fewer parameters <Cite id="openvla-2024" />.

Also: SmolVLA, trained on one GPU <Cite id="smolvla-2025" />; CogACT's separate diffusion action model <Cite id="cogact-2024" />.

## Writing actions as words is slow

Each token waits for the one before it. OpenVLA writes seven tokens per step <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />, about 6 moves a second on a gaming GPU <Cite id="openvla-2024" />. PI researchers likened it to "controlling your arm by verbally saying which muscles should contract" <Cite id="knowledge-insulation-2025" />.

PI's FAST tokenizer compresses motion with the discrete cosine transform and matched diffusion VLAs while training up to five times faster <Cite id="pi0-fast-2025" />. With stable dynamics and a smooth policy, the cost of rounding grows only polynomially with task length <Cite id="bc-action-quantization-2026" />.

The alternative is an <Term id="action-chunking">action chunk</Term> of continuous moves: π0's action expert generates one by <Term id="flow-matching">flow matching</Term> at up to 50 moves a second <Cite id="pi0-2024" />. OpenVLA-OFT made OpenVLA output chunks at 109.7 actions a second against 4.2. With a wrist camera and joint readings, its LIBERO <Cite id="libero-2023" /> success rose from 76.5% to 97.1% <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />.

Also: PD-VLA's parallel decoding, 2.52 times the action rate <Cite id="pd-vla-2025" />.

## High benchmark scores hide brittleness

Models scoring 95% on LIBERO fell below 30% under modest changes such as a new camera angle, and often ignored the instruction <Cite id="libero-plus-2025" />. Papers differ in actions, data and protocols, so their numbers rarely compare <Cite id="rt2-2023" /> <Cite id="openvla-2024" />.

People underspecify: their instructions grow from 9.1 words for one-step tasks to only 15.8 for three or more steps. Told only the intent, π0.5 lost 15.2 points from its 38.0% on RoboLab's 120 simulated tasks <Cite id="commgap-2026" />.

Also: RoboArena's double-blind trials <Cite id="roboarena-2025" />; SIMPLER's sim-to-real correlation <Cite id="simpler-2024" />; the [evaluation crisis](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis/).

## Newer systems split the work or predict video

GR00T N1 pairs a vision-language "System 2" that reads the scene with a fast diffusion "System 1" that moves <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" />. π0.7 adds strategy notes and subgoal images to the instruction and folded laundry on a robot type it never trained on <Cite id="pi07-2026" />. PI serves its models through an API beta <Cite id="pi-api-beta-2026" />.

| System | Reported result |
| --- | --- |
| Gemini Robotics 2 | Controls full humanoids <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2-2026" /> |
| Gemini Robotics 2.5, partners only | 30 to 100% higher success than earlier baselines on over 200 tasks <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2-5-2026" /> |
| GR00T 2, predicts video | 40% on 50 tasks with no target-robot demonstrations; 95% after five each, on tasks picked because five sufficed <Cite id="gr00t-2-2026" /> |
| Runway Praxis-1, open weights | 46.2% on RoboLab-120 against π0.5's 28.0% <Cite id="runway-praxis-1-open-2026" /> <Cite id="robolab-2026" /> |
| Apple AMP1, no VLA | 85.6% on RoboLab-120 with no demonstrations, against 49.2% for the leading VLA <Cite id="apple-amp1-2026" /> |

## Choose the prior and the interface separately

A VLA is a prior, what the model already knows, plus a control interface: action format, horizon and speed. OFT made OpenVLA faster and more successful without replacing the prior <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />.
