Foundation models are machine learning models that are pretrained at scale on broad datasets and then adapted to a wide range of tasks <Cite id="bommasani-foundation-models-2021" />. In the context of robotics, that means <Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action models</Term> as well as world models, and the litmus test is whether one checkpoint can transfer to new tasks, environments or robots with little additional data.

## Four things a claim must name

A claim should say what the model is trained on, what it is predicting, what it outputs and how much data is required to train on a new task. Open X-Embodiment aggregates data from 22 embodiments <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />; GR00T 2 trains on 402,730 hours of first-person human video along with 23,500 hours of teleoperation data <Cite id="gr00t-2-2026" />.

## Models differ most in what a new task costs

Most of the numbers are self-reported.

| Model | Data for a new task | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RoboCat <Cite id="robocat-2023" /> | 100 to 1,000 examples | New tasks and robots |
| Gemini Robotics <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2025" /> | 100 demonstrations | New short tasks |
| Octo <Cite id="octo-2024" /> | Hours of fine-tuning on consumer GPUs | New sensors |
| GR00T 2 <Cite id="gr00t-2-2026" /> | None, or five demonstrations | 40% or 95% on 50 tasks |
| Skild AI S1 <Cite id="skild-s1-2026" /> | One video, weights unchanged | 66% on an unseen long task, like fine-tuning on 380 demonstrations |
| GEN-1.5 <Cite id="gen-1-5-2026" /> | One 3 to 12 second demonstration | Learns the task |
| Frozen DreamZero plus a retrieved video <Cite id="robota-embed-2026" /> | One video | 47.0% to 56.3% on three simulated DROID <Cite id="droid-2024" /> tasks |

Also: π*0.6, whose practice more than doubles throughput on some of the hardest tasks <Cite id="pistar06-2025" />; Physical Intelligence offers fine-tuning through its API <Cite id="pi-api-beta-2026" />.

## Vision-language-action models start from web knowledge

RT-2 co-trained a vision-language model on web and robot data and used special tokens to represent actions <Cite id="rt2-2023" />. π0 adds an action expert for continuous actions <Cite id="pi0-2024" />, while π0.5 emphasizes task diversity as key to its generalization <Cite id="pi05-2025" />.

New setups still need tuning: the OpenVLA-OFT fine-tuning procedure improved OpenVLA <Cite id="openvla-2024" /> from 76.5% to 97.1% on four simulated task suites <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />.

Also: RT-1 <Cite id="rt1-2022" />, the steerable π0.7 <Cite id="pi07-2026" /> and Kyutai's open REKI <Cite id="reki-2026" />.

## Memory lets a policy use the past

Policies that condition only on the current observation cannot solve tasks that require remembering previously observed information.

| Model | Memory | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T²Mem <Cite id="t2mem-2026" /> | Weights updated while the policy runs | Learned from actions alone |
| RoboTTT <Cite id="robottt-2026" /> | The same, over 8K timesteps | 79% on long real tasks, against 42% for GR00T N1.7 |
| ECoMEM <Cite id="ecomem-2026" /> | A written record of task facts | 86.1% on two new real tasks, against 8.6% without |
| IMPRINT <Cite id="imprint-2026" /> | A learned summary of past frames and actions | π0.5 from 25.4% to 72.8% on nine simulated memory tasks |

## Humanoids: the body limits what transfers

GR00T N1 couples a vision-language model with a diffusion-based action generator <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" />. LeVERB sends learned commands to a separate whole-body controller <Cite id="leverb-2025" />.

Rather than pretraining on robot data, pretraining can be done on human data. Figure's [Helix 2.5](/manipulation/generalist-policies/), which uses Index of human behavior as its pretraining dataset, reached 56% zero-shot success in 30 unseen homes (9% for a model trained from scratch) <Cite id="helix-2-5-2026" />. In a proof of concept with Mercedes-Benz, Gemini Robotics 2.5 achieved a 96% success rate kitting parts on Apptronik's Apollo 2 <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2-5-2026" />.

Also: WholeBodyVLA, which learns from unlabeled video <Cite id="wholebodyvla-2025" />.

## World models learn how scenes change

V-JEPA 2 was trained on more than 1M hours of video from the internet, then on less than 62 hours of DROID robot video to learn the effect of actions <Cite id="vjepa2-2025" />. Dreamer 4, trained inside such a model, was the first agent to obtain diamonds in Minecraft purely from recorded data <Cite id="dreamer4-2025" />.

<Image id="cosmos-policy-aloha-2026" />

Cosmos Policy uses NVIDIA's general world model, Cosmos <Cite id="cosmos-predict-2025" />, as its backbone, and achieves 98.5% on the LIBERO <Cite id="cosmos-policy-2026" />.

Also: R3M, which uses Ego4D <Cite id="ego4d-2022" /> video <Cite id="r3m-2022" />, Cosmos 3 with actions <Cite id="cosmos-3-2026" /> and Ctrl-World for policy ranking <Cite id="ctrl-world-2025" />.

## World action models predict video and actions together

A [world action model](/world-models/taxonomy/) jointly models future video and the actions that produce it using one network. DreamZero is a 14-billion-parameter model that operates at 7 Hz, and on unseen environments and tasks it achieves more than double the average task progress of leading VLAs <Cite id="dreamzero-2026" />. Runway's open Praxis-1 reports 46.2% on RoboLab-120 <Cite id="robolab-2026" />, compared to 28.0% for π0.5 <Cite id="runway-praxis-1-open-2026" />.

Also: GR00T N2 <Cite id="nvidia-gtc-gr00t-n2-2026" />, GR00T 2 <Cite id="nvidia-corl-gr00t-2-2026" /> and Dyna-2 <Cite id="dyna-2-2026" />.

## Pretraining saves data but does not remove the need

In Toyota Research Institute's blind, randomized trials, pretrained policies matched from-scratch ones with 3 to 5 times less data <Cite id="tri-lbm-2025" />. Generalist's GEN-1, pretrained with over half a million hours of human data, achieved on average 99% success on three tasks with about an hour of robot data for each task, whereas training from scratch achieved 19% <Cite id="generalist-gen1-2026" />.

Training with only robot data harms visual-language understanding in the backbone <Cite id="lbm-cotraining-2026" />. Training a new action expert in a naive manner also negatively impacts transfer <Cite id="knowledge-insulation-paper-2025" /> ([knowledge insulation](/manipulation/knowledge-insulation/)). RoboPAIR jailbroke robots with language-model controllers, including a deployed Unitree Go2, with attack success rates often of 100% <Cite id="robopair-2024" />.

Also: VC-1, no visual representation best everywhere <Cite id="vc1-2023" />.

## Single scores hide brittleness

Convincing claims vary tasks, environments, robots and amounts of adaptation data, and compare against an equal-budget from-scratch baseline. VLAs that report near 95% success rates on LIBERO drop below 30% with minor changes in camera or start positions <Cite id="libero-plus-2025" />. Some models obtain greater than 90% success rate on LIBERO but degrade to 0.0% on LIBERO-PRO <Cite id="libero-pro-2025" />.
