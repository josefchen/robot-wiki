A foundation model is a network trained on broad data at scale, then adapted to many tasks <Cite id="bommasani-foundation-models-2021" />. In robotics that means <Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action models</Term> and world models, and the test is whether one checkpoint carries to new tasks, places or robots with little new data.

## Four things a claim must name

A claim should say what the model learned from, what it predicts, what it outputs and how much data a new task needs. Open X-Embodiment pools 22 kinds of robot <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />; GR00T 2 adds 402,730 hours of first-person human video to 23,500 hours of teleoperation <Cite id="gr00t-2-2026" />.

## Models differ most in what a new task costs

Most figures are self-reported.

| Model | Data for a new task | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RoboCat <Cite id="robocat-2023" /> | 100 to 1,000 examples | New tasks and robots |
| Gemini Robotics <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2025" /> | 100 demonstrations | New short tasks |
| Octo <Cite id="octo-2024" /> | Hours of fine-tuning on consumer GPUs | New sensors |
| GR00T 2 <Cite id="gr00t-2-2026" /> | None, or five demonstrations | 40% or 95% on 50 tasks |
| Skild AI S1 <Cite id="skild-s1-2026" /> | One video, weights unchanged | 66% on an unseen long task, like fine-tuning on 380 demonstrations |
| GEN-1.5 <Cite id="gen-1-5-2026" /> | One 3 to 12 second demonstration | Learns the task |
| Frozen DreamZero plus a retrieved video <Cite id="robota-embed-2026" /> | One video | 47.0% to 56.3% on three simulated DROID <Cite id="droid-2024" /> tasks |

Also: π*0.6, whose practice more than doubles throughput on some of the hardest tasks <Cite id="pistar06-2025" />; hosted fine-tuning in Physical Intelligence's API <Cite id="pi-api-beta-2026" />.

## Vision-language-action models start from web knowledge

RT-2 trained a vision-language model on robot and web data together, writing actions as reserved words <Cite id="rt2-2023" />. π0 adds an action expert for continuous motion <Cite id="pi0-2024" />, and π0.5 credits its generalization to training on many kinds of task <Cite id="pi05-2025" />.

New setups still need tuning: OpenVLA-OFT's recipe lifted OpenVLA <Cite id="openvla-2024" /> from 76.5% to 97.1% on four simulated task suites <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />.

Also: RT-1 <Cite id="rt1-2022" />, steerable π0.7 <Cite id="pi07-2026" />, Kyutai's open REKI <Cite id="reki-2026" />.

## Memory lets a policy use the past

A policy that sees only the current view fails tasks that depend on something seen earlier.

| Model | Memory | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| T²Mem <Cite id="t2mem-2026" /> | Weights updated while the policy runs | Learned from actions alone |
| RoboTTT <Cite id="robottt-2026" /> | The same, over 8K timesteps | 79% on long real tasks, against 42% for GR00T N1.7 |
| ECoMEM <Cite id="ecomem-2026" /> | A written record of task facts | 86.1% on two new real tasks, against 8.6% without |
| IMPRINT <Cite id="imprint-2026" /> | A learned summary of past frames and actions | π0.5 from 25.4% to 72.8% on nine simulated memory tasks |

## Humanoids: the body limits what transfers

A humanoid's body limits what transfers from other robots. GR00T N1 pairs a vision-language module with a diffusion model that generates motion <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" />, and LeVERB sends learned commands to a separate whole-body controller <Cite id="leverb-2025" />.

Human data can replace robot pretraining. Pretrained on Figure's Index of human behavior, [Helix 2.5](/manipulation/generalist-policies/) reached 56% zero-shot success in 30 unseen homes, against 9% from scratch <Cite id="helix-2-5-2026" />. Gemini Robotics 2.5 drove Apptronik's Apollo 2 to 96% success kitting parts in a Mercedes-Benz pilot <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2-5-2026" />.

Also: WholeBodyVLA, from unlabeled video <Cite id="wholebodyvla-2025" />.

## World models learn how scenes change

V-JEPA 2 learns from over 1M hours of internet video, then the effect of actions from under 62 hours of DROID robot video <Cite id="vjepa2-2025" />. Dreamer 4, trained inside such a model, was the first agent to obtain diamonds in Minecraft purely from recorded data <Cite id="dreamer4-2025" />.

<Image id="cosmos-policy-aloha-2026" />

Cosmos Policy, built on NVIDIA's general Cosmos world model <Cite id="cosmos-predict-2025" />, reaches 98.5% on LIBERO <Cite id="cosmos-policy-2026" />.

Also: R3M on Ego4D <Cite id="ego4d-2022" /> video <Cite id="r3m-2022" />, Cosmos 3 with actions <Cite id="cosmos-3-2026" />, Ctrl-World for policy ranking <Cite id="ctrl-world-2025" />.

## World action models predict video and actions together

A [world action model](/world-models/taxonomy/) predicts future video and the actions that produce it in one network. DreamZero more than doubled leading VLAs' average task progress on new tasks and environments, at 7 Hz with 14 billion parameters <Cite id="dreamzero-2026" />. Runway's open Praxis-1 reports 46.2% on RoboLab-120 <Cite id="robolab-2026" /> against 28.0% for π0.5 <Cite id="runway-praxis-1-open-2026" />.

Also: GR00T N2 <Cite id="nvidia-gtc-gr00t-n2-2026" />, GR00T 2 <Cite id="nvidia-corl-gr00t-2-2026" />, Dyna-2 <Cite id="dyna-2-2026" />.

## Pretraining saves data but does not remove the need

In Toyota Research Institute's blind, randomized trials, pretrained policies matched from-scratch ones with 3 to 5 times less data <Cite id="tri-lbm-2025" />. Generalist's GEN-1, pretrained on over half a million hours of human data, averaged 99% on three tasks with about an hour of robot data each, against 19% from scratch <Cite id="generalist-gen1-2026" />.

Robot-only training degrades the backbone's visual-language understanding <Cite id="lbm-cotraining-2026" />, and a naively trained new action expert weakens transfer <Cite id="knowledge-insulation-paper-2025" /> ([knowledge insulation](/manipulation/knowledge-insulation/)). RoboPAIR jailbroke language-model-controlled robots, including a deployed Unitree Go2, often at 100% attack success <Cite id="robopair-2024" />.

Also: VC-1, no visual representation best everywhere <Cite id="vc1-2023" />.

## Single scores hide brittleness

A convincing claim varies tasks, places, robots and adaptation data against an equal-budget from-scratch baseline. VLAs near 95% on LIBERO drop below 30% under modest camera or start-position changes <Cite id="libero-plus-2025" />, and models above 90% fall to 0.0% under LIBERO-PRO <Cite id="libero-pro-2025" />.
