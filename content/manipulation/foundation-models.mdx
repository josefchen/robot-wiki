A foundation model is a network trained on broad data at scale and adaptable to many downstream tasks <Cite id="bommasani-foundation-models-2021" />. In robotics three families carry the label (<Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action models</Term>, predictive world models and multimodal backbones adapted into either), and the test is whether one checkpoint transfers to new tasks, environments or embodiments after bounded adaptation.

## What must be specified

A claim names its pretraining data, objective, interface and adaptation budget. Data spans web text and images, human video, robot trajectories and simulation; Open X-Embodiment pools 22 robots from 21 institutions <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />. Objectives include next-token prediction, contrastive learning, video prediction and action denoising.

Budgets run from zero-shot prompting through parameter-efficient and full fine-tuning to new robot data at scale. RoboCat adapts to new tasks and robots from 100 to 1,000 examples <Cite id="robocat-2023" />, Octo fine-tunes to new sensors and action spaces in hours on consumer GPUs <Cite id="octo-2024" />, and π*0.6's RL from experience more than doubles throughput on some of the hardest tasks <Cite id="pistar06-2025" />. The newest rung is in-context learning: Generalist reports GEN-1.5 learning a task from one 3 to 12 second demonstration with no training <Cite id="gen-1-5-2026" />.

Robota-Embed, from Yunze Liu, Linxi Fan, Han Cai and colleagues at NVIDIA, MIT, UC Berkeley, Oxford and CMU (October 2026), encodes vision, language and action in one vector, so a robot can retrieve recorded experience as context. Seeding a frozen DreamZero policy with a retrieved video, chosen by a reranking agent, raised its average success on three simulated DROID-Sim tasks from 47.0% to 56.3% over 100 trials per task, while random reference videos lowered it to 36.0%. Without the reranker, its top-1 retrievals reached 50.3% against 43.3% for Omni-Embed-Nemotron-3B <Cite id="robota-embed-2026" />.

## VLA foundations

RT-1 argued for task-agnostic training of high-capacity models on diverse robot data <Cite id="rt1-2022" />. RT-2 co-fine-tunes a vision-language model on robot trajectories and internet-scale vision-language tasks, writing actions as reserved tokens <Cite id="rt2-2023" />, and OpenVLA made a comparable recipe public on Open X-Embodiment <Cite id="openvla-2024" />. VLAs still struggle with novel robot setups until fine-tuned: OpenVLA-OFT's recipe lifted OpenVLA's average success across four task suites from 76.5% to 97.1% <Cite id="openvla-oft-2025" />.

π0 pairs a pretrained VLM with a separate flow-matching expert for continuous action chunks <Cite id="pi0-2024" />. π0.5 credits its generalization to co-training on heterogeneous tasks <Cite id="pi05-2025" />, and π0.7's authors call their 2026 model a steerable generalist robotic foundation model <Cite id="pi07-2026" />.

Test-time training stores past observations in fast weights that gradient descent updates while the policy runs. RoboTTT (July 2026) extends visuomotor context to 8K timesteps without growing inference latency and averaged a 79% task completion score on long-horizon real-robot tasks, against 42% for the single-step-context GR00T N1.7 baseline <Cite id="robottt-2026" />. ECoMEM (Stanford, September 2026) keeps an explicit record of task facts that a learned reader turns into memory tokens for the VLA, reaching 86.1% success on two new real-robot tasks against 8.6% without memory <Cite id="ecomem-2026" />.

IMPRINT, from Yilin Wu, Homanga Bharadhwaj, Guanya Shi and colleagues at Amazon FAR and Carnegie Mellon (October 2026), trains a latent memory of past camera frames, actions and joint states with the policy, so no rule has to pick which frames to keep. On the nine memory-dependent RMBench simulation tasks, with 100 trials per task, it raised π0.5 from 25.4% to 72.8% average success; EventVLA, the strongest of six memory baselines, reports 67.8%. The recurrent version took 122.8 ms per 50-step action chunk on an L40S GPU, against 91.0 ms for π0.5 without memory and 960 ms for EventVLA <Cite id="imprint-2026" />.

## Humanoid foundations

GR00T N1 combines a vision-language module with a diffusion transformer, trained on real-robot trajectories, human videos and synthetic datasets <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" />. For a humanoid, morphology, whole-body balance and shared action coordinates set the reuse boundary. Figure pretrained [Helix 2.5](/manipulation/generalist-policies/) from random initialization entirely on Index, its dataset of human behavior; with identical task data, it succeeded on 56% of zero-shot trials in 30 unseen homes, against 9% for a policy trained from scratch <Cite id="helix-2-5-2026" />.

## Predictive foundations

V-JEPA 2 learns representations from over 1M hours of internet video, then trains an action-conditioned stage on fewer than 62 hours of unlabeled DROID video <Cite id="vjepa2-2025" />. R3M's embedding, pretrained on Ego4D human video, beats from-scratch training by over 20% <Cite id="r3m-2022" />.

<Image id="cosmos-policy-aloha-2026" />

Cosmos frames a world foundation model as a general-purpose model fine-tuned into custom ones <Cite id="cosmos-predict-2025" />. Cosmos 3 unifies understanding, generation, forward and inverse dynamics and policy outputs in one omnimodal model <Cite id="cosmos-3-2026" />, and Cosmos Policy turns Cosmos-Predict2 into a policy in one post-training stage, reaching 98.5% on LIBERO <Cite id="cosmos-policy-2026" />. Each output needs its own test: Ctrl-World ranks policies without real rollouts <Cite id="ctrl-world-2025" />.

DreamZero (February 2026) is a [world action model](/world-models/taxonomy/) on a pretrained video diffusion backbone that predicts video and actions together; in real-robot tests it more than doubled average task progress on new tasks and environments over state-of-the-art VLAs, and its 14-billion-parameter model ran closed-loop at 7 Hz <Cite id="dreamzero-2026" />.

## What pretraining does not erase

In TRI's blind, randomized trials, multitask pretraining matched from-scratch policies with 3 to 5 times less data <Cite id="tri-lbm-2025" />. VC-1 found no pretrained visual representation universally dominant <Cite id="vc1-2023" />, and Toyota Research Institute's 89-policy study found that robot-only training degrades the backbone's visiolinguistic understanding <Cite id="lbm-cotraining-2026" />. Generalist reports that GEN-1's base model, pretrained on over half a million hours of wearable human data and no robot data, averaged 99% on three tasks with about one hour of robot data per task, against 19% from scratch <Cite id="generalist-gen1-2026" />.

Pretraining leaves calibration and control integration, closed-loop evaluation under perturbation, latency limits and safety cases to the builder. RoboPAIR jailbroke LLM-controlled robots, including a deployed Unitree Go2, often at 100% attack success <Cite id="robopair-2024" />, and a downloadable checkpoint can hide its training mixture, adaptation data or benchmark protocol.

## Evaluating a foundation claim

Look for a matrix: rows vary tasks and environments, columns vary embodiment, adaptation data and evaluation shift. Report the checkpoint, every tuned component, the new robot data and a same-budget from-scratch baseline. VLAs near 95% on LIBERO drop below 30% under modest camera or initial-state perturbations <Cite id="libero-plus-2025" />, and models above 90% fall to 0.0% under LIBERO-PRO's generalized setting <Cite id="libero-pro-2025" />.
