Surgical robots hold and move instruments inside the patient for <Term id="minimally-invasive-surgery">minimally invasive surgery</Term>, mostly as teleoperated arms that scale and steady a surgeon's hand. Intuitive had 12,094 da Vinci systems installed by September 30, 2026 <Cite id="intuitive-q3-2026" />, yet most commercial platforms make no decisions of their own <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

<Image id="da-vinci-xi-patient-cart-2018" />

## The robot moves only as the surgeon moves

A da Vinci surgeon works hand controllers at a console, and arms at the patient scale the motion down, filter tremor and bend inside the body.

<MotionPantograph className="my-6" />

Yang and colleagues call this level 0 of six, since even scaled output is the surgeon's motion <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. At level 1 a robot assists; at level 2 it performs a task on its own <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

<Image id="da-vinci-xi-surgeon-console-vesoul-2023" />

Also: CT-guided brain surgery robot, 1988 <Cite id="kwoh-ct-robot-1988" />, transatlantic telesurgery, 2001 <Cite id="marescaux-telesurgery-2001" />.

## Da Vinci 5 lets the surgeon feel force

Da Vinci 5, cleared in March 2024, lets the surgeon feel tip forces <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />, and surgeons press less hard with it on.

| Study | Scope | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awad et al. (2024) | Preclinical, 28 surgeons | Maximal force down 36, 41 and 55 percent in retraction, dissection and suturing at the high setting <Cite id="awad-force-feedback-2024" /> |
| Kneuertz et al. (2025) | 444 thoracic procedures, 73 surgeons | Median tip force 1.79 N with feedback off, 1.32 N at the high setting <Cite id="kneuertz-dv5-force-2025" /> |
| Chang et al. (2025) | Pilot, 68 colorectal operations | Less applied force, without significant changes in bowel function or length of stay <Cite id="chang-dv5-colorectal-2025" /> |

Intuitive's installed base grew 12 percent in 2025 to 11,106 systems <Cite id="intuitive-q4-2025" />, and da Vinci 5s were 315 of 546 placements in the third quarter of 2026 <Cite id="intuitive-q3-2026" />.

Also: cardiac clearance, January 2026 <Cite id="davinci5-cardiac-2026" />.

## The challengers compete on fit

CMR's Versius has separate bedside arms that move between rooms. Its 2024 De Novo authorization for gallbladder removal came when robots did about 2.5 percent of nearly 10 million US major operations <Cite id="cmr-versius-authorization-2024" />. In October 2026 Stryker agreed to invest about \$400 million in CMR, with an option to buy <Cite id="stryker-cmr-2026" />.

Medtronic's Hugo was cleared in the US for urology in December 2025 <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-clearance-2025" />. J&J's OTTAVA, built into the operating table, was authorized for general surgery in July 2026 <Cite id="jnj-ottava-2026" />.

Moon Surgical's Maestro holds the hospital's own laparoscope and instruments, and one surgeon used it with no human assistant in 30 patients <Cite id="maestro-tavac-2023" />. ScoPilot aims its camera at the instrument tip on demand <Cite id="scopilot-clearance-2025" />.

Also: Michigan adoption <Cite id="sheetz-adoption-2020" />, Hugo's first US case <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-first-us-2026" />, Versius Plus <Cite id="versius-plus-510k-2025" />, 45,000 Versius patients <Cite id="cmr-45000-patients-2026" />, Hugo general-surgery filing <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-510k-2026" />, Maestro commercial clearance <Cite id="maestro-commercial-510k-2024" />.

## Reliability is the bar

A policy with 95 percent per-step success finishes 30 steps 21.5 percent of the time ([reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/)). FDA reports on robotic surgery from 2000 to 2013 included 144 deaths, 1,391 injuries and 8,061 malfunctions <Cite id="alemzadeh-adverse-events-2016" />. In the ROLARR trial of 471 rectal-cancer patients, robotic assistance did not significantly cut conversion to open surgery, 8.1 against 12.2 percent <Cite id="rolarr-2017" />.

## Autonomy works only in animals and on the bench

Research robots have done whole tasks alone, in animals or on excised tissue, often supervised. SRT-H plans in language and guides a low-level policy through long steps and recoveries <Cite id="srt-h-2025" /> ([hierarchical policies](/manipulation/hierarchical/)).

| System | Setting | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STAR (2016) | Supervised bowel anastomosis (joining two ends) in live pigs | Suture spacing and leak pressure matched or exceeded expert surgeons and robot-assisted technique <Cite id="star-suturing-2016" /> |
| Saeidi et al. (2022) | Laparoscopic small-bowel anastomosis in pigs, one-week survival; operator picked among robot-made plans | Beat expert manual and robot-assisted technique on consistency and accuracy <Cite id="saeidi-laparoscopic-2022" /> |
| SRT-H (2025) | Gallbladder removal, ex vivo | 8 of 8 unseen gallbladders with no human intervention <Cite id="srt-h-2025" /> |
| UC San Diego humanoids (2026) | Gallbladder removal in live pigs, fully teleoperated | Worked, but needed several recalibrations and took much longer than dedicated systems <Cite id="liang-humanoid-surgery-2026" /><Cite id="ucsd-humanoid-surgery-2026" /> |
| AGRO-SUVIDE (2026) | Coding agent retrying failed skills; debridement on the da Vinci Research Kit, 340 trials, one demonstration | 85 percent single-fragment success; 60 percent for three in a row, 95 percent with one intervention <Cite id="agro-suvide-2026" /> |
| GR00T-H (2026) | Ex vivo suturing | 64 percent average across a 29-step sequence <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" /> |

## Regulators clear one procedure at a time

Each authorization covers one named procedure in one population; expanding it needs a new clearance.

Yang and colleagues argue that a highly autonomous robot practices medicine and should be licensed like a surgeon. Full premarket approval costs tens of millions of dollars and years more than 510(k) equivalence <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. Haidegger calls missing autonomy benchmarks a major barrier <Cite id="haidegger-autonomy-2019" /> ([safety and assurance](/frontier/safety-and-assurance/)).

## Surgery offers open hardware and recorded operations

The da Vinci Research Kit exposes every control layer on retired first-generation arms <Cite id="dvrk-research-kit-2014" />. SRT showed imitation learning works on the da Vinci's approximate joint readings if the policy predicts relative moves, so clinical archives may serve uncorrected <Cite id="srt-2024" />. ImitateCholec adds about 20 hours and 18,000 demonstrations from 34 ex vivo cholecystectomies <Cite id="imitatecholec-2026" />.

Open-H-Embodiment pools data from more than 50 institutions; its GR00T-H alone finished a suturing benchmark end to end, in 25 percent of trials <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" /> ([major datasets](/data-hardware/datasets/)). Force Feedback adds a data stream <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />; surgical data science still lacks clinical success stories <Cite id="maier-hein-sds-2022" />.

Also: gesture labels JIGSAWS <Cite id="jigsaws-benchmark-2017" />, phase labels EndoNet <Cite id="endonet-2016" />, action triplets CholecT50 <Cite id="cholect50-benchmarking-2022" />.

How much evidence earns autonomy is open in [autonomous vehicles](/adjacent/autonomous-vehicles/), [drones](/adjacent/drones/) and [space robotics](/adjacent/space/).
