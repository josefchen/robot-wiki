Surgical robotics is the use of robots to position or move surgical instruments, today mostly teleoperated systems for <Term id="minimally-invasive-surgery">minimally invasive surgery</Term> that scale and filter a surgeon's hand motion. Intuitive had 11,710 da Vinci systems installed by June 30, 2026 <Cite id="intuitive-q2-2026" />, and most commercial platforms sit at level 0, with no decision autonomy <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

<Image id="da-vinci-xi-patient-cart-2018" />

## The teleoperation baseline

A da Vinci surgeon sits at a console away from the table and moves hand controllers, while patient-side arms carry wristed instruments and an endoscope. The robot adds motion scaling, tremor filtering and wrists that articulate inside the body. Yang and colleagues place such a system at Level 0, no autonomy, because even with motion scaling its output represents the surgeon's desired motion <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. At level 1 a robot gives cognitive and physical assistance, and at level 2 it performs a surgical task autonomously <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

The field began with positioning. In 1988 Kwoh and colleagues reported a robot with improved absolute positioning accuracy for CT-guided stereotactic brain surgery <Cite id="kwoh-ct-robot-1988" />. Marescaux and colleagues reported transatlantic robot-assisted telesurgery in 2001 <Cite id="marescaux-telesurgery-2001" />. Intuitive says cardiac surgery was the first specialty cleared on the original da Vinci, in 2002 <Cite id="davinci5-cardiac-2026" />.

Da Vinci 5, cleared in March 2024, has more than 150 enhancements over Xi and more than 10,000 times its computing power <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />. Its optional Force Feedback instruments let the surgeon feel forces at the instrument tips, and Intuitive reported up to 43 percent less force on tissue in preclinical trials <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />. In a preclinical study by 28 surgeons, the feature at its high setting cut maximal force by 36, 41 and 55 percent in retraction, dissection and suturing <Cite id="awad-force-feedback-2024" />.

Logs from 444 thoracic procedures by 73 surgeons show median tip force falling from 1.79 N with feedback off to 1.32 N at the high setting <Cite id="kneuertz-dv5-force-2025" />. A pilot study of 68 colorectal operations found less applied force without a significant change in bowel function or length of stay <Cite id="chang-dv5-colorectal-2025" />. In January 2026 the FDA cleared da Vinci 5 for selected cardiac procedures using instruments without force feedback <Cite id="davinci5-cardiac-2026" />.

Intuitive reports that its installed base grew 12 percent in 2025 to 11,106 systems, and that da Vinci procedures grew roughly 18 percent worldwide in 2025 <Cite id="intuitive-q4-2025" />. Of 532 fourth-quarter placements, 303 were da Vinci 5s <Cite id="intuitive-q4-2025" />. In the second quarter of 2026, procedures grew about 15 percent and 246 of 468 placements were da Vinci 5s <Cite id="intuitive-q2-2026" />.

## The challengers and what they compete on

CMR Surgical's Versius is a modular multiport system whose arms are separate bedside units that can move between operating rooms, driven from an open console <Cite id="cmr-versius-authorization-2024" />. In October 2024 the FDA authorized it through De Novo for adult cholecystectomy, the first multiport soft-tissue general surgical system authorized that way <Cite id="cmr-versius-authorization-2024" />. CMR put robot-assisted surgery at about 2.5 percent of nearly 10 million annual US major operating-room procedures <Cite id="cmr-versius-authorization-2024" />. Versius Plus was cleared in December 2025, and by March 2026 CMR reported more than 45,000 Versius patients in more than 30 countries <Cite id="versius-plus-510k-2025" /><Cite id="cmr-45000-patients-2026" />.

Medtronic's Hugo was cleared on December 3, 2025 for urologic procedures including prostatectomy, nephrectomy and cystectomy <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-clearance-2025" />. Medtronic says it had already been used in more than 30 countries <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-clearance-2025" />. Its first US case followed at Cleveland Clinic in February 2026 <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-first-us-2026" />.

Medtronic says about 80 percent of US abdominal urologic surgery is already robot-assisted <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-first-us-2026" />. In June 2026 it filed 510(k)s for general and gynecologic surgery <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-510k-2026" />.

Johnson & Johnson's OTTAVA, a table-integrated soft-tissue robot, received De Novo authorization in July 2026 for general-surgery procedures including gastric bypass, cholecystectomy and hiatal hernia repair <Cite id="jnj-ottava-2026" />. J&J says it occupies 30 to 50 percent less space than boom- and cart-mounted systems <Cite id="jnj-ottava-2026" />. In a Michigan registry of 169,404 patients, robotic use in common general-surgery procedures rose from 1.8 percent in 2012 to 15.1 percent in 2018 <Cite id="sheetz-adoption-2020" />.

Moon Surgical's Maestro is a two-armed assistant that holds and positions the laparoscopic camera and instruments, using the cameras and graspers a hospital already owns. Its first 510(k), in December 2022, rested on substantial equivalence to a 1990s endoscope positioner <Cite id="maestro-tavac-2023" />. A single surgeon used it without an assistant in a 30-patient series <Cite id="maestro-tavac-2023" />.

The commercial system was cleared in June 2024 <Cite id="maestro-commercial-510k-2024" />. ScoPilot, cleared in March 2025, runs on NVIDIA Holoscan on the robot and lets the laparoscope follow the instrument tip on demand <Cite id="scopilot-clearance-2025" />, Level 1 assistance on the Yang scale <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />.

## The precision and reliability bar

A policy that succeeds 95 percent of the time per step compounds to 21.5 percent over 30 steps, the arithmetic of the [reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/). FDA reports on robotic surgery from 2000 to 2013 included 144 deaths (1.4 percent of 10,624 reports), 1,391 patient injuries and 8,061 device malfunctions <Cite id="alemzadeh-adverse-events-2016" />. At clearance, Intuitive said da Vinci 5 would first go to a small number of US customers to gather data on real use <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />. In the ROLARR trial of 471 rectal-cancer patients, robotic assistance did not significantly reduce conversion to open surgery: 8.1 percent against 12.2 percent for conventional laparoscopy (P = .16) <Cite id="rolarr-2017" />.

In 2016 STAR, the Smart Tissue Autonomous Robot, performed supervised autonomous soft-tissue anastomosis in vivo in pigs <Cite id="star-suturing-2016" />. Its suture spacing and leak pressure matched or exceeded both expert surgeons and robot-assisted technique <Cite id="star-suturing-2016" />. In 2022 a robot performed laparoscopic small-bowel anastomosis in pigs over a one-week survival period, with the operator choosing among autonomously generated plans <Cite id="saeidi-laparoscopic-2022" />. It outperformed expert manual and robot-assisted technique on consistency and accuracy <Cite id="saeidi-laparoscopic-2022" />.

In 2025 SRT-H, whose high-level language policy guides a low-level policy through long-horizon steps and helps it recover from errors, ran ex vivo cholecystectomy experiments <Cite id="srt-h-2025" />. It succeeded on all eight gallbladders tested without human intervention <Cite id="srt-h-2025" />. [Hierarchical policies](/manipulation/hierarchical/) covers that design.

A device is authorized for an indication, a named procedure in a named population, and expansion needs a new clearance. Yang and colleagues argue that a highly autonomous robot starts practicing medicine, which in the US falls to medical societies <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. They propose licensing robotic surgeons through the medical establishment, as is done for human surgeons <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />.

Yang and colleagues put the gap between the 510(k) and premarket-approval pathways at tens of millions of dollars and years of review, and add that risk tolerance to autonomous robots is expected to change as autonomous machines such as self-driving cars become commonplace <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. Haidegger names the lack of autonomy benchmarks and standards as a major barrier to commercialising autonomous surgical robots <Cite id="haidegger-autonomy-2019" />, and [safety and assurance](/frontier/safety-and-assurance/) covers the general case.

## What robot learning takes from it

Clinical recordings could become training data. SRT showed that imitation learning works on the da Vinci despite its approximate kinematics, using a relative action formulation, so the large repository of clinical data may be used for robot learning without further corrections <Cite id="srt-2024" />. Open-H-Embodiment pools synchronized video and kinematics from more than 50 institutions and platforms including Versius, da Vinci, the da Vinci Research Kit and Maestro <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" />.

Its GR00T-H model was the only one evaluated to finish a structured suturing benchmark end to end, in 25 percent of trials against 0 percent for all others <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" />. It reached a 64 percent average <Term id="success-rate">success rate</Term> across a 29-step ex vivo suturing sequence <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" />. [Major datasets](/data-hardware/datasets/) lists its general-robotics peers.

In September 2026, Shutong Jin, Ken Goldberg and colleagues reported AGRO-SUVIDE, a coding-agent framework that builds debridement skills from one expert demonstration and retries a skill when its checks fail. In 340 physical trials on the da Vinci Research Kit, it removed single fragments from a viscoelastic substrate with 85% average success and completed three consecutive removals 60% of the time, or 95% with one human intervention <Cite id="agro-suvide-2026" />.

Intuitive says da Vinci 5's <Term id="force-feedback">Force Feedback</Term> measurements add a new data stream to surgical data science <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />, and Moon Surgical frames Maestro's sensing and onboard compute as the substrate for next-generation physical AI <Cite id="scopilot-clearance-2025" />. A 2022 review found surgical data science still lacking translational success stories, and its roadmap covers data acquisition under regulatory constraints, annotation and sharing <Cite id="maier-hein-sds-2022" />. The same question of how much evidence earns autonomy runs through [autonomous vehicles](/adjacent/autonomous-vehicles/), [drones](/adjacent/drones/) and [space robotics](/adjacent/space/).
