Surgical robots hold and move instruments inside the patient, today mostly as teleoperated systems for <Term id="minimally-invasive-surgery">minimally invasive surgery</Term> that scale and steady a surgeon's hand motion. Intuitive had 12,094 da Vinci systems installed by September 30, 2026 <Cite id="intuitive-q3-2026" />, yet most commercial platforms make no decisions of their own <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

<Image id="da-vinci-xi-patient-cart-2018" />

## The teleoperation baseline

A da Vinci surgeon sits at a console moving hand controllers, while arms at the patient carry wristed instruments and a camera. The robot scales hand movements down, filters out tremor and adds wrists that bend inside the body.

<MotionPantograph className="my-6" />

Yang and colleagues place this at level 0 of six levels of autonomy, since even scaled output is the surgeon's desired motion <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. At level 1 a robot gives cognitive or physical assistance, and at level 2 it performs a surgical task on its own <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

<Image id="da-vinci-xi-surgeon-console-vesoul-2023" />

The field began with positioning: in 1988 Kwoh and colleagues reported a robot with improved positioning accuracy for CT-guided brain surgery <Cite id="kwoh-ct-robot-1988" />. In 2001 Marescaux and colleagues operated across the Atlantic by robot <Cite id="marescaux-telesurgery-2001" />. Intuitive says cardiac surgery was the original da Vinci's first cleared specialty, in 2002 <Cite id="davinci5-cardiac-2026" />.

## Da Vinci 5 lets the surgeon feel force

Da Vinci 5, cleared in March 2024, offers Force Feedback instruments that let the surgeon feel forces at the tips <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />. In three studies, surgeons pressed less hard with it on:

| Study | Scope | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awad et al. (2024) | Preclinical, 28 surgeons | Maximal force down 36, 41 and 55 percent in retraction, dissection and suturing at the high setting <Cite id="awad-force-feedback-2024" /> |
| Kneuertz et al. (2025) | 444 thoracic procedures, 73 surgeons | Median tip force 1.79 N with feedback off, 1.32 N at the high setting <Cite id="kneuertz-dv5-force-2025" /> |
| Chang et al. (2025) | Pilot, 68 colorectal operations | Less applied force, without significant changes in bowel function or length of stay <Cite id="chang-dv5-colorectal-2025" /> |

In January 2026 the FDA cleared da Vinci 5 for selected cardiac procedures using instruments without force feedback <Cite id="davinci5-cardiac-2026" />. Intuitive's installed base grew 12 percent in 2025 to 11,106 systems, and da Vinci procedures grew roughly 18 percent worldwide <Cite id="intuitive-q4-2025" />. In the third quarter of 2026 procedures rose about 14 percent, and 315 of 546 new placements were da Vinci 5s <Cite id="intuitive-q3-2026" />.

All of these commercial systems sit at level 0 or level 1 on the Yang scale <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />.

## The challengers compete on fit

Rivals differ mostly in how they fit the operating room. CMR Surgical's Versius has separate bedside arms that move between operating rooms. In October 2024 the FDA authorized it through the De Novo pathway for novel devices, for adult gallbladder removal (cholecystectomy), the first multiport soft-tissue general surgical system authorized that way; CMR put robot-assisted surgery at about 2.5 percent of nearly 10 million annual US major operating-room procedures <Cite id="cmr-versius-authorization-2024" />. Versius Plus was cleared in December 2025, and by March 2026 CMR reported more than 45,000 Versius patients in more than 30 countries <Cite id="versius-plus-510k-2025" /><Cite id="cmr-45000-patients-2026" />. In October 2026 Stryker agreed to invest about \$400 million in CMR and sell Versius exclusively in the US, with an option to buy the company <Cite id="stryker-cmr-2026" />.

Medtronic's Hugo, used in over 30 countries, was cleared in the US on December 3, 2025 for urologic procedures including prostate, kidney and bladder removal <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-clearance-2025" />. After its first US case, at Cleveland Clinic in February 2026, Medtronic said about 80 percent of US abdominal urologic surgery is already robot-assisted <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-first-us-2026" />. In June 2026 it filed for clearance in general and gynecologic surgery <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-510k-2026" />. Johnson & Johnson's OTTAVA, built into the operating table and, J&J says, taking 30 to 50 percent less space than boom- and cart-mounted systems, received De Novo authorization in July 2026 for general surgery including gastric bypass and cholecystectomy <Cite id="jnj-ottava-2026" />. In a Michigan registry of 169,404 patients, robotic use in common general-surgery procedures rose from 1.8 percent in 2012 to 15.1 percent in 2018 <Cite id="sheetz-adoption-2020" />.

Moon Surgical's Maestro is a two-armed assistant that holds the hospital's existing laparoscopic camera and instruments. Its first 510(k) clearance, in December 2022, rested on equivalence to a 1990s endoscope positioner, and one surgeon used it with no human assistant in a 30-patient series <Cite id="maestro-tavac-2023" />. The commercial system was cleared in June 2024 <Cite id="maestro-commercial-510k-2024" />. ScoPilot, cleared in March 2025, lets the laparoscope follow the instrument tip on demand <Cite id="scopilot-clearance-2025" />, level 1 assistance on the Yang scale <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />.

## The precision and reliability bar

A policy with 95 percent per-step success compounds to 21.5 percent over 30 steps, the arithmetic of the [reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/). FDA reports on robotic surgery from 2000 to 2013 included 144 deaths (1.4 percent of 10,624 reports), 1,391 patient injuries and 8,061 device malfunctions <Cite id="alemzadeh-adverse-events-2016" />. Even teleoperation's benefits are hard to prove: in the ROLARR trial of 471 rectal-cancer patients, robotic assistance did not significantly reduce conversion to open surgery, 8.1 percent against 12.2 percent for conventional laparoscopy (P = .16) <Cite id="rolarr-2017" />.

## Autonomy so far: animals and the bench

Research robots have done whole surgical tasks alone, but only in animals or on tissue outside the body, often supervised. In SRT-H a high-level policy plans in language and guides a low-level policy through long steps and error recovery <Cite id="srt-h-2025" /> ([hierarchical policies](/manipulation/hierarchical/)).

| System | Setting | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STAR (2016) | Supervised bowel anastomosis (joining two ends) in live pigs | Suture spacing and leak pressure matched or exceeded expert surgeons and robot-assisted technique <Cite id="star-suturing-2016" /> |
| Saeidi et al. (2022) | Laparoscopic small-bowel anastomosis in pigs, one-week survival; operator picked among robot-made plans | Beat expert manual and robot-assisted technique on consistency and accuracy <Cite id="saeidi-laparoscopic-2022" /> |
| SRT-H (2025) | Gallbladder removal, ex vivo | 8 of 8 unseen gallbladders with no human intervention <Cite id="srt-h-2025" /> |
| UC San Diego humanoids (2026) | Gallbladder removal in live pigs, fully teleoperated | Worked, but needed several recalibrations and took much longer than dedicated systems <Cite id="liang-humanoid-surgery-2026" /><Cite id="ucsd-humanoid-surgery-2026" /> |
| AGRO-SUVIDE (2026) | Coding agent retrying failed skills; debridement on the da Vinci Research Kit, 340 trials, one demonstration | 85 percent single-fragment success; 60 percent for three in a row, 95 percent with one intervention <Cite id="agro-suvide-2026" /> |
| GR00T-H (2026) | Ex vivo suturing | 64 percent average across a 29-step sequence <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" /> |

## Regulators clear one procedure at a time

Surgery has what manipulation lacks: a regulator that decides, procedure by procedure, what a robot may do. An authorization covers an indication, a named procedure in a named population, and expanding it needs a new clearance. Yang and colleagues argue that a highly autonomous robot is practicing medicine, which in the US falls to medical societies, and propose licensing robot surgeons as human ones are. They put the gap between the 510(k) route, which needs only equivalence to an existing device, and full premarket approval at tens of millions of dollars and years of review <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. Haidegger calls missing autonomy benchmarks and standards a major barrier to commercial autonomous surgical robots <Cite id="haidegger-autonomy-2019" /> ([safety and assurance](/frontier/safety-and-assurance/)).

## What robot learning takes from surgery

Surgery offers an open research platform and an archive of recorded operations. The da Vinci Research Kit exposes every control layer through open electronics and software on retired first-generation da Vinci arms <Cite id="dvrk-research-kit-2014" />. SRT showed that imitation learning works on the da Vinci despite its approximate joint readings if the policy predicts relative moves, so the large archive of clinical da Vinci data may serve robot learning without corrections <Cite id="srt-2024" />. The same Johns Hopkins group released ImitateCholec, about 20 hours of video and kinematics with more than 18,000 demonstrations, recoveries included, from 34 ex vivo cholecystectomies <Cite id="imitatecholec-2026" />.

Open-H-Embodiment pools video and kinematics from more than 50 institutions and platforms including Versius, da Vinci, the research kit and Maestro. Its model GR00T-H was the only one evaluated to finish a structured suturing benchmark end to end, in 25 percent of trials against 0 percent for all others <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" /> ([major datasets](/data-hardware/datasets/)). Older surgical datasets label surgical gestures with kinematics <Cite id="jigsaws-benchmark-2017" />, operation phases and tool presence <Cite id="endonet-2016" />, or instrument, action and target <Cite id="cholect50-benchmarking-2022" />.

Intuitive says Force Feedback adds a new data stream for surgical data science <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />. A 2022 review found that field still short of clinical success stories and set out a roadmap for collecting, annotating and sharing data under regulatory constraints <Cite id="maier-hein-sds-2022" />. How much evidence earns autonomy is the same question in [autonomous vehicles](/adjacent/autonomous-vehicles/), [drones](/adjacent/drones/) and [space robotics](/adjacent/space/).
