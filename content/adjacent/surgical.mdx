Surgical robots hold and move instruments inside a patient's body for <Term id="minimally-invasive-surgery">minimally invasive surgery</Term>. Typically, these robotic systems are teleoperated, where the robot arms follow the surgeon's hand movements with motion scaling and tremor filtration. As of September 30, 2026, Intuitive reported 12,094 installed da Vinci systems <Cite id="intuitive-q3-2026" />. However, the majority of commercially available systems make no decisions of their own <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

<Image id="da-vinci-xi-patient-cart-2018" />

## The robot moves only as the surgeon moves

In the da Vinci, hand controllers manipulated by the surgeon at a console move robotic arms at the patient's side, which scale the motion down, filter tremor and bend inside the body.

<MotionPantograph className="my-6" />

Such systems were classified as level 0 (out of six) by Yang and colleagues because the motions performed are those of the surgeon, albeit scaled down <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. In level 1 the robotic system provides assistance, whereas in level 2 the robot autonomously accomplishes a task <Cite id="attanasio-autonomy-2021" />.

<Image id="da-vinci-xi-surgeon-console-vesoul-2023" />

Also: CT-guided brain surgery robot, 1988 <Cite id="kwoh-ct-robot-1988" />, transatlantic telesurgery, 2001 <Cite id="marescaux-telesurgery-2001" />.

## Da Vinci 5 lets the surgeon feel force

Force feedback, as available in the da Vinci 5 system (cleared in March 2024), lets the surgeon feel tip forces <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />, and when force feedback is on, surgeons apply less force.

| Study | Scope | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Awad et al. (2024) | Preclinical, 28 surgeons | Maximal force down 36, 41 and 55 percent in retraction, dissection and suturing at the high setting <Cite id="awad-force-feedback-2024" /> |
| Kneuertz et al. (2025) | 444 thoracic procedures, 73 surgeons | Median tip force 1.79 N with feedback off, 1.32 N at the high setting <Cite id="kneuertz-dv5-force-2025" /> |
| Chang et al. (2025) | Pilot, 68 colorectal operations | Less applied force, without significant changes in bowel function or length of stay <Cite id="chang-dv5-colorectal-2025" /> |

Intuitive's installed base grew 12 percent in 2025 to 11,106 systems <Cite id="intuitive-q4-2025" />. Of the 546 da Vinci placements during the third quarter of 2026, 315 were da Vinci 5 systems <Cite id="intuitive-q3-2026" />.

Also: cardiac clearance, January 2026 <Cite id="davinci5-cardiac-2026" />.

## The challengers compete on fit

CMR's Versius system has separate bedside arms that can be wheeled from room to room, and it received De Novo authorization in 2024 for gallbladder removal. At that time, robots were used for about 2.5 percent of the nearly 10 million major operations performed in the US <Cite id="cmr-versius-authorization-2024" />. In October 2026, Stryker agreed to invest approximately \$400 million in CMR, with an option to acquire the company <Cite id="stryker-cmr-2026" />.

Others include Medtronic's Hugo (cleared for urological procedures in the US in December 2025) <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-clearance-2025" /> and J&J's OTTAVA, which integrates the robotic system into the operating table (authorized in July 2026 for general surgery) <Cite id="jnj-ottava-2026" />.

Maestro is Moon Surgical's robotic system to hold the hospital's own laparoscope and instruments; one surgeon used Maestro without a human assistant in 30 patients <Cite id="maestro-tavac-2023" />. ScoPilot directs its camera toward the tip of an instrument when requested <Cite id="scopilot-clearance-2025" />.

Also: Michigan adoption <Cite id="sheetz-adoption-2020" />, Hugo's first US case <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-first-us-2026" />, Versius Plus <Cite id="versius-plus-510k-2025" />, 45,000 Versius patients <Cite id="cmr-45000-patients-2026" />, Hugo general-surgery filing <Cite id="medtronic-hugo-510k-2026" />, Maestro commercial clearance <Cite id="maestro-commercial-510k-2024" />.

## Reliability is the bar

A policy with a per-step success rate of 95 percent will complete 30 steps 21.5 percent of the time ([reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/)). Reports submitted to the FDA between 2000 and 2013 for robotic surgery systems identified 8,061 malfunctions, 1,391 injuries and 144 deaths <Cite id="alemzadeh-adverse-events-2016" />. In the ROLARR trial, which studied 471 patients undergoing surgery for rectal cancer, there was no significant reduction in conversion to open surgery with robotic assistance (8.1 versus 12.2 percent) <Cite id="rolarr-2017" />.

## Autonomy works only in animals and on the bench

Research robots have been shown to perform full surgical tasks without human assistance on excised tissue, phantoms, gel or animals, although often with human supervision. SRT-H is an example of [hierarchical policies](/manipulation/hierarchical/), where a high-level policy outputs a language-based plan and guides a low-level policy over long time horizons including error recovery <Cite id="srt-h-2025" />.

| System | Setting | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| STAR (2016) | Supervised bowel anastomosis (joining two ends) in live pigs | Suture spacing and leak pressure matched or exceeded expert surgeons and robot-assisted technique <Cite id="star-suturing-2016" /> |
| Saeidi et al. (2022) | Laparoscopic small-bowel anastomosis in pigs, one-week survival; operator picked among robot-made plans | Beat expert manual and robot-assisted technique on consistency and accuracy <Cite id="saeidi-laparoscopic-2022" /> |
| SRT-H (2025) | Gallbladder removal, ex vivo | 8 of 8 unseen gallbladders with no human intervention <Cite id="srt-h-2025" /> |
| UC San Diego humanoids (2026) | Gallbladder removal in live pigs, fully teleoperated | Worked, but needed several recalibrations and took much longer than dedicated systems <Cite id="liang-humanoid-surgery-2026" /><Cite id="ucsd-humanoid-surgery-2026" /> |
| AGRO-SUVIDE (2026) | Coding agent retrying failed skills; debridement on the da Vinci Research Kit, 340 trials, one demonstration | 85 percent single-fragment success; 60 percent for three in a row, 95 percent with one intervention <Cite id="agro-suvide-2026" /> |
| ThERMO (2026) | Thermal-camera speed control; virtual-tumor margin cuts, ex vivo pig tongues | Thermal injury 52 percent below 2 mm/s cutting; margin error 59 percent below clinician-derived 7 mm/s speed <Cite id="thermo-electrosurgery-2026" /> |
| GR00T-H (2026) | Ex vivo suturing | 64 percent average across a 29-step sequence <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" /> |
| SimRetract (2026) | Arm takes a retractor from the surgeon's hand on an abdominal phantom, trained only in simulation | 90 percent grasped and 78 percent held retraction, of 50 surgeon-set poses <Cite id="simretract-2026" /> |

## Regulators clear one procedure at a time

Each authorization covers one named procedure in one population; if a company wanted to extend the authorized use, it would need a new clearance.

Yang and colleagues argue that if robots become highly autonomous they will be practicing medicine and should be licensed like surgeons. Full premarket approval is expensive (tens of millions of dollars more) and time-consuming (years more) compared to the 510(k) process, which requires showing equivalence <Cite id="yang-autonomy-2017" />. For Haidegger, a major barrier is the lack of benchmarks for measuring autonomy <Cite id="haidegger-autonomy-2019" /> ([safety and assurance](/frontier/safety-and-assurance/)).

## Surgery offers open hardware and recorded operations

The da Vinci Research Kit provides access to all levels of control for decommissioned, first-generation da Vinci arms <Cite id="dvrk-research-kit-2014" />. The three-armed ARTS surgical robot is open and can be constructed for approximately \$137,000 using off-the-shelf components, according to its developers <Cite id="arts-surgical-platform-2026" />. SRT showed that imitation learning works with the da Vinci's approximate joint-level kinematic states as long as relative actions are predicted, which may allow clinical da Vinci data to be used without correction <Cite id="srt-2024" />. ImitateCholec adds about 20 hours and 18,000 demonstrations from 34 ex vivo cholecystectomies <Cite id="imitatecholec-2026" />.

Open-H-Embodiment aggregates robot learning datasets from more than 50 institutions, and its GR00T-H alone completed a suturing benchmark from beginning to end in 25 percent of trials <Cite id="open-h-embodiment-2026" /> ([major datasets](/data-hardware/datasets/)). Force Feedback adds a data stream <Cite id="davinci5-clearance-2024" />. Nevertheless, surgical data science still lacks clinical success stories <Cite id="maier-hein-sds-2022" />.

Also: gesture labels JIGSAWS <Cite id="jigsaws-benchmark-2017" />, phase labels EndoNet <Cite id="endonet-2016" />, action triplets CholecT50 <Cite id="cholect50-benchmarking-2022" />.

How much evidence is enough to certify autonomy in [autonomous vehicles](/adjacent/autonomous-vehicles/), [drones](/adjacent/drones/) and [space robotics](/adjacent/space/) is an open question.
