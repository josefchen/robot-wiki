The software controlling self-driving cars and trucks is tasked with sensing the surroundings, predicting the actions of other road users, planning a trajectory and controlling the steering. Waymo's robotaxis, as of October 2026, have driven more than 300 million fully autonomous miles <Cite id="alphabet-q3-2026" />. Unlike many areas neighboring robot learning, autonomous driving already has fleets in operation, recalls and a public crash record.

<Image id="waymo-jaguar-ipace-san-francisco-2023" />

## Driving splits into four stages

Production pipelines for self-driving cars and trucks consist of separate components: perception, prediction, planning and control <Cite id="uniad-2023" /><Cite id="paden-2016" />.

<AvStackTable className="my-6" />

Learning to drive was demonstrated as early as 1988 with ALVINN <Cite id="alvinn-1988" />, which could be trained in five minutes by 1991 <Cite id="pomerleau-1991" />. Other learning-based drivers include RALPH, which crossed the US in 1995 <Cite id="no-hands-across-america-1995" />, and Stanley in 2005 <Cite id="stanley-2006" />.

### Perception fuses every sensor

Waymo aggregates <Term id="lidar">lidar</Term>, radar and camera measurements over time <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" />, noting that no single sensor is enough <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />. Camera-only BEVFormer achieves comparable performance to lidar-based methods on the nuScenes test set <Cite id="bevformer-2022" />.

Different approaches have been proposed in the literature for <Term id="point-cloud">point-cloud</Term> detection, including PointPillars <Cite id="pointpillars-2018" />, PV-RCNN <Cite id="pv-rcnn-2019" /> and CenterPoint <Cite id="centerpoint-2020" />.

<Image id="waymo-roof-sensor-suite-2025" />

### Prediction keeps several futures

A pedestrian could cross the road or wait, and the average of the two is neither. VectorNet encodes lanes and tracks as line segments and matches image-based predictors using over 70 percent fewer parameters <Cite id="vectornet-2020" />. MotionLM predicts the motions of multiple agents by encoding them as tokens, like words <Cite id="motionlm-2023" />.

Also: multi-future predictors MultiPath <Cite id="multipath-2019" />, LaneGCN <Cite id="lanegcn-2020" />, Trajectron++ <Cite id="trajectron-plus-plus-2020" />, Wayformer <Cite id="wayformer-2022" />, Motion Transformer <Cite id="motion-transformer-2022" />.

### Planning needs more than copying drivers

CMU's Boss won the 2007 DARPA Urban Challenge with a three-level hierarchical planner that consisted of mission, behavior and motion planners <Cite id="boss-urban-challenge-2008" />. Such a decomposition is also used in Paden's survey <Cite id="paden-2016" /> ([motion planning](/classical/motion-planning/)).

<Image id="cmu-boss-urban-challenge-winner" />

Waymo's ChauffeurNet used perturbed expert driving data along with collision-penalizing losses, since <Term id="behavior-cloning">behavior cloning</Term> on 30 million driving examples alone was insufficient <Cite id="chauffeurnet-2018" />. People working on manipulation later rediscovered the same solution to combat <Term id="covariate-shift">covariate shift</Term> ([behavior cloning foundations](/manipulation/bc-foundations/)).

Control involves tracking a planned trajectory and is accomplished with techniques ranging from pure pursuit to <Term id="mpc">model-predictive control</Term>, even in cases where the planner is learned <Cite id="paden-2016" /> ([control](/classical/control/)).

### Simple planners still match end-to-end networks

Information is inevitably lost when passing information between modules, so <Term id="end-to-end-driving">end-to-end</Term> approaches have been developed that jointly train the modules, while modular systems are easier to debug <Cite id="e2e-ad-survey-2024" />. UniAD trains all four as one network <Cite id="uniad-2023" />, and EMMA predicts driving outputs as text <Cite id="emma-2024" />.

In closed-loop evaluation, a planner that relies on rule-based prior knowledge won the 2023 nuPlan planning challenge <Cite id="nuplan-2021" /><Cite id="dauner-misconceptions-2023" />, and in NAVSIM evaluation the simple TransFuser performed on par with UniAD <Cite id="navsim-2024" />.

Waymo provided 15 million rides in 2025 <Cite id="waymo-year-in-review-2025" />. Different companies use different metrics.

| Tesla | Miami, Orlando and Tampa from July 2026 <Cite id="tesla-q2-2026-update" />; Cybercab in Austin from September 3 <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" /> | Nearly two million unsupervised miles by end of September 2026 <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" /> |

### The long tail still causes recalls

The <Term id="long-tail">long tail</Term> is the mass of rare situations, like police officers directing traffic through red lights, that no log holds enough of to learn from. Waymo's WOD-E2E benchmark collects 4,021 clips, totaling approximately 12 hours, of scenarios that occur less than 0.03 percent of the time <Cite id="wod-e2e-2025" />.

| Date | Action | Defect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dec 2025 | Waymo recall, 3,067 vehicles | Drove on past school buses with red lights flashing <Cite id="waymo-recall-25e084-2025" /> |
| May 2026 | Waymo recall, 3,791 vehicles | Slowed but did not stop for a flooded lane <Cite id="waymo-recall-26e026-2026" /> |
| Nov 2026 | NHTSA query | Most school-bus incidents came after the fix <Cite id="nhtsa-rq26006-2026" /> |

Waymo's World Model, based on Genie 3, simulates lidar and camera data of rare driving scenarios, "from a tornado to a casual encounter with an elephant" <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. Similarly, Wayve's GAIA-2 generates driving videos <Cite id="gaia-2-2025" />. Whether such generative models can extrapolate beyond their training data remains to be seen ([generative video](/world-models/generative-video/)).

## Proving safety is a measurement problem

RAND estimated the amount of test driving alone needed at hundreds of millions, and in some cases hundreds of billions, of miles <Cite id="kalra-paddock-2016" />. Mobileye has proposed Responsibility-Sensitive Safety, which models safe distances explicitly instead <Cite id="rss-2017" />. Koopman adds accountability and no risk hot spots to "safe enough" <Cite id="koopman-safe-enough-2026" />.

<AvSafetyMiles className="my-6" />

Waymo crashes far less than human drivers on the same roads.

| Study | Scope | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waymo, peer-reviewed, 2025 | 56.7 million rider-only miles | 96% fewer injury crashes between vehicles at intersections <Cite id="waymo-crash-rates-2025" /> |
| Waymo safety hub | Through June 2026 | 95% fewer serious-injury crashes, 82% fewer injury crashes <Cite id="waymo-safety-hub-2026" /> |
| IIHS, independent, 2026 | Same areas as human drivers | 68% fewer police-reportable crashes per mile <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" /><Cite id="iihs-rise-of-machines-2026" /> |

Most operators do not publish the denominator: US companies are required to report crashes, but not miles driven <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" />. Tesla recently included miles driven in its reporting of robotaxi collisions <Cite id="tesla-robotaxi-safety-2026" />. California said that disengagement data reported by companies cannot be used to compare companies' performance <Cite id="ca-dmv-av-miles-2026" />.

## Regulators set the terms

There are six levels of driving automation, numbered 0 to 5, defined in SAE J3016 <Cite id="sae-j3016-2021" />. Waymo has referred to the progression from level 2 driver assist systems to fully autonomous driving as "a false summit" <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />. All deployed level 4 systems operate within an <Term id="operational-design-domain">operational design domain</Term>, a defined set of roads, places, driving speeds and weather conditions.

| Date | Regulator | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 2026 | UNECE | First global rule for driverless systems: credible virtual tests and a <Term id="safety-case">safety case</Term> <Cite id="un-ads-regulation-2026" /><Cite id="unece-ads-press-2026" /> |
| March 2026 | NHTSA | Proposed standards for cars without manual controls; streamlined crash reporting <Cite id="nhtsa-av-framework-2026" /> |
| July 2026 | NHTSA | Zoox robotaxi exempt from parts of eight standards, up to 2,500 a year for two years <Cite id="nhtsa-zoox-exemption-2026" /> |
| October 2026 | FMCSA | Level 4 trucks may use cab-mounted beacons for five years <Cite id="fmcsa-aurora-beacons-2026" /> |

Aurora completed its safety case prior to operating vehicles without a safety driver <Cite id="aurora-driverless-launch-2025" />. Zoox started accepting paying customers in Las Vegas <Cite id="amazon-q3-2026" />. Chris Paxton pointed out trucks require much longer distances to come to a stop and, if unmanned, could not set up the roadside warning devices that regulations required <Cite id="paxton-autonomous-trucks-2026" />.

## Uber and Cruise failed on public roads

Uber's self-driving test car fatally struck a pedestrian in Tempe, Arizona in 2018. The pedestrian was walking alongside her bicycle at night. The car did not detect her until 5.6 seconds before impact and kept switching between different classifications for her; emergency braking was left to a human operator who was watching TV <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />. Every layer failed at once ([reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/)).

In 2023, a driverless Cruise car dragged a pedestrian about 20 feet. Cruise accepted a \$1.5 million penalty for not including that in its crash reports <Cite id="nhtsa-cruise-consent-2024" />, and GM stopped funding Cruise's robotaxis that December <Cite id="gm-cruise-refocus-2024" />.

## Robot learning borrowed driving's tools

Driving gave robot learning four tools ([data bottleneck](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck/)).

| Tool | What driving showed | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Term id="dagger">DAgger</Term> | Fixes covariate shift; errors grow linearly with task length, not quadratically | Racing-game car <Cite id="dagger-2011" /> |
| Shared benchmarks | Set the pattern for shared datasets; Open X-Embodiment pools a million robot trajectories from 22 embodiments <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" /> | Waymo Open Dataset <Cite id="waymo-open-dataset-2020" /> |
| <Term id="scaling-law">Scaling laws</Term> | Forecasting and planning improve as a power law in compute | 447,000 hours of driving <Cite id="waymo-scaling-laws-2025" /> |
| RL in <Term id="gaussian-splatting">Gaussian splatting</Term> | Training inside replicas of real places cuts collisions threefold | RAD <Cite id="rad-2025" /> |

## Driving models are becoming VLAs

EMMA is a <Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action model</Term> whose body is a car <Cite id="emma-2024" />. Driving VLAs are single networks or dual systems, where a slow vision-language model informs fast execution <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />, analogous to [hierarchical](/manipulation/hierarchical/) models for manipulation.

Waymo couples a driving model trained with Gemini with a fast sensor-fusion encoder <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" /> ([knowledge insulation](/manipulation/knowledge-insulation/)). NVIDIA's Alpamayo-R1 runs onboard with a latency of 99 ms <Cite id="alpamayo-r1-2025" />. In NVIDIA's own closed-loop simulation (AlpaSim), Alpamayo 2 Edge, a 2B-parameter model targeting DRIVE AGX Thor with open weights (OpenMDW-1.1), achieved 15.15 km driving distance per at-fault incident (evaluated over 875 scenarios with three rollouts each) <Cite id="alpamayo-2-edge-2026" />.

These models do not reliably act on what they see or say.

| Study, October 2026 | Test | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MIT CSAIL <Cite id="mind-temporal-gap-2026" /> | 200 clips of a car reversing | Six video-language models answered "forward" every time, until fine-tuned |
| Cercola, Pavone and colleagues <Cite id="cercola-binding-gap-2026" /> | NVIDIA Alpamayo-1.5 | Hazards shape its path; its own speed history sets its speed |
| Manalu and colleagues <Cite id="manalu-reasoning-coupling-2026" /> | Four driving VLAs | Rewriting the reasoning moved paths far less than blanking the camera |

## Contact does not transfer

Control of a car boils down to two controls, speed and steering, and there are mature models of tire dynamics. Controlling a manipulator involves dozens of coordinated joints and making and breaking contact. Control that works for tracking a car <Cite id="paden-2016" /> has no clean purchase on a manipulator ([why RL won locomotion](/rl-sim2real/why-rl-locomotion/)).

Waymo argues that replaying data in an open-loop fashion is not enough <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />, but many manipulation papers still report results on short-horizon tasks with few rollouts ([evaluation crisis](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis/)). A driving simulator has no contact to model, and Waymo says its World Model helps it scale safely <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. Genie-class models for robots still offer a narrow range of actions <Cite id="genie-3-2025" />.

Open: whether driving VLAs converge on hierarchical manipulation <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />, and whether robotics adopts driving's safety cases, from RSS <Cite id="rss-2017" /> to credibility rules for virtual tests <Cite id="unece-ads-press-2026" />. [Drones](/adjacent/drones/) covers flight, where a sim-trained policy has beaten human champions.
