Autonomous vehicles are cars and trucks whose software perceives the road, predicts what other road users will do, plans a path and steers. By October 2026 Waymo robotaxis had driven more than 300 million fully autonomous miles <Cite id="alphabet-q3-2026" />. For robot learning, driving is the neighbor that already has fleets, regulators, recalls and a public crash record.

<Image id="waymo-jaguar-ipace-san-francisco-2023" />

ALVINN, a 1988 neural network trained on simulated roads, steered a van from camera and laser range-finder images <Cite id="alvinn-1988" />; by 1991 it learned to drive in under five minutes by watching a human <Cite id="pomerleau-1991" />. In 1995 CMU's RALPH steered 2,797 of 2,849 miles across the US while researchers worked the pedals <Cite id="no-hands-across-america-1995" />, and Stanley won the 2005 DARPA Grand Challenge with software built mainly on machine learning and probabilistic reasoning <Cite id="stanley-2006" />.

## The driving stack

A production vehicle splits driving into four stages, perception, prediction, planning and control, each with its own models, metrics and failure modes <Cite id="uniad-2023" /><Cite id="paden-2016" />.

<AvStackTable className="my-6" />

### Perception

Perception answers where everything is and what it is. Waymo's encoder fuses camera, <Term id="lidar">lidar</Term> and radar inputs over time <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" />, and Waymo argues that no single sensor can replicate that redundant view <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />. BEVFormer, which turns camera images alone into a bird's-eye map, matched lidar-based baselines on the nuScenes test set <Cite id="bevformer-2022" />. Lidar detectors differ mainly in how they arrange the <Term id="point-cloud">point cloud</Term>, as columns, small cubes or object centers <Cite id="pointpillars-2018" /><Cite id="pv-rcnn-2019" /><Cite id="centerpoint-2020" />.

<Image id="waymo-roof-sensor-suite-2025" />

### Prediction

Prediction forecasts what every other road user will do over the next several seconds. The future branches: a pedestrian may cross or wait, and a predictor that averages the two forecasts a path through neither. Early predictors drew the map and recent motion as a top-down image; VectorNet (2020) instead fed lanes and tracks to a graph network as line segments, and matched or beat the image version with over 70 percent fewer parameters and an order of magnitude less computation <Cite id="vectornet-2020" />. Later predictors keep several candidate futures with their uncertainty <Cite id="multipath-2019" /><Cite id="lanegcn-2020" /><Cite id="trajectron-plus-plus-2020" /><Cite id="wayformer-2022" /><Cite id="motion-transformer-2022" />, and MotionLM writes the motion of several agents as tokens, the way a language model writes words <Cite id="motionlm-2023" />.

### Planning and control

Planning decides the lane, speed and path for the next few seconds. CMU's Boss won the 2007 DARPA Urban Challenge with a three-layer planner for mission, behavior and motion <Cite id="boss-urban-challenge-2008" />, a split Paden's survey also uses <Cite id="paden-2016" /> ([motion planning](/classical/motion-planning/)).

<Image id="cmu-boss-urban-challenge-winner" />

Copying human drivers was not enough. ChauffeurNet, Waymo's 2018 imitative planner, found standard <Term id="behavior-cloning">behavior cloning</Term> on 30 million examples insufficient. Its fix was to train on perturbed versions of the expert's driving, which create situations such as collisions or leaving the road, with added losses that penalize those events <Cite id="chauffeurnet-2018" />, a remedy for <Term id="covariate-shift">covariate shift</Term> that manipulation later rediscovered ([behavior cloning foundations](/manipulation/bc-foundations/)). On nuPlan, the first closed-loop benchmark for machine-learned planning <Cite id="nuplan-2021" />, a simple planner built on rule-based priors won the 2023 challenge, and its authors found learned planners limited in complex scenarios <Cite id="dauner-misconceptions-2023" />.

Control then turns the path into steering, brake and throttle and holds the car on it despite disturbances, tire limits and actuator delay, using methods from pure pursuit to <Term id="mpc">model-predictive control</Term> <Cite id="paden-2016" />. Even a learned planner's trajectory is tracked by a classical [controller](/classical/control/).

### One network or four

Each hand-off between stages is a lossy summary that later stages cannot undo. <Term id="end-to-end-driving">End-to-end</Term> systems train perception and planning together to avoid that loss, while modular designs are easier to interpret and debug <Cite id="e2e-ad-survey-2024" />. UniAD kept the four tasks but trained them as one network <Cite id="uniad-2023" />, and Waymo's EMMA, built on a multimodal large language model such as Gemini, maps raw camera data to driving outputs written as text <Cite id="emma-2024" />.

NAVSIM was built because scores on recorded driving do not reflect closed-loop performance, and on it a simple method, TransFuser, matched large end-to-end architectures such as UniAD <Cite id="navsim-2024" />. Waymo says its own foundation model has significant benefits over both pure end-to-end and modular approaches <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" />. Public benchmarks have moved from labeled frames toward closed-loop simulation.

## Robotaxis run at scale

Waymo says it gave 15 million rides in 2025 <Cite id="waymo-year-in-review-2025" />. Other operators publish less, and each reports a different unit.

| Operator | Service | Reported scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waymo | 15 major US cities, October 2026 <Cite id="alphabet-q3-2026" /> | 271.3 million rider-only miles through June 2026 <Cite id="waymo-safety-hub-2026" /> |
| Tesla | Miami, Orlando and Tampa from July 2026 <Cite id="tesla-q2-2026-update" />; Cybercab in Austin from September 3 <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" /> | Nearly two million unsupervised miles by end of September 2026 <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" /> |

Aurora reported zero collisions attributed to its system through June 2026 <Cite id="aurora-q2-2026" />; with production about six weeks behind plan, it expects to pass 200 driverless trucks by the end of February 2027 <Cite id="aurora-q3-2026" />.

### The long tail still causes recalls

Scale has not solved driving because of the <Term id="long-tail">long tail</Term>: situations that are individually rare and collectively decisive, such as an officer waving cars through a red light. No driving log holds enough of any one to learn from. Waymo's WOD-E2E benchmark gathers 4,021 segments, about 12 hours, of scenarios occurring less than 0.03 percent of the time <Cite id="wod-e2e-2025" />.

In December 2025 Waymo recalled software on 3,067 vehicles that resumed driving past school buses with red lights flashing or stop arms extended <Cite id="waymo-recall-25e084-2025" />. On November 6, 2026, NHTSA opened a query into whether the fix worked, since most incidents it had identified came after the November 17, 2025 remedy date <Cite id="nhtsa-rq26006-2026" />. In May 2026 Waymo recalled 3,791 vehicles that could slow but not stop for an untraversable flooded lane on higher-speed roads <Cite id="waymo-recall-26e026-2026" />.

One response is to generate rare events. Waymo's World Model (February 2026), built on Google DeepMind's Genie 3, generates camera and lidar data to rehearse events "from a tornado to a casual encounter with an elephant" <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. Wayve's GAIA-2 does the same for multi-camera video <Cite id="gaia-2-2025" />. Whether such models cover the tail or only their training data is open, as it is for [generative video](/world-models/generative-video/).

## Proving safety is a measurement problem

Kalra and Paddock's 2016 RAND study found that test driving alone would need hundreds of millions of miles, sometimes hundreds of billions <Cite id="kalra-paddock-2016" />. Mobileye's Responsibility-Sensitive Safety instead writes safe following and side distances as an explicit model <Cite id="rss-2017" />. Koopman argues that "safe enough" also requires no risk hot spots, standards conformance and accountability <Cite id="koopman-safe-enough-2026" />.

<AvSafetyMiles className="my-6" />

Waymo's peer-reviewed 2025 study of 56.7 million rider-only miles through January 2025, against human drivers in the same vehicle types, roads and places, found significantly fewer crashes with any reported injury, an airbag deployment or a suspected serious injury, and 96 percent fewer vehicle-to-vehicle intersection crashes with injury <Cite id="waymo-crash-rates-2025" />. Through June 2026 Waymo counts 95 percent fewer serious-injury-or-worse crashes and 82 percent fewer injury-causing crashes than human drivers <Cite id="waymo-safety-hub-2026" />. An independent IIHS study found 68 percent fewer police-reportable crashes per mile than human drivers in the same areas <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" /><Cite id="iihs-rise-of-machines-2026" />.

For most operators the denominator is missing. US companies must report crashes but not the miles they log <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" />, though Tesla now publishes its robotaxi collisions alongside its driverless mileage <Cite id="tesla-robotaxi-safety-2026" /> and NHTSA has streamlined the reporting order <Cite id="nhtsa-av-framework-2026" />. California's DMV says its disengagement reports are not designed for comparing companies, and deployment-permit operations need not report them <Cite id="ca-dmv-av-miles-2026" />.

## Regulators set the terms

SAE J3016 defines six levels of driving automation, 0 through 5 <Cite id="sae-j3016-2021" />. Waymo calls improving a level 2 driver-assist system toward full autonomy "a false summit" <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />. Every deployed level 4 system runs inside an <Term id="operational-design-domain">operational design domain</Term>, a defined set of places, roads, speeds and weather.

On June 24, 2026, the UNECE World Forum for Harmonization of Vehicle Regulations adopted the first global regulation for fully driverless systems <Cite id="un-ads-regulation-2026" /><Cite id="unece-ads-press-2026" />. Makers must show that their test environments, virtual ones included, meet strict credibility criteria, and present a <Term id="safety-case">safety case</Term> that the system poses no unreasonable risk <Cite id="unece-ads-press-2026" />; Aurora closed its own safety case before going driverless <Cite id="aurora-driverless-launch-2025" />. In the US, NHTSA announced an AV framework on April 24, 2025, and on March 16, 2026 proposed amending safety standards for vehicles without manual controls <Cite id="nhtsa-av-framework-2026" />. In July 2026 it exempted Zoox's purpose-built robotaxi from parts of eight standards for two years and up to 2,500 vehicles in any 12-month period <Cite id="nhtsa-zoox-exemption-2026" />, and Zoox began charging fares in Las Vegas <Cite id="amazon-q3-2026" />.

Trucks raise the stakes: Chris Paxton argued in September 2026 that their potential for catastrophic failure is much higher, since they need far longer to stop, and the law required roadside warning devices an unoccupied truck could not set out <Cite id="paxton-autonomous-trucks-2026" />. In October 2026 the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration let level 4 trucks use cab-mounted warning beacons instead, for five years <Cite id="fmcsa-aurora-beacons-2026" />.

## Two failures on public roads

On March 18, 2018, an Uber test vehicle in Tempe, Arizona killed a pedestrian pushing a bicycle across a road at night. The system detected her 5.6 seconds before impact, classified her in turn as a vehicle, an unknown object and a bicyclist, and never predicted her path correctly. It judged a collision imminent 1.2 seconds before impact, but its design ruled out emergency braking for crash mitigation alone and relied on the operator, who was streaming a television show <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />. Every layer failed at once, the correlated-safeguard problem of the [reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/).

On October 2, 2023, a driverless Cruise vehicle dragged a pedestrian about 20 feet. Under a September 2024 consent order Cruise agreed to a \$1.5 million penalty for crash reports that omitted those details <Cite id="nhtsa-cruise-consent-2024" />; that December GM stopped funding Cruise's robotaxi work, citing the time and resources scaling would need <Cite id="gm-cruise-refocus-2024" />.

## What robot learning took from driving

<Term id="dagger">DAgger</Term>, the standard fix for covariate shift, was demonstrated steering a car in a 3D racing game; it makes mistakes grow linearly with task length instead of quadratically <Cite id="dagger-2011" />. The Waymo Open Dataset set the pattern of shared perception benchmarks <Cite id="waymo-open-dataset-2020" />. Open X-Embodiment pools over a million real robot trajectories from 22 embodiments <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />, while Waymo's <Term id="scaling-law">scaling</Term> study used 500,000 hours of driving and found forecasting and planning improve as a power law in compute, closed-loop metrics included <Cite id="waymo-scaling-laws-2025" /> ([data bottleneck](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck/)). RAD goes past imitation, training driving policies by reinforcement learning inside photorealistic <Term id="gaussian-splatting">Gaussian splatting</Term> replicas of real places, and reports a 3x lower collision rate <Cite id="rad-2025" />.

## Driving models are becoming VLAs

EMMA, co-trained on planning, detection and road-graph tasks <Cite id="emma-2024" />, is a <Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action model</Term> whose body is a car. A late-2025 survey splits driving VLAs into single networks and dual systems, in which a slow vision-language model deliberates over fast, safety-critical execution <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />. Waymo's foundation model is a dual "Think Fast and Think Slow" design: a sensor-fusion encoder for rapid reactions beside a driving model trained using Gemini <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" />, mirroring [hierarchical](/manipulation/hierarchical/) manipulation and the case for [knowledge insulation](/manipulation/knowledge-insulation/). NVIDIA reports its Alpamayo-R1 runs onboard at 99 ms latency <Cite id="alpamayo-r1-2025" />.

These models do not reliably act on what they see or say, three October 2026 studies found. Six open video-language models tested at MIT CSAIL answered "forward" for all 200 simulated clips of a car reversing, until fine-tuning fixed it <Cite id="mind-temporal-gap-2026" />. Matteo Cercola, Marco Pavone and colleagues found that NVIDIA's Alpamayo-1.5 encodes hazards in its path but sets its speed mostly from its own speed history <Cite id="cercola-binding-gap-2026" />. Rewriting the reasoning text of four driving VLAs changed their planned paths far less than blanking the camera, Gabriel Manalu and colleagues found <Cite id="manalu-reasoning-coupling-2026" />.

## What does not transfer

A car has two controls, steering and speed, governed by tire friction and well-studied models. A manipulator moves dozens of joints while making and breaking contact, so the model-predictive control that tracks a car's path <Cite id="paden-2016" /> has no clean purchase on a robot wiping a table ([why RL won locomotion](/rl-sim2real/why-rl-locomotion/)).

Waymo says replaying recorded data in open-loop simulation is insufficient <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />, while manipulation still reports short-horizon success on small trial counts ([evaluation crisis](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis/)). A driving simulator has no contact to model, and Waymo says its World Model helps it scale safely <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />; robot learning tries the same with Genie-class models whose range of actions remains constrained <Cite id="genie-3-2025" />.

## What is still open

The open questions are whether dual-system driving VLAs settle into the shape of hierarchical manipulation systems <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />, whether generative simulators extend from appearance into contact, and whether robotics adopts the safety cases of driving, from RSS <Cite id="rss-2017" /> to credibility rules for virtual testing <Cite id="unece-ads-press-2026" />. [Drones](/adjacent/drones/) covers flight, where a policy trained in simulation has already beaten human champions.
