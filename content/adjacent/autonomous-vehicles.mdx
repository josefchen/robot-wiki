Autonomous vehicles are cars and trucks whose software perceives the road, predicts other road users, plans a path and steers. Waymo robotaxis passed 300 million fully autonomous miles by October 2026 <Cite id="alphabet-q3-2026" />, and driving is the robot-learning neighbor that already has fleets, recalls and a public crash record.

<Image id="waymo-jaguar-ipace-san-francisco-2023" />

## Driving splits into four stages

Production vehicles run perception, prediction, planning and control as separate stages <Cite id="uniad-2023" /><Cite id="paden-2016" />.

<AvStackTable className="my-6" />

Also: early learned drivers ALVINN, 1988 <Cite id="alvinn-1988" />, ALVINN in five minutes, 1991 <Cite id="pomerleau-1991" />, RALPH across the US, 1995 <Cite id="no-hands-across-america-1995" />, Stanley, 2005 <Cite id="stanley-2006" />.

### Perception fuses every sensor

Waymo fuses camera, <Term id="lidar">lidar</Term> and radar over time <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" /> and says no single sensor can replace that redundancy <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />. Camera-only BEVFormer matched lidar baselines on the nuScenes test set <Cite id="bevformer-2022" />.

Also: <Term id="point-cloud">point-cloud</Term> detectors PointPillars <Cite id="pointpillars-2018" />, PV-RCNN <Cite id="pv-rcnn-2019" />, CenterPoint <Cite id="centerpoint-2020" />.

<Image id="waymo-roof-sensor-suite-2025" />

### Prediction keeps several futures

A pedestrian may cross or wait, and the average of the two is neither. VectorNet read lanes and tracks as line segments and matched image-based predictors with over 70 percent fewer parameters <Cite id="vectornet-2020" />. MotionLM writes several agents' motion as tokens, like words <Cite id="motionlm-2023" />.

Also: multi-future predictors MultiPath <Cite id="multipath-2019" />, LaneGCN <Cite id="lanegcn-2020" />, Trajectron++ <Cite id="trajectron-plus-plus-2020" />, Wayformer <Cite id="wayformer-2022" />, Motion Transformer <Cite id="motion-transformer-2022" />.

### Planning needs more than copying drivers

CMU's Boss won the 2007 DARPA Urban Challenge with a three-layer planner for mission, behavior and motion <Cite id="boss-urban-challenge-2008" />, the split Paden's survey uses <Cite id="paden-2016" /> ([motion planning](/classical/motion-planning/)).

<Image id="cmu-boss-urban-challenge-winner" />

Waymo's ChauffeurNet found <Term id="behavior-cloning">behavior cloning</Term> on 30 million examples insufficient, so it trained on perturbed expert driving with losses that penalize collisions <Cite id="chauffeurnet-2018" />. Manipulation later rediscovered that cure for <Term id="covariate-shift">covariate shift</Term> ([behavior cloning foundations](/manipulation/bc-foundations/)).

Control tracks the path with methods from pure pursuit to <Term id="mpc">model-predictive control</Term>, even behind a learned planner <Cite id="paden-2016" /> ([control](/classical/control/)).

### Simple planners still match end-to-end networks

Every hand-off between stages loses information, so <Term id="end-to-end-driving">end-to-end</Term> systems train the stages together; modular ones are easier to debug <Cite id="e2e-ad-survey-2024" />. UniAD trains all four as one network <Cite id="uniad-2023" />, and EMMA writes driving outputs as text <Cite id="emma-2024" />.

In closed loop, a planner built on rule-based priors won the 2023 nuPlan challenge <Cite id="nuplan-2021" /><Cite id="dauner-misconceptions-2023" />, and on NAVSIM the simple TransFuser matched UniAD <Cite id="navsim-2024" />.

| Benchmark | Year | What it tests |
| --- | --- | --- |
| KITTI | 2012 | Stereo, optical flow, odometry, 3D detection <Cite id="kitti-benchmark-2012" /> |
| Cityscapes | 2016 | Pixel labels of city streets <Cite id="cityscapes-2016" /> |

## Robotaxis run at scale

Waymo gave 15 million rides in 2025 <Cite id="waymo-year-in-review-2025" />. Every operator reports a different unit.

| Operator | Service | Reported scale |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waymo | 15 major US cities, October 2026 <Cite id="alphabet-q3-2026" /> | 271.3 million rider-only miles through June 2026 <Cite id="waymo-safety-hub-2026" /> |
| Tesla | Miami, Orlando and Tampa from July 2026 <Cite id="tesla-q2-2026-update" />; Cybercab in Austin from September 3 <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" /> | Nearly two million unsupervised miles by end of September 2026 <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" /> |
| Baidu Apollo Go | 28 cities, August 2026 <Cite id="baidu-q2-2026" /> | Over 240 million fully driverless kilometers <Cite id="baidu-q2-2026" /> |

### The long tail still causes recalls

The <Term id="long-tail">long tail</Term> is the mass of rare situations, such as an officer waving cars through a red light, that no log holds enough of to learn from. Waymo's WOD-E2E benchmark gathers 4,021 segments, about 12 hours, of scenarios rarer than 0.03 percent <Cite id="wod-e2e-2025" />.

| Date | Action | Defect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dec 2025 | Waymo recall, 3,067 vehicles | Drove on past school buses with red lights flashing <Cite id="waymo-recall-25e084-2025" /> |
| May 2026 | Waymo recall, 3,791 vehicles | Slowed but did not stop for a flooded lane <Cite id="waymo-recall-26e026-2026" /> |
| Nov 2026 | NHTSA query | Most school-bus incidents came after the fix <Cite id="nhtsa-rq26006-2026" /> |

Waymo's World Model, built on Genie 3, generates camera and lidar data for rare events "from a tornado to a casual encounter with an elephant" <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. Wayve's GAIA-2 does the same for video <Cite id="gaia-2-2025" />. Whether they reach past their training data is open ([generative video](/world-models/generative-video/)).

## Proving safety is a measurement problem

Test driving alone would need hundreds of millions of miles, sometimes hundreds of billions, RAND found <Cite id="kalra-paddock-2016" />. Mobileye's Responsibility-Sensitive Safety writes safe distances as an explicit model instead <Cite id="rss-2017" />, and Koopman adds accountability and no risk hot spots to "safe enough" <Cite id="koopman-safe-enough-2026" />.

<AvSafetyMiles className="my-6" />

Waymo crashes far less than human drivers on the same roads.

| Study | Scope | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Waymo, peer-reviewed, 2025 | 56.7 million rider-only miles | 96% fewer injury crashes between vehicles at intersections <Cite id="waymo-crash-rates-2025" /> |
| Waymo safety hub | Through June 2026 | 95% fewer serious-injury crashes, 82% fewer injury crashes <Cite id="waymo-safety-hub-2026" /> |
| IIHS, independent, 2026 | Same areas as human drivers | 68% fewer police-reportable crashes per mile <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" /><Cite id="iihs-rise-of-machines-2026" /> |

Most operators publish no denominator: US companies must report crashes but not miles <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" />. Tesla now pairs its robotaxi collisions with mileage <Cite id="tesla-robotaxi-safety-2026" />. California says its disengagement reports cannot compare companies <Cite id="ca-dmv-av-miles-2026" />.

## Regulators set the terms

SAE J3016 defines six levels of automation, 0 through 5 <Cite id="sae-j3016-2021" />, and Waymo calls pushing level 2 driver assist toward full autonomy "a false summit" <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />. Every deployed level 4 system runs inside an <Term id="operational-design-domain">operational design domain</Term>, a defined set of places, roads, speeds and weather.

| Date | Regulator | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| June 2026 | UNECE | First global rule for driverless systems: credible virtual tests and a <Term id="safety-case">safety case</Term> <Cite id="un-ads-regulation-2026" /><Cite id="unece-ads-press-2026" /> |
| March 2026 | NHTSA | Proposed standards for cars without manual controls; streamlined crash reporting <Cite id="nhtsa-av-framework-2026" /> |
| July 2026 | NHTSA | Zoox robotaxi exempt from parts of eight standards, up to 2,500 a year for two years <Cite id="nhtsa-zoox-exemption-2026" /> |
| October 2026 | FMCSA | Level 4 trucks may use cab-mounted beacons for five years <Cite id="fmcsa-aurora-beacons-2026" /> |

Aurora closed its safety case before going driverless <Cite id="aurora-driverless-launch-2025" />, and Zoox began charging fares in Las Vegas <Cite id="amazon-q3-2026" />. Trucks take far longer to stop, and an empty cab could not set out the roadside warning devices the law required, Chris Paxton noted <Cite id="paxton-autonomous-trucks-2026" />.

## Uber and Cruise failed on public roads

In 2018 an Uber test car in Tempe, Arizona killed a pedestrian walking a bicycle at night. It saw her 5.6 seconds out, kept reclassifying her, and left emergency braking to an operator who was streaming a television show <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />. Every layer failed at once ([reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/)).

In 2023 a driverless Cruise car dragged a pedestrian about 20 feet. Cruise agreed to a \$1.5 million penalty for crash reports that left this out <Cite id="nhtsa-cruise-consent-2024" />, and GM stopped funding its robotaxis that December <Cite id="gm-cruise-refocus-2024" />.

## Robot learning borrowed driving's tools

Driving gave robot learning four tools ([data bottleneck](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck/)).

| Tool | What driving showed | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <Term id="dagger">DAgger</Term> | Fixes covariate shift; errors grow linearly with task length, not quadratically | Racing-game car <Cite id="dagger-2011" /> |
| Shared benchmarks | Set the pattern for shared datasets; Open X-Embodiment pools a million robot trajectories from 22 embodiments <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" /> | Waymo Open Dataset <Cite id="waymo-open-dataset-2020" /> |
| <Term id="scaling-law">Scaling laws</Term> | Forecasting and planning improve as a power law in compute | 500,000 hours of driving <Cite id="waymo-scaling-laws-2025" /> |
| RL in <Term id="gaussian-splatting">Gaussian splatting</Term> | Training inside replicas of real places cuts collisions threefold | RAD <Cite id="rad-2025" /> |

## Driving models are becoming VLAs

EMMA is a <Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action model</Term> whose body is a car <Cite id="emma-2024" />. Driving VLAs are single networks or dual systems, with a slow vision-language model over fast execution <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />, the shape of [hierarchical](/manipulation/hierarchical/) manipulation.

Waymo pairs a fast sensor-fusion encoder with a driving model trained using Gemini <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" /> ([knowledge insulation](/manipulation/knowledge-insulation/)). NVIDIA's Alpamayo-R1 runs onboard at 99 ms <Cite id="alpamayo-r1-2025" />.

These models do not reliably act on what they see or say.

| Study, October 2026 | Test | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| MIT CSAIL <Cite id="mind-temporal-gap-2026" /> | 200 clips of a car reversing | Six video-language models answered "forward" every time, until fine-tuned |
| Cercola, Pavone and colleagues <Cite id="cercola-binding-gap-2026" /> | NVIDIA Alpamayo-1.5 | Hazards shape its path; its own speed history sets its speed |
| Manalu and colleagues <Cite id="manalu-reasoning-coupling-2026" /> | Four driving VLAs | Rewriting the reasoning moved paths far less than blanking the camera |

## Contact does not transfer

A car has two controls, steering and speed, under well-studied tire models. A manipulator moves dozens of joints while making and breaking contact, where the control that tracks a car <Cite id="paden-2016" /> has no clean purchase ([why RL won locomotion](/rl-sim2real/why-rl-locomotion/)).

Waymo calls open-loop replay insufficient <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />, yet manipulation still reports short tasks on few trials ([evaluation crisis](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis/)). A driving simulator has no contact to model, and Waymo says its World Model helps it scale safely <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. Genie-class models for robots still offer a narrow range of actions <Cite id="genie-3-2025" />.

Open: whether driving VLAs converge on hierarchical manipulation <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />, and whether robotics adopts driving's safety cases, from RSS <Cite id="rss-2017" /> to credibility rules for virtual tests <Cite id="unece-ads-press-2026" />. [Drones](/adjacent/drones/) covers flight, where a sim-trained policy has beaten human champions.
