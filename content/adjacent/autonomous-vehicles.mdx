Autonomous vehicles are cars and trucks that drive themselves, with sensors and software for perception, prediction, planning and control. The field runs from ALVINN, a 1988 neural network that steered a van from camera and laser range-finder images <Cite id="alvinn-1988" />, to Waymo robotaxis that gave more than 500,000 fully autonomous rides a week in April 2026 <Cite id="alphabet-q1-2026" />.

<Image id="waymo-jaguar-ipace-san-francisco-2023" />

ALVINN trained on simulated road images <Cite id="alvinn-1988" />. By 1991 it learned to drive the Navlab van in under five minutes by watching a human driver <Cite id="pomerleau-1991" />. On the 1995 No Hands Across America tour, CMU's RALPH program steered for 2,797 of 2,849 miles, 98.2 percent, while the researchers handled throttle and brake <Cite id="no-hands-across-america-1995" />. Stanley won the 2005 DARPA Grand Challenge with software built mainly on machine learning and probabilistic reasoning <Cite id="stanley-2006" />.

## The driving stack

A production autonomous vehicle is a pipeline of perception, prediction, planning and control, each with its own models, metrics and failure modes <Cite id="uniad-2023" /><Cite id="paden-2016" />. UniAD calls this "modular tasks in sequential order" <Cite id="uniad-2023" />. Paden's survey adds a vehicle-control layer below motion planning <Cite id="paden-2016" />.

<AvStackTable className="my-6" />

### Perception

Perception turns sensor streams into a model of the scene: which objects exist, where they are, how they move and what the road permits. Waymo's encoder fuses camera, lidar and radar inputs over time <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" />, and Waymo argues that no single sensor can replicate that redundant view <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />. BEVFormer, a camera-only bird's-eye-view detector, matched lidar-based baselines on the nuScenes test set <Cite id="bevformer-2022" />.

<Image id="waymo-roof-sensor-suite-2025" />

nuScenes, 1,000 scenes of 20 seconds, was the first dataset with a full vehicle sensor suite: 6 cameras, 5 radars and 1 lidar <Cite id="nuscenes-2020" />. The Waymo Open Dataset added 1,150 scenes of synchronized lidar and camera data <Cite id="waymo-open-dataset-2020" />. By its own metric it was 15 times more diverse than the largest earlier camera-plus-lidar set <Cite id="waymo-open-dataset-2020" />. Box detectors struggle with general, out-of-vocabulary objects, so the Occ3D benchmark predicts the occupancy and semantics of the whole scene <Cite id="occ3d-2023" />.

Every later stage inherits a perception error. At Tempe in 2018 the system first detected a pedestrian 5.6 seconds before impact <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />. It classified her in turn as a vehicle, an unknown object and a bicyclist, and never predicted her path correctly <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />.

### Prediction

Prediction estimates what every other agent will do over the next several seconds. The future is multi-modal: a pedestrian may cross or wait, and a predictor that averages the two forecasts a path through neither. The Waymo Open Motion Dataset made interactive forecasting a benchmark: over 100,000 scenes of 20 seconds at 10 Hz, more than 570 hours over 1,750 km of roads <Cite id="waymo-open-motion-2021" />.

Early predictors drew agent histories and map features into a top-down image for a convolutional network. VectorNet replaced that raster with a hierarchical graph network over vectorized polylines <Cite id="vectornet-2020" />. It matched or beat the raster with over 70 percent fewer parameters and an order of magnitude less computation <Cite id="vectornet-2020" />. MotionLM then cast multi-agent forecasting as language modeling over discrete motion tokens <Cite id="motionlm-2023" />.

### Planning

Planning decides the route, lane, speed profile and trajectory for the next few seconds. CMU's Boss won the 2007 DARPA Urban Challenge with a three-layer planner for mission, behavior and motion <Cite id="boss-urban-challenge-2008" />. Paden's survey splits urban planning the same way, with feedback control below <Cite id="paden-2016" />, the families covered under [motion planning](/classical/motion-planning/).

<Image id="cmu-boss-urban-challenge-winner" />

On nuPlan, the first closed-loop machine-learning planning benchmark <Cite id="nuplan-2021" />, an extremely simple planner built on rule-based priors won the 2023 challenge <Cite id="dauner-misconceptions-2023" />. Dauner and colleagues found learned planners limited in complex scenarios <Cite id="dauner-misconceptions-2023" />.

ChauffeurNet, Waymo's 2018 imitative planner, found standard behavior cloning on 30 million examples insufficient, even with perception preparing its inputs and a controller executing its outputs <Cite id="chauffeurnet-2018" />. It perturbed the expert's driving into collisions and off-road excursions and added losses that penalize them <Cite id="chauffeurnet-2018" />. Manipulation later rediscovered that fix for covariate shift, as [behavior cloning foundations](/manipulation/bc-foundations/) explains.

### Control

Control turns a planned trajectory into steering, brake and throttle commands, and holds the vehicle on it despite disturbances, tire nonlinearity and actuator delay. Paden's survey covers the feedback path-tracking families: pure pursuit, Lyapunov-based trajectory tracking and model-predictive control <Cite id="paden-2016" />. A learned planner outputs a trajectory, and a classical [controller](/classical/control/) tracks it.

### Modular versus end-to-end

Each interface between stages is a lossy summary, and an upstream error cannot be corrected downstream. A 2024 survey frames the trade: end-to-end systems optimize perception and planning features jointly, while modular designs are easier to interpret and debug <Cite id="e2e-ad-survey-2024" />. UniAD kept the task structure but trained it as one network, with stages linked by unified query interfaces <Cite id="uniad-2023" />. Waymo's EMMA, built on a multimodal large language model foundation like Gemini, maps raw camera data to driving outputs written as text <Cite id="emma-2024" />.

On NAVSIM, open-loop results on real data did not reflect closed-loop performance <Cite id="navsim-2024" />. Simple methods such as TransFuser matched large architectures such as UniAD <Cite id="navsim-2024" />. Waymo says its own foundation model has significant benefits over both pure end-to-end and modular approaches <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" />.

## Why AV is not solved

Robotaxis now run at scale, and driving is still not solved. Waymo says it gave 15 million rides in 2025 and passed 20 million lifetime rides <Cite id="waymo-year-in-review-2025" />. By April 2026 it operated in 11 major US cities <Cite id="alphabet-q1-2026" />.

### The levels and the operating domain

SAE J3016 defines six levels of driving automation, 0 through 5. Its 2021 revision named levels 1 and 2 "Driver Support Systems" and clarified the line between levels 3 and 4 <Cite id="sae-j3016-2021" />. Waymo calls improving a level 2 driver-assist system toward full autonomy "a false summit" <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />.

Every deployed level 4 system runs inside an <Term id="operational-design-domain">operational design domain</Term>, a defined geography, road set, speed range and weather envelope. Waymo's crash comparisons align the human benchmark to the same vehicle types, road types and locations <Cite id="waymo-crash-rates-2025" />.

On June 24, 2026, the UNECE World Forum for Harmonization of Vehicle Regulations adopted the first global regulations for fully autonomous driving systems <Cite id="un-ads-regulation-2026" />. Manufacturers must show that test environments, including virtual testing tools, meet strict credibility criteria, and that the system poses no unreasonable risk <Cite id="un-ads-regulation-2026" />. In the US, DOT and NHTSA announced an AV framework on April 24, 2025. On March 16, 2026, NHTSA proposed amending federal safety standards for automated vehicles without manual controls <Cite id="nhtsa-av-framework-2026" />.

In July 2026 NHTSA exempted Zoox's purpose-built robotaxi from parts of eight of those standards for commercial deployment <Cite id="nhtsa-zoox-exemption-2026" />. The exemption runs two years, for up to 2,500 vehicles in any 12-month period <Cite id="nhtsa-zoox-exemption-2026" />.

### The long tail

The technical obstruction is the <Term id="long-tail">long tail</Term>: scenarios that are individually rare and collectively decisive, such as an officer waving cars through a red light. No driving log holds enough of any one of them to learn from. Waymo's WOD-E2E benchmark collects 4,021 segments, about 12 hours, of scenarios that occur less than 0.03 percent of the time <Cite id="wod-e2e-2025" />.

Generative simulation is the industry's main answer. Waymo's World Model, built on Google DeepMind's Genie 3 and announced in February 2026, generates camera and lidar data controllable by driving actions, scene layout and language <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. Waymo built it to rehearse events "from a tornado to a casual encounter with an elephant" <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. The same post reported nearly 200 million fully autonomous miles on real roads and billions in virtual worlds <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />.

Wayve's GAIA-2 likewise generates multi-camera driving video for common and rare scenarios <Cite id="gaia-2-2025" />. Whether such models cover the tail or only what their training data showed is open, as it is for [generative video](/world-models/generative-video/) in manipulation.

The tail still produces recalls. In December 2025 Waymo recalled software on 3,067 vehicles that resumed driving past school buses with red lights flashing or stop arms extended <Cite id="waymo-recall-25e084-2025" />. In May 2026 it recalled 3,791 vehicles that could slow but not stop for an untraversable flooded lane on higher-speed roads <Cite id="waymo-recall-26e026-2026" />.

### The measurement problem

Kalra and Paddock's 2016 RAND study found that test driving alone would need hundreds of millions of miles, sometimes hundreds of billions, to show safety <Cite id="kalra-paddock-2016" />. Mobileye's Responsibility-Sensitive Safety defines safe longitudinal and lateral distances as a white-box, interpretable model <Cite id="rss-2017" />. Its authors warned that without such standards the field risked a "winter of autonomous driving" <Cite id="rss-2017" />. Koopman argues that "safe enough" also requires no risk hot spots, such as reckless driving or risk shifted onto vulnerable populations, plus standards conformance and accountability <Cite id="koopman-safe-enough-2026" />.

US companies must report crashes but not the miles they log, so crash rates are unknown for operators that do not volunteer mileage <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" />. NHTSA has also streamlined its Standing General Order on crash reporting <Cite id="nhtsa-av-framework-2026" />. California's DMV says its disengagement reports are not designed for comparison across companies, and deployment-permit operations need not report disengagements at all <Cite id="ca-dmv-av-miles-2026" />.

In September 2026, Chris Paxton argued that an automated system should be judged by its potential for catastrophic failure, which is much higher for a truck: it needs far longer to stop, and when one breaks down on a highway the law requires warning flares that a truck with no one aboard cannot set out <Cite id="paxton-autonomous-trucks-2026" />. He expected driverless trucks to spread over the following year mainly in China and the US Sun Belt, and put highways where ice forms years away <Cite id="paxton-autonomous-trucks-2026" />.

### The deployment record

Waymo's peer-reviewed 2025 study covered 56.7 million rider-only miles through January 2025 <Cite id="waymo-crash-rates-2025" />. Against human benchmarks on the same roads, it found statistically significant lower rates of any-injury-reported, airbag-deployment and suspected-serious-injury crashes <Cite id="waymo-crash-rates-2025" />. Vehicle-to-vehicle intersection crashes with any injury fell 96 percent, with no significant disbenefit in any of its 11 crash-type groups <Cite id="waymo-crash-rates-2025" />.

Waymo's own safety hub reports 271.3 million rider-only miles through June 2026 <Cite id="waymo-safety-hub-2026" />. Over the same distance it counts 95 percent fewer serious-injury-or-worse crashes and 82 percent fewer injury-causing crashes than human drivers <Cite id="waymo-safety-hub-2026" />. IIHS found that Waymo's driverless vehicles in San Francisco, Phoenix, Los Angeles and Austin had 68 percent fewer police-reportable crashes per mile than human drivers <Cite id="iihs-waymo-2026" />.

Tesla says it launched unsupervised rides in Miami, Orlando and Tampa in July 2026 and expanded its unsupervised area in Austin <Cite id="tesla-q2-2026-update" />. Baidu reported in August 2026 that Apollo Go had reached 28 cities and more than 240 million fully driverless kilometres <Cite id="baidu-q2-2026" />. Aurora began driverless customer deliveries between Dallas and Houston in May 2025 after closing its safety case <Cite id="aurora-driverless-launch-2025" />. By its count it drove nearly 440,000 driverless miles through June 2026 with zero collisions attributed to its system, and expected to end the year with 200 driverless trucks <Cite id="aurora-q2-2026" />.

On March 18, 2018, an Uber ATG test vehicle in Tempe, Arizona struck and killed a 49-year-old pedestrian crossing N. Mill Avenue at night, pushing a bicycle, where there was no crosswalk <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />. The system judged a collision imminent 1.2 seconds before impact, and its design precluded emergency braking for crash mitigation alone, relying on the operator <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />. The operator was streaming a television show, glancing down until about one second before the crash <Cite id="ntsb-uber-2019" />. Every layer failed at once, the correlated-safeguard problem the [reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/) article examines.

On October 2, 2023, a driverless Cruise vehicle dragged a pedestrian about 20 feet before stopping. Under a September 2024 consent order, Cruise agreed to a \$1.5 million penalty for crash reports that omitted those details <Cite id="nhtsa-cruise-consent-2024" />. In December 2024 GM stopped funding Cruise's robotaxi work, citing the time and resources needed to scale it <Cite id="gm-cruise-refocus-2024" />.

## The relation to robot learning

The imitation toolkit was built on driving before it reached arms. DAgger, the canonical fix for covariate shift, was demonstrated on steering a car in a 3D racing game <Cite id="dagger-2011" />. It cuts the growth of mistakes over the horizon from quadratic to linear <Cite id="dagger-2011" />. On 500,000 hours of driving, Waymo found that forecasting and planning improve as a power law in compute, and closed-loop metrics improve too <Cite id="waymo-scaling-laws-2025" />.

RAD trains end-to-end policies by reinforcement learning in photorealistic 3D Gaussian splatting replicas, to address imitation's causal confusion and open-loop gap <Cite id="rad-2025" />. It reports a 3x lower collision rate <Cite id="rad-2025" />.

### The convergence is now explicit

EMMA is a vision-language model co-trained as a generalist on planning, detection and road-graph tasks, with its driving outputs written as text <Cite id="emma-2024" />. It is a <Term id="vision-language-action-model">vision-language-action model</Term> whose embodiment is a car. A late-2025 survey splits driving VLAs into end-to-end models, which perceive, reason and plan in one network, and dual-system models, where a slow VLM deliberates over fast, safety-critical execution <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />.

Waymo's foundation model uses such a "Think Fast and Think Slow" design, a sensor-fusion encoder for rapid reactions beside a driving VLM trained using Gemini <Cite id="waymo-foundation-model-2025" />. The split mirrors the end-to-end versus [hierarchical](/manipulation/hierarchical/) divide in manipulation and the [knowledge insulation](/manipulation/knowledge-insulation/) case for a separate fast path. NVIDIA reports that its Alpamayo-R1 driving VLA runs on the vehicle in real time at 99 ms latency <Cite id="alpamayo-r1-2025" />.

The Waymo Open Dataset set the pattern of shared perception benchmarks <Cite id="waymo-open-dataset-2020" />. Open X-Embodiment pools over a million real robot trajectories across 22 embodiments from 34 labs <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />. Driving data is counted in hundreds of thousands of hours, such as the 500,000 in Waymo's scaling study <Cite id="waymo-scaling-laws-2025" />, a contrast that [the data bottleneck](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck/) examines.

### What differs in kind

A car steers and accelerates through two channels governed by tire friction and well-studied vehicle models. A manipulator moves dozens of joints that make and break contact. The <Term id="mpc">model-predictive control</Term> that tracks a car's trajectory <Cite id="paden-2016" /> has no equally clean purchase on a robot wiping a table, the argument of [why RL won locomotion](/rl-sim2real/why-rl-locomotion/).

Evaluation differs too. Waymo says replaying recorded data in open-loop simulation is insufficient <Cite id="waymo-ai-lessons-2026" />, and nuPlan moved planning evaluation from open-loop L2 metrics to closed-loop simulation <Cite id="nuplan-2021" />. Manipulation still reports short-horizon success on small trial counts, the subject of the [evaluation crisis](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis/).

Driving simulation covers rigid-body motion and appearance with no contact-rich phase, and Waymo says its World Model helps it scale its service safely <Cite id="waymo-world-model-2026" />. Robot learning attempts the same with Genie-class models whose range of actions is still constrained <Cite id="genie-3-2025" />.

### What to watch

One open question is whether dual-system driving VLAs settle into the shape of hierarchical manipulation systems <Cite id="vla-ad-survey-2026" />. Another is whether generative simulators extend from appearance into contact. A third is whether robotics adopts AV's safety-case machinery, from formal models like RSS <Cite id="rss-2017" /> to credibility rules for virtual testing <Cite id="un-ads-regulation-2026" />, before robots deploy at scale. [Drones](/adjacent/drones/) covers flight, where a policy trained in simulation has already beaten human champions.
