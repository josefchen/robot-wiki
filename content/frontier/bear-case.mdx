The bear case says robot learning will repeat earlier AI winters: money arrives on demonstrations, useful work lags, and funding runs out before robots are reliable enough to sell. Crunchbase counted \$9.9B of robotics venture funding in the third quarter of 2026, more than double a year earlier <Cite id="crunchbase-robotics-q3-2026" />.

<Image id="asimo-miraikan-2016" />

Eight milestones would settle it. RL-100 and π*0.6 report some of the highest published success rates, from policies trained for specific tasks, toward one of them: reinforcement learning above 99% <Cite id="rl-100-2025" /> <Cite id="pistar06-2025" />.

<span id="the-skeptic-record" />
## Brooks calls the dexterity forecasts fantasy, and Morgan Stanley asks for ROI

Rodney Brooks calls practical humanoid dexterity from current methods within decades "pure fantasy thinking", because video carries no sense of force ([dexterity](/frontier/dexterity/)) <Cite id="brooks-dexterity-2025" />. His January 2026 scorecard says deployable dexterity "will remain pathetic compared to human hands" beyond 2036 <Cite id="brooks-scorecard-2026" />. The iRobot co-founder's Rethink Robotics raised \$150 million and closed in 2018 when sales fell short <Cite id="rethink-shutdown-2018" />.

Also: Brooks on 50-year deployment cycles <Cite id="brooks-four-time-scales-2026" />, on machine learning running out of steam <Cite id="brooks-does-until-2026" />.

<Image id="roomba-870-2014" />

Morgan Stanley's July 2026 note says humanoids have a "PR problem" and that investors "are increasingly looking for tangible evidence of real-world return on investment" <Cite id="morgan-stanley-pr-problem-2026" />.

<span id="unit-economics" />
## Revenue is still small next to losses

Agility's merger filings show \$1.8 million of 2025 revenue against a \$138.1 million net loss, with over half the revenue from five robots one investor bought <Cite id="bi-agility-ipo-2026" />. Unitree's Q1 2026 adjusted net profit fell 52.55% <Cite id="unitree-prospectus-2026" />. Bessemer expects data costs above \$3B over two years and calls capital the moat <Cite id="bessemer-robotics-2026" /> ([industrial deployment](/data-hardware/industrial-deployment/)).

<span id="the-demo-to-deployment-gap" />
## Vendor hours and shipment counts do not document productive work

Agility reports 65,000+ hours of Digit operation <Cite id="agility-nasdaq-listing-2026" /> and Figure 1,250+ hours at BMW Spartanburg <Cite id="figure-bmw-production-2025" />, but neither counts autonomous units or supervised time ([evaluation crisis](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis/)). BMW told Forbes only one Figure robot worked at a time <Cite id="forbes-adcock-figure-2026" />.

| Tracker | Humanoid count |
| --- | --- |
| Omdia | About 13,000 shipped in 2025 <Cite id="agibot-omdia-ranking-2026" /> <Cite id="agibot-omdia-2026" /> |
| SAG | 19.1K shipped in the first half of 2026 <Cite id="sag-humanoid-shipments-2026" /> |
| IFR | About 7,000 full-size humanoids sold in 2025 <Cite id="ifr-service-robots-2026" /> |

Tesla says Optimus production began in Fremont <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" />, but Elon Musk put outside deliveries near the end of 2027 and called the hand the hardest problem <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-call" />.

<span id="the-capital-cycle" />
## Robotics funding passed 23 billion dollars by June 2026, and China repriced first

Crunchbase put 2026 robotics funding at \$18.8B by late June <Cite id="crunchbase-robotics-funding-2026" />, and a June 2 newsletter, naming no source, put it above \$23B <Cite id="robotics-funding-23b-2026" />. Bessemer calls the sector "structurally underinvested" while conceding "some valuations are stretched" <Cite id="bessemer-robotics-2026" />. Brooks calls some humanoid hype "downright delusional" <Cite id="brooks-scorecard-2026" />; Morgan Stanley makes no bubble call <Cite id="morgan-stanley-pr-problem-2026" /> ([competing theses](/frontier/competing-theses/)).

<FundingHumanoidCounts className="my-6" />

After Unitree's shares rose more than fivefold on debut and fell 55% from the peak, Chinese regulators slowed humanoid listings <Cite id="reuters-china-humanoid-ipo-2026" />. In late October, after a 65% rise in seven trading days, Unitree warned investors of valuation risks <Cite id="caixin-unitree-valuation-2026" />.

<span id="how-a-winter-would-happen" />
## Three hypothetical failures could start a winter

The precedent is Britain's 1973 Lighthill report, which found robot-building progress "even more disappointing" than the rest of AI <Cite id="lighthill-1973" />.

| Failure | What it would mean |
| --- | --- |
| The [reliability gap](/frontier/reliability-gap/) outlasts patience | The last 20% "takes longer than most timelines assume" <Cite id="bessemer-robotics-2026" />, and 2026 funds get judged on pilots |
| Data stops paying | Ten times the data buys no <Term id="success-rate">success-rate</Term> gain, so touch, contact-faithful simulation or world models must supply it ([data bottleneck](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck/)) |
| The humanoid form is wrong | Task-specific systems keep winning on cost per task <Cite id="brooks-dexterity-2025" /> |

Ken Goldberg grants that data-driven methods "will eventually produce general purpose robots"; his 100,000-year data gap asks when <Cite id="goldberg-data-gap-2025" />.

<span id="the-watchlist" />
## As of November 2026, none of the eight milestones is met

Progress still falls short of the bars:

| Result | Reported |
| --- | --- |
| π0.5 | Cleaned kitchens and bedrooms in unseen homes <Cite id="pi05-2025" /> |
| Figure Helix | 56% zero-shot success in 30 unseen homes, against 9% without Index pretraining <Cite id="helix-2-5-2026" /> |
| Generalist GEN-1 | 99% average success against fine-tuned GEN-0's 64%, though "not all tasks" reach it <Cite id="generalist-gen1-2026" /> |
| BEHAVIOR Challenge | Best full-task simulated success up from 12% to 48% in a year <Cite id="behavior-challenge-2026-results" /> |

Touch is arriving. Figure's Helix 02 reads fingertip tactile sensors <Cite id="helix-02-2026" />, and LingBot-Touch feeds touch into <Term id="vision-language-action-model">VLA</Term> models such as π0.5 <Cite id="lingbot-touch-2026" />. T-Rex beats its strongest baseline by over 30%, though most VLAs still "overlook the tactile modality" <Cite id="t-rex-2026" />.

Surveys name [contact modeling](/rl-sim2real/sim2real-transfer/) a key source of the <Term id="sim-to-real-gap">sim-to-real gap</Term>, "particularly problematic in contact-rich tasks" <Cite id="reality-gap-survey-2026" />.

Also, shared evaluation: RoboArena <Cite id="roboarena-2025" />, RoboChallenge <Cite id="robochallenge-2025" />, ManipulationNet <Cite id="manipulationnet-2026" />.

## Four measurable results would falsify the bear case

| Result | Premise it breaks |
| --- | --- |
| A verified deployment of 10,000 humanoids doing documented work | Commercial, now measured in vendor-recorded hours <Cite id="agility-digit-production" /> <Cite id="figure-bmw-production-2025" /> |

| A real-world <Term id="scaling-law">scaling law</Term> for success rate across tasks | Plateau: EgoScale's law fits validation loss <Cite id="egoscale-2026" />, and Lin et al. find a "roughly power-law relationship" for single-task generalization <Cite id="lin-data-scaling-laws-2024" /> |
