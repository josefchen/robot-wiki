A robot learning dataset records what a robot's cameras and sensors saw, paired with the commands it carried out. Open datasets trade breadth for consistency: <Term id="open-x-embodiment">Open X-Embodiment</Term> pools over a million trajectories from 22 kinds of robot <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />, while all of DROID's 76,000 come from one arm setup <Cite id="droid-2024" />.

<Image id="open-x-embodiment-overview-2023" />

## Count datasets in hours, not episodes

<DatasetTable className="my-6 wide-measure" />

A trajectory can last eight seconds or eight minutes: DROID's average about 17 seconds and AgiBot World's about 11 <Cite id="droid-2024" /> <Cite id="agibot-world-2025" />. NVIDIA's open Physical AI dataset counts bytes, 15 terabytes for 320,000+ trajectories <Cite id="nvidia-physical-ai-dataset-2025" />; the 2025 BEHAVIOR Challenge counts 1,200+ hours in 10,000 teleoperated demonstrations <Cite id="behavior-challenge-2025" />.

<DatasetRunsHours className="my-6" />

Also: synthetic InternData-A1, 7,433 hours <Cite id="interndata-a1-2025" />.

## Open X-Embodiment pools many labs in one format

OXE (2023) converted 60 datasets from 34 labs into the shared RLDS episode format <Cite id="rlds-2021" />, for over a million trajectories across 22 robot types and 527 skills <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />.

The pool helped small datasets most. RT-1-X beat each one's original method by 50% on average, and RT-2-X roughly tripled RT-2's success on skills seen only in another robot's data <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />.

Later <Term id="cross-embodiment">cross-embodiment</Term> pools are larger:

| Pool | Size | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ARIO | About 3 million episodes | <Cite id="ario-2024" /> |
| CrossFormer | 900,000 trajectories from 20 embodiments, quadcopters among them | <Cite id="crossformer-2024" /> |
| OXE-AugE | Over 4.4 million trajectories, adding nine robot types | <Cite id="oxe-auge-2025" /> |

Federation costs consistency: action spaces, control rates, cameras and licenses differ, and four robot types supply over 85% of OXE's real data <Cite id="oxe-auge-2025" />. Moritz Reuss's ICLR 2026 review of VLA papers calls OXE mostly low-quality data <Cite id="oxe-quality-critique-2026" />. Re-Mix's learned source weighting beat an even mix by 38% on average <Cite id="remix-2024" />.

Also: RoboNet <Cite id="robonet-2019" />; [VLA models](/manipulation/vla-models) trained on OXE, Octo <Cite id="octo-2024" /> and OpenVLA <Cite id="openvla-2024" />.

## DROID trades robot variety for scene variety

Fifty operators at 13 institutions collected its 76,000 trajectories in 564 scenes, all on one rig: a Franka Panda with three cameras, [teleoperated](/data-hardware/teleop-rigs) through a Meta Quest 2 headset <Cite id="droid-2024" />. <Term id="diffusion-policy">Diffusion policies</Term> trained on DROID beat the next-best dataset by 22% in familiar conditions and 17% outside them <Cite id="droid-2024" />.

Updates added language labels for 75,000 episodes and better camera calibrations for 36,000 <Cite id="droid-project-page" />. DROID's CC BY 4.0 license <Cite id="droid-2024" /> allows commercial use and adaptation with attribution <Cite id="cc-by-4-0-deed" />.

Single-platform collections can be bigger:

| Dataset | Size | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RT-1 | About 130,000 episodes from 13 robots over 17 months | <Cite id="rt1-2022" /> |
| Language-Table | Nearly 600,000 language-labeled trajectories | <Cite id="language-table-2022" /> |
| Galaxea Open-World | 500 hours across 50 scenes, one embodiment | <Cite id="galaxea-open-world-2025" /> |

## BridgeData V2 is built on a cheap arm

BridgeData V2 uses the WidowX 250, a six-degree-of-freedom arm. Its 60,096 trajectories <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-project-page" /> are 50,365 VR-teleoperated demonstrations and 9,731 scripted pick-and-place runs in 24 environments <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-2023" />. With its 2021 predecessor, joint training with 50 demonstrations of a new task in a new setting doubled success on average <Cite id="bridge-data-2021" />.

<Image id="bridgedata-v2-sink-tasks-2023" />

The data is CC BY 4.0 <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-project-page" /> and mostly ships inside OXE <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />; the paper adds goal-conditioned <Term id="behavior-cloning">behavior cloning</Term> baselines <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-2023" />.

## AgiBot World is one company's fleet data

The Beta release holds 1,001,552 trajectories totaling 2,976 hours, all from the AgiBot G1. AgiBot reports 217 tasks and a 30% average gain over OXE pretraining, a vendor figure with no independent replication <Cite id="agibot-world-2025" />. The million trajectories cover less interaction than Ego4D's 3,670 hours of passive video <Cite id="ego4d-2022" />.

The repository now lists 1,003,672 trajectories under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 <Cite id="agibot-world-repo" />, which bars commercial use <Cite id="cc-by-nc-sa-4-0-deed" />. AgiBot World 2026, recorded on the newer G2, lists 17.5 TB under the same license and no episode or hour totals <Cite id="agibot-world-2026" />.

## RoboMIND records four robots under one protocol

RoboMIND reports 107,000 successful trajectories, 305.5 hours real and simulated, across 479 tasks on four robots. It also releases 5,000 real-world failures with annotated causes <Cite id="robomind-2024" />. RoboMIND 2.0 (December 2025) reports over 310,000 dual-arm trajectories from six embodiments, 12,000 with touch sensing <Cite id="robomind-2-2025" />.

Its [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/x-humanoid-robomind/RoboMIND) says Apache-2.0 but gates download behind access conditions <Cite id="robomind-dataset-card" />.

## The largest new releases record people

Community data now trains real models: SmolVLA used fewer than 30,000 community-contributed episodes <Cite id="smolvla-2025" />, and continual pretraining on the community-built AXIS raised π0.5's overall success rate by 5.8% <Cite id="axis-2026" />.

| Release | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| RoboCOIN (2025) | Over 180,000 bimanual demonstrations from 15 platforms and 421 tasks <Cite id="robocoin-2025" /> |
| FineART (Scale AI, Hugging Face; September 2026) | 1,718 hours of bimanual teleoperation in 40,543 episodes, with 533,913 human-labeled subtasks <Cite id="fineart-2026" /> |
| SharpaDex (Sharpa, 2026) | 28,993 teleoperated episodes, about 300 hours, on two touch-sensing dexterous hands; non-commercial license <Cite id="sharpadex-v1-2026" /> |
| RoboCasa365 (2026) | Simulated: 365 tasks in 2,500 kitchens, over 600 hours of human and 1,600 of synthetic demonstrations <Cite id="robocasa365-2026" /> |
| Human-1M-100K (NVIDIA, November 2026) | 100,000 hours of egocentric work video with hand and head tracking from 41,288 collectors; CC BY 4.0 <Cite id="human-1m-100k-2026" /> |
| Open-HumanWAM-50K (2026) | 50,000 annotated hours <Cite id="open-humanwam-2026" /> |
| EgoVerse (2026) | 1,362 hours from 2,087 demonstrators <Cite id="egoverse-2026" /> |
| TacClaw-Infinity (2026) | Over 10,000 hours of handheld recordings with fingertip touch, maker-reported <Cite id="tacclaw-infinity-2026" /> |

RH20T pairs vision, force, audio and actions in over 110,000 contact-rich sequences <Cite id="rh20t-2023" />, and Humanoid Everyday adds depth, LiDAR and touch to 10,300 humanoid trajectories <Cite id="humanoid-everyday-2025" />.

## A usable dataset is legal, consistent and versioned

A dataset must be legal for its use, consistent in actions, units, frames and rates, and downloadable as versioned episodes. LeRobot's format moved to multi-episode files in 2025, after one file per episode hit file-system limits <Cite id="lerobot-dataset-v3-2025" />.

Mixing needs one schema for observations and actions, an adapter per source and source labels kept through training, to tell real transfer from one corpus dominating <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" /> <Cite id="octo-2024" /> <Cite id="remix-2024" />. The [robot-learning stack](/data-hardware/robot-learning-stack) covers the tooling.
