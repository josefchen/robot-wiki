## Open X-Embodiment: breadth by federation

OXE pooled 60 existing datasets from 34 labs into one standardized RLDS format: over a million trajectories spanning 22 robot embodiments, 527 skills and 160,266 tasks <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />. RLDS is a standard, lossless episode format for sharing and reusing datasets <Cite id="rlds-2021" />.

RT-1-X trained on the pool beat each small-scale domain's original method by 50% on average, but underperformed RT-1 trained on a large-scale domain's own data, which the authors attribute to underfitting. The higher-capacity RT-2-X roughly tripled performance on out-of-distribution skills over the same model trained without the pool <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />. Generalist policies train on it ([VLA models](/manipulation/vla-models)): Octo used 800k OXE trajectories <Cite id="octo-2024" />, and OpenVLA used 970k real-world demonstrations <Cite id="openvla-2024" />.

Later <Term id="cross-embodiment">cross-embodiment</Term> pools are larger or broader. ARIO reports about 3 million episodes across 321,064 tasks <Cite id="ario-2024" />, and CrossFormer trained one policy on 900K trajectories from 20 embodiments, including wheeled robots, quadcopters and quadrupeds <Cite id="crossformer-2024" />.

Federation costs consistency: constituents differ in action spaces, control rates, cameras and licenses. Moritz Reuss's review of VLA papers at ICLR 2026 calls OXE mostly low-quality data and notes that <Term id="imitation-learning">imitation learning</Term> still lacks good ways to quantify data quality <Cite id="oxe-quality-critique-2026" />. Re-Mix's learned domain weights beat uniform weighting by 38% on average on the data used to train the RT-X models <Cite id="remix-2024" />.

## DROID: depth on one platform

DROID's distinction is scene and site diversity: 76,000 trajectories totaling 350 hours, across 564 scenes and 86 tasks, gathered by 50 operators at 13 institutions over 12 months <Cite id="droid-2024" />. Every trajectory came from one rig, a Franka Panda with two ZED 2 cameras and a ZED Mini wrist camera, [teleoperated](/data-hardware/teleop-rigs) through a Meta Quest 2. One platform gives one action space and one camera geometry, at the price of embodiment lock-in.

Single-platform collections can be larger. RT-1's fleet of 13 robots gathered about 130k episodes over 17 months <Cite id="rt1-2022" />, and Language-Table released nearly 600,000 language-labelled trajectories <Cite id="language-table-2022" />. The Galaxea Open-World Dataset holds 500 hours across 50 scenes, all from one consistent robot embodiment <Cite id="galaxea-open-world-2025" />.

<Term id="diffusion-policy">Diffusion policies</Term> trained on DROID beat policies trained on the next-best dataset by 22% in-distribution and 17% out-of-distribution <Cite id="droid-2024" />. The project added language annotations for 75,000 episodes in December 2024 and improved camera calibrations for 36,000 in April 2025 <Cite id="droid-project-page" />. The paper releases DROID under CC BY 4.0 <Cite id="droid-2024" />, whose deed allows commercial sharing and adaptation subject to attribution, a license link, change notices and the other license terms, and warns that other rights may limit a particular use <Cite id="cc-by-4-0-deed" />.

## BridgeData V2: the academic workhorse

BridgeData V2 holds 60,096 trajectories on a WidowX 250, a low-cost 6-DoF arm: 50,365 teleoperated demonstrations collected with a VR controller at 5 Hz and 9,731 scripted pick-and-place rollouts, across 24 environments and 13 skills <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-2023" />. Trajectories average 38 timesteps <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-project-page" />. Its 2021 predecessor had 7,200 demonstrations of 71 tasks in 10 environments, and joint training with 50 demonstrations of a new task in a new domain doubled success on average <Cite id="bridge-data-2021" />.

<Image id="bridgedata-v2-sink-tasks-2023" />

BridgeData V2's official project page states CC BY 4.0 for all data <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-project-page" />. The paper shipped baselines from goal-conditioned <Term id="behavior-cloning">behavior cloning</Term> to ACT and RT-1 <Cite id="bridgedata-v2-2023" />, and most of the data also ships inside OXE <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" />.

## AgiBot World: the corporate fleet

AgiBot World's Beta release holds 1,001,552 trajectories totaling 2,976 hours, all from the AgiBot G1 platform with a multi-camera rig. AgiBot reports 217 tasks in five deployment scenarios and a 30% average gain over OXE pretraining for policies pretrained on its data, a vendor figure with no independent replication <Cite id="agibot-world-2025" />. Its repository now lists 1,003,672 trajectories and places all data and code under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 <Cite id="agibot-world-repo" />, whose NonCommercial term bars commercial use <Cite id="cc-by-nc-sa-4-0-deed" />.

At about 11 seconds per trajectory, the million-trajectory headline covers less interaction than Ego4D's 3,670 hours of passive video <Cite id="ego4d-2022" />. AgiBot World 2026 was collected on the AgiBot G2 in real commercial and home spaces, as long-horizon restocking episodes with three annotation layers and a digital-twin simulation counterpart. Its card lists a 13.6 TB total file size but no episode or hour totals <Cite id="agibot-world-2026" />.

## RoboMIND: multi-embodiment under one protocol

RoboMIND's paper reports 107,000 successful trajectories totaling 305.5 interaction hours across 479 tasks and 96 object classes. The cohort mixes real-world and simulated demonstrations for four embodiments, a Franka Emika Panda, a UR5e, an AgileX dual-arm robot and a humanoid with dual dexterous hands, under one collection platform and standardized protocol. It also releases 5,000 real-world failure demonstrations with annotated causes and a digital twin of its tasks in Isaac Sim <Cite id="robomind-2024" />.

RoboMIND 2.0 (December 2025) reports over 310K dual-arm trajectories from six embodiments and 739 tasks, including 12K tactile-enhanced episodes and 20K mobile manipulation trajectories <Cite id="robomind-2-2025" />. The [public dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/x-humanoid-robomind/RoboMIND) shows an Apache-2.0 tag but makes users accept access conditions before downloading, so check the terms before commercial use <Cite id="robomind-dataset-card" />.

## How to read dataset claims

Hours compare better than episodes. DROID averages about 17 seconds per trajectory and AgiBot World Beta about 11, so a large episode count can hide less interaction than a smaller set of long tasks ([the data bottleneck](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck)). The 2025 BEHAVIOR Challenge supplies 10,000 teleoperated demonstrations totaling 1,200+ hours <Cite id="behavior-challenge-2025" />, and the synthetic InternData-A1 holds over 630k trajectories and 7,433 hours <Cite id="interndata-a1-2025" />.

Modalities differ as much as size. RH20T records visual, force, audio and action data in over 110,000 contact-rich sequences <Cite id="rh20t-2023" />. Humanoid Everyday adds depth, LiDAR and tactile streams to 10.3k humanoid trajectories across 260 tasks, with a cloud evaluation platform <Cite id="humanoid-everyday-2025" />. Scaling work finds task diversity worth more than per-task demonstration count, and multi-embodiment pretraining optional given high-quality single-embodiment data <Cite id="diversity-scaling-2025" />.

## Releases since 2025

RoboCOIN offers over 180,000 bimanual demonstrations from 15 platforms and 421 tasks <Cite id="robocoin-2025" />. Scale AI and Hugging Face's FineART (September 2026) has 1,718 hours of bimanual teleoperation in 40,543 episodes, with 533,913 human-labelled subtasks across 151 tasks <Cite id="fineart-2026" />. SmolVLA trained on fewer than 30k episodes, all from community-contributed datasets <Cite id="smolvla-2025" />. The community-built AXIS holds 50K+ trajectories over 207 tasks, and continual pretraining on it raised π0.5's overall success rate by 5.8% <Cite id="axis-2026" />.

In simulation, RoboCasa365 offers 365 tasks across 2,500 kitchens, with over 600 hours of human and over 1,600 hours of synthetic demonstrations <Cite id="robocasa365-2026" />.

## From dataset headline to training contract

A useful dataset is legal for the intended deployment, consistent in actions, units, frames and rates, complete for the claimed tasks, scenes and embodiments, and downloadable as versioned episodes traceable to a release. Datasheets for Datasets asks every dataset to document its motivation, composition, collection process and recommended uses <Cite id="datasheets-for-datasets-2018" />. LeRobot's format moved to multi-episode files in 2025, after one file per episode hit file-system limits at millions of episodes <Cite id="lerobot-dataset-v3-2025" />.

Mixing datasets needs a canonical observation and action schema, one adapter per source and source labels kept through training, so positive transfer can be told apart from one corpus dominating <Cite id="open-x-embodiment-2023" /> <Cite id="octo-2024" /> <Cite id="remix-2024" />. The [robot-learning stack](/data-hardware/robot-learning-stack) supplies the versioned artifacts, and the [robot-learning roadmap](/manipulation/robot-learning-roadmap) places dataset selection in a broader program.
