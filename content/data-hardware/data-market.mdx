An hour of real-robot training data sells for RMB 500 to 1,000 in three reports from China <Cite id="yicai-mifeng-2026" /> <Cite id="jiazi-data-collection-2026" /> <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" />. Eidon’s Sam Padilla reports selling first-person video with arm motion for about \$60 an hour <Cite id="padilla-eidon-data-sales-2026" />. [The Data Bottleneck](/data-hardware/data-bottleneck) explains why demonstrations are scarce. [Teleoperation Rigs](/data-hardware/teleop-rigs) describes the recording hardware.

## China prices real-robot data at RMB 500 to 1,000 an hour

Mifeng, the Beijing humanoid center and Huxiu reported that range in April, June and September respectively <Cite id="yicai-mifeng-2026" /> <Cite id="jiazi-data-collection-2026" /> <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" />. MIIT institute researcher Li Shuai gave the same range for collection cost in November <Cite id="cls-miit-data-cost-2026" />.

| Data | Price per hour | Who stated it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Real robot, sold by the hour or task | RMB 500 to 1,000 | Beijing humanoid center's data lead, reported <Cite id="jiazi-data-collection-2026" /> |
| Real robot | RMB 500 to 1,000 | Mifeng's chief executive, reported <Cite id="yicai-mifeng-2026" /> |
| Real robot | RMB 500 to 1,000 | Huxiu survey, reported <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" /> |
| UMI-style, no robot | RMB 300 to 400 | Huxiu survey, reported <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" /> |
| Simulated, synthetic | RMB 200 to 500 | Huxiu survey, reported <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" /> |
| Custom teleoperation, full service | RMB 800 (\$117, the authors' conversion) | Quote obtained by the PyRUA-DataFarm authors, vendor not named <Cite id="pyrua-datafarm-2026" /> |
| Real robot | RMB 300 to 1,000 or more | Lingyu's chief executive, a reference price, reported <Cite id="cls-data-price-investigation-2026" /> |
| Recorded without a robot | RMB 100 to 300 | Lingyu's chief executive, a reference price, reported <Cite id="cls-data-price-investigation-2026" /> |
| Home recordings resold by intermediaries | RMB 300 to 500 | Tech Xingqiu, reported <Cite id="techxingqiu-data-collectors-2026" /> |
| Raw single-camera video, some sellers | RMB 5 to 10 | An industry veteran quoted under a pseudonym, reported <Cite id="cls-data-price-investigation-2026" /> |

At the European Central Bank's reference rates of 13 November 2026, RMB 500 to 1,000 is \$75 to \$150 <Cite id="ecb-reference-rates-2026-11-13" />.

Huxiu reports that usable time varies with operator skill, task success, scene and robot condition <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" />. Lingyu’s Jin Ge says robot-free recordings often sell three to five times. His team fingerprints real-robot data but cannot trace the rest <Cite id="cls-data-price-investigation-2026" />. In April, Mifeng’s chief executive predicted robot-free data would fetch a third to a half of the real-robot price <Cite id="techxingqiu-data-collectors-2026" />.

## Selling robot data

Eidon sold in 1,000-hour batches. Padilla says the team shared samples early. Some labs wanted joint angles, others the raw inertial readings. A seller who keeps only the angles cannot later deliver raw readings <Cite id="padilla-eidon-data-sales-2026" />.

## What an accepted hour costs

In Michelle Sun’s example, an operator paid \$30 an hour records for 60% of a shift. If 80% passes review, each staffed hour yields 0.48 accepted hours. Labor alone costs \$62.50 per accepted hour <Cite id="sun-robot-data-economics-2026" />.

## China's center count depends on who counts

Interact Analysis tracks state-backed and large company centers <Cite id="interact-analysis-centers-2026" />, while Huxiu counts publicly disclosed centers with fixed sites <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" />, so the counts cannot be added together.

| Counter | As of | Count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Standard | End of 2025 | At least 30 facilities <Cite id="standard-umi-collectors-2026" /> |
| Interact Analysis, via Rest of World | December 2025 | Over 40 state-owned centers announced, about two dozen operating <Cite id="restofworld-china-data-centers-2026" /> |
| Interact Analysis | End of April 2026 | At least 90 operating or planned, at least 64 of them operating <Cite id="interact-analysis-centers-2026" /> |
| STAR Market Daily | June 2026 | Over 70 open, over 40 more being built or planned <Cite id="cls-data-price-investigation-2026" /> |
| Huxiu | August 2026 | 106 disclosed, 84 operating <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" /> |

The Beijing humanoid center's base runs over 120 robots, has capacity for over 100,000 hours a year and sells mainly to leading robot makers and model companies <Cite id="jiazi-data-collection-2026" />. Huxiu found that the 84 operating centers had yet to reach a stable business model. From the 52 centers that disclose about 3,972 robots, it estimates over 5,000 in all; 65.96% of centers use a single brand, whose data formats do not carry over to other robots <Cite id="huxiu-collection-centers-2026" />.

The model is disputed. Mech-Mind founder Shao Tianlan said in September that some companies book false, unsustainable revenue through data centers and related-party deals. Lingyu's Jin Ge argues that the arrangement resembles contract farming and holds up when the data trains real models <Cite id="cls-data-price-investigation-2026" />. Li Shuai put the stock of effective data at about one million hours, against the 100 million that leading makers say they need <Cite id="cls-miit-data-cost-2026" />.

South Korea opened its first humanoid data factory, with 12 humanoids at KIMM in Daejeon, on 3 November 2026 <Cite id="sedaily-kimm-data-factory-2026" />.

## Open data: the license decides commercial use
