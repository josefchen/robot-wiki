Industrial deployment puts robots into factories and warehouses, where each installation must repay its cost. The installed base reached a record 5 million industrial robots in 2025 <Cite id="ifr-world-robotics-2026-release" />.

## A cell pays back when its labor covers its capital

A robot cell is the robot plus everything built around it. The calculator divides cell capital by the monthly value of the labor it replaces, leaving out the damage and throughput savings in EVST's full formula <Cite id="evst-cell-cost-2026" />.

<JamOverhead className="my-6" />

Every input is an assumption. The model amortizes 730-hour months over 60 months and judges payback against 24 months, the top of EVST's multi-shift range <Cite id="evst-cell-cost-2026" />.

## Clearing time decides payback

A failure costs what it takes to clear. With 15-second clearing, dropping per-pick success from 99.9 to 99 percent stretches payback 2.2 percent, from 11.56 to 11.82 months. With 300-second clearing the same drop moves payback from 12.11 to 17.30 months and cuts output 30 percent, to 380.0 picks an hour.

The <Term id="intervention-rate">intervention rate</Term>, how often a person steps in, and the cost of each one decide whether a cell ships. Training on deployment data with expert corrections more than doubled Physical Intelligence's throughput on some of its hardest tasks <Cite id="pistar06-2025" />. Ken Goldberg would let robots do useful work while they collect the data to improve <Cite id="goldberg-data-gap-2025" />.

Investors now want return on investment, not demos <Cite id="morgan-stanley-pr-problem-2026" />. Figure's livestreamed logistics run lasted 200 hours <Cite id="helix-2-5-2026" />, at one task and one site, with [no published success rate](/data-hardware/evaluation-crisis). [The Reliability Gap](/frontier/reliability-gap) tracks that distance; [Autonomous Vehicles](/adjacent/autonomous-vehicles) asks the same of driverless cars.

## Pace, uptime and payback decide a deployment

| Metric | What it measures |
| --- | --- |
| <Term id="cycle-time">Cycle time</Term> | Time one operation takes <Cite id="lei-cycle-time-definition" /> |
| <Term id="takt-time">Takt time</Term> | Available production time divided by customer demand <Cite id="lei-takt-time-definition" /> |
| Inherent availability | Uptime from <Term id="mean-time-between-failures">mean time between failures</Term> and repair time alone <Cite id="nasa-availability-prediction-analysis" /> |
| Operational availability | Adds logistics, administrative and preventive-maintenance time <Cite id="nasa-availability-prediction-analysis" /> |
| <Term id="payback-period">Payback period</Term> | System capital cost over monthly labor, damage and throughput value <Cite id="evst-cell-cost-2026" /> |

EVST estimates payback at 12 to 24 months for multi-shift palletizing cells and 24 to 36 months for single-shift ones <Cite id="evst-cell-cost-2026" />.

## The arm is a third to half of the cell

EVST puts the robot at a third to half of total cell cost, so a complete palletizing cell runs two to three times the arm's price. The rest is tooling, guarding, safety controllers, integration, commissioning, programming, software and vision <Cite id="evst-cell-cost-2026" />.

<Image id="kuka-bread-palletizing-bakery-2005" />

The integrator owns the risk assessment. Under ANSI/RIA R15.06-2012, the <Term id="systems-integrator">systems integrator</Term> completes and documents it before commissioning and hands it to the employer, who stays responsible for a safe workplace <Cite id="osha-otm-robots" />.

Bessemer expects near-term value to go to full-stack companies, because deployment needs site data, local fine-tuning and hardware integration <span className="max-sm:[&_[role=tooltip]]:-translate-x-8"><Cite id="bessemer-robotics-2026" /></span>. IndustReal moved simulated insertion policies onto real hardware <Cite id="industreal-2023" />.

The operational stock grew 9 percent in 2025. China installed 354,000 units, and the United States, at almost 38,500, passed Japan for second place <Cite id="ifr-world-robotics-2026-release" />. In 2024 the counts were 4,663,698 and 542,076, with 54 percent in China <Cite id="ifr-world-robotics-2025" /> <Cite id="ifr-china-five-year-plan-2026" />.

Most of these robots weld, paint, load machines, assemble and pack in fixed cells with little perception <span className="max-sm:[&_[role=tooltip]]:-translate-x-8"><Cite id="osha-otm-robots" /></span>. Collaborative robots were 19.6 percent of the 36,766 robots ordered in North America in 2025, and 10.7 percent of the \$2.25 billion order value <Cite id="a3-orders-2025" />.

## Logistics is where learned systems enter

<Term id="automated-storage-and-retrieval">Automated storage and retrieval</Term> replaces aisles and forklifts with high-bay racks and shuttles; AutoStore reports about 2,000 systems in 68 countries <Cite id="autostore-q2-2026" />. <Term id="autonomous-mobile-robot">Autonomous mobile robots</Term> move cases and totes, and transport robots were 117,500 of the almost 250,000 professional service robots sold in 2025 <Cite id="ifr-service-robots-2026" />. <Term id="goods-to-person">Goods-to-person</Term> stations bring the shelf to a stationary worker and set the bar automated picking must beat.

Only the stow paper reports failures: 9.31 percent unproductive, 4.83 percent defects <Cite id="amazon-stow-2025" />. Vulcan's post gives no success rate <Cite id="amazon-vulcan-2026" />.

Grid contracts are large and can unwind. Symbotic reported about \$22.5 billion of backlog against \$2.247 billion of FY2025 revenue, anchored by all 42 Walmart regional distribution centers <span className="max-sm:[&_[role=tooltip]]:-translate-x-8"><Cite id="symbotic-10k-2025" /></span>. In October 2026 the two expanded the deal to 25 US regional centers, with an initial commitment of about \$6 billion <Cite id="symbotic-walmart-2026" />.

Kroger is closing three automated fulfillment centers and expects about \$2.6 billion of impairment and related charges <Cite id="kroger-8k-ecommerce-2025" />; five sites remain <Cite id="kroger-ocado-closures-2025" />. Ocado takes a one-off \$350 million payment and about \$50 million less FY26 fee revenue <Cite id="ocado-kroger-rns-2025" />. It now sells the same grid warehouses into healthcare, third-party logistics and industrial parts <Cite id="ocado-oia-2026" />.

## Humanoids arrive in small numbers

IFR counts about 7,000 [full-size humanoids](/data-hardware/hardware-taxonomy) sold in 2025, mostly in specialized jobs and often teleoperated <Cite id="ifr-service-robots-2026" />. SAG estimates 16,500 shipped in the third quarter of 2026, up 347% on a year earlier, led by AgiBot, UBTECH and Unitree <Cite id="sag-humanoid-shipments-q3-2026" />. UBTECH's own filing reports 921 sold in the first half of 2026, for RMB 590.3 million <Cite id="ubtech-interim-2026" />.

Every deployment figure is self-reported:

| Robot and site | What the company reports |
| --- | --- |
| Agility Digit 4 | 65,000+ operational hours; 100,000 totes at GXO Flowery Branch, about 98% accurate while on-task <Cite id="agility-digit-5-2026" /> |
| Agility Digit, GXO at SPANX | Robots-as-a-service from June 2024, moving totes from robots to conveyors <Cite id="agility-gxo-raas-2024" /> |
| Figure, BMW (11 months) | Targets: 84-second cycle, 99%+ success per shift, zero interventions. Reported: 1,250+ hours and 90,000+ parts, no achieved rates <Cite id="figure-bmw-production-2025" /> |
| Boston Dynamics Atlas, Hyundai | Training to sequence parts; component assembly expected by 2030 <Cite id="bd-rmac-2026" /> |
| Tesla Optimus, Fremont | On high-volume lines, October 2026 <Cite id="tesla-q3-2026-update" /> |

The business is still small. Agility listed on Nasdaq on November 5, 2026, with more than \$300 million in committed Digit 5 orders <Cite id="agility-nasdaq-listing-2026" />, against 2025 net sales of \$1.78 million <Cite id="agility-s4a-2026" />. Unitree's first-quarter 2026 revenue growth slowed to 68.49%, from 332.64%, and adjusted net profit fell 52.55% <Cite id="unitree-prospectus-2026" />.

Safety rules shape the machine. Insurers and regulators kept Digit 4 behind barriers, so Digit 5 sets down its load, sits and cuts motor power before a person can touch it. The added bulk means it can no longer run or jump <Cite id="hurst-spectrum-2026" />.

Also: Walker S2 three-minute battery swap <Cite id="ubtech-walker-s2-2026" />, AgiBot A2 quick battery change <Cite id="agibot-a2-2026" />.

## Old buildings must fit the robots in

A greenfield site is designed around its automation. Amazon built Shreveport with 10 times more robotics, and its Sequoia holds more than 30 million items <Cite id="amazon-shreveport-2024" />. A <Term id="brownfield-deployment">brownfield deployment</Term> goes into a running building; Symbotic says it can install in phases while the warehouse keeps operating <Cite id="symbotic-10k-2025" />.

Robots that work apart from people need fences, interlocked guards and presence sensors, and lockout/tagout for maintenance <Cite id="osha-otm-robots" />. [Safety and Assurance](/frontier/safety-and-assurance) covers the rest.

## Robots cost jobs in some economies and add them in others

| Country | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| United States | One more robot per 1,000 workers cuts the employment-to-population ratio 0.2 points and wages 0.42% <Cite id="acemoglu-restrepo-2020" /> |
| Germany | Manufacturing losses fully offset by service jobs; young entrants bear the cost <Cite id="dauth-robots-germany-2021" /> |
| Spain | Adopters raised output 20 to 25% within four years and created net jobs at 10% <Cite id="koch-robots-firms-2021" /> |
| France | Adopters grew jobs at competitors' expense; industry employment fell <Cite id="acemoglu-lelarge-restrepo-2020" /> |

MIT's Work of the Future task force found no compelling evidence of a jobless future in 2020: automation displaces some tasks and creates new work <Cite id="mit-work-future-2020" />.
