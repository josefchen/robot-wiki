Since 2019, <Term id="reinforcement-learning">reinforcement learning</Term> (RL) in simulation has been the standard way to teach legged robots to walk <Cite id="hwangbo-2019" />, while most robot hands still learn from human demonstrations. The difference is contact: feet on the ground are cheap to simulate, and fingers working an object at millimeter scale are not <Cite id="reality-gap-survey-2026" />.

## Walking is cheap to simulate

In 2021 an ANYmal trained with <Term id="ppo">PPO</Term> and a <Term id="curriculum-learning">curriculum</Term> of harder terrain learned to walk on flat ground in under four minutes on one GPU. Its policy needed only <Term id="proprioception">proprioception</Term>, 108 terrain-height samples and a learned actuator model <Cite id="rudin-2021" />.

With <Term id="domain-randomization">randomized physics</Term> the recipe spread. Boston Dynamics uses RL where conditions are easy to simulate but good strategies are hard to describe <Cite id="bd-spot-rl-2024" />.

| Work | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Lee et al. | Natural terrain <Cite id="lee-2020" /> |
| RMA | An A1 with no fine-tuning <Cite id="rma-2021" /> |
| Radosavovic et al. | A real humanoid, zero-shot <Cite id="radosavovic-2023" /> |
| FastSAC (2025) | Humanoid walking in 15 minutes on one gaming GPU <Cite id="seo-2025" /> |

Manipulation is the reverse: simplified contact models break down in contact-rich tasks <Cite id="reality-gap-survey-2026" />. So manipulation learns from real [demonstrations](/manipulation/bc-foundations), which need no contact model. Diffusion Policy beat prior methods by 46.9% across 12 tasks <Cite id="diffusion-policy-2023" />, and ACT slotted a battery at 80 to 90% success from 10 minutes of demonstrations <Cite id="act-aloha-2023" />.

## Contact geometry is where the gap lives

A gait recovers from a wrong contact force on the next step; a jammed peg cannot. Three simulators tested missed elastic impacts <Cite id="acosta-2021" />, and rigid-body contact models hit a task-specific accuracy ceiling <Cite id="fazeli-2017" />. Factory simulates 1,000 nut-and-bolt interactions at once and still calls assembly contact a longstanding challenge <Cite id="factory-2022" />.

Also: errors that compound over finger contact <Cite id="regrind-2026" />, touch sensing as the bottleneck <Cite id="hydroshear-2026" />.

## Robots built to be simulated

Boston Dynamics builds Atlas from rotary actuators "much easier to represent well in simulation" <Cite id="bd-atlas-hard-work-2026" />. Its 13-degree-of-freedom hand uses rigid backdrivable drives so it can be simulated accurately <Cite id="bd-atlas-hand-2026" />. NVIDIA's SimReady standard specifies each asset's physical properties, such as mass and friction <Cite id="nvidia-simready-2026" />.

## Most manipulation still learns from people

Sim-to-real dexterous RL is still mostly single-hand or state-based <Cite id="lin-humanoid-sim2real-2025" />. A 2026 survey of 113 dexterous methods found almost half distill simulated RL into a vision-only student, but learning from demonstrations is growing faster <Cite id="dexterous-learning-survey-2026" />.

Also: π0.5 cleaning new homes <Cite id="pi05-2025" />, multi-task pretraining at TRI <Cite id="tri-lbm-2025" />, real-robot RL from demonstrations <Cite id="rl-100-2025" />, Gemini Robotics 2 multi-finger control <Cite id="gemini-robotics-2-2026" />.
