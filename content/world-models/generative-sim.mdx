Generative simulation is the use of generative models to author a simulator's tasks, scenes, assets and rewards while a physics engine, itself an unlearned <Term id="world-model">world model</Term>, keeps the dynamics <Cite id="robogen-2024" />. A 2026 survey finds the field shifting "from visual realism toward interaction readiness" <Cite id="3d-gen-embodied-survey-2026" />.

<Image id="robocasa-object-library-2024" />

A rendered scene has no dynamics, and realism does not supply them: on Physics-IQ, physical understanding in video generators was "unrelated to visual realism" <Cite id="physics-iq-2025" />. Try it: push the mug as a picture only, then as a picture plus physics.

<span id="generate-content-inside-a-physics-engine" />
## A physics engine keeps generated content physically consistent

[Generative video](/world-models/generative-video/) learns physics from pixels, and a plausible future can ignore the pending action; the 2026 world-model survey calls visual plausibility a weak proxy for control utility <Cite id="world-model-survey-2026" />. Generative simulation moves the model off that path: a solver executes the generated task, layout, mesh or reward, so a mug with the wrong friction still cannot fall upward.

<span id="robogen-propose-generate-learn" />
## RoboGen automates task proposal, but humans still inspect the results

RoboGen runs a propose-generate-learn cycle: GPT-4 proposes tasks from a robot type and a sampled object, generates scene configurations, decomposes each task into sub-tasks, picks a learning method and supplies rewards or target-shape supervision <Cite id="robogen-2024" />.

Locomotion, presented as an RL task, uses cross-entropy-method planning with the ground-truth simulator. Manipulation uses SAC with motion-planned primitives. Prompt design, in-context examples and human inspection of scenes, rewards, and learned skills remain. The authors name large-scale skill verification and sim-to-real transfer as open <Cite id="robogen-2024" />.

GenSim used GPT-4 to expand a benchmark tenfold to over 100 tasks, and pretrained policies beat baselines by 25% on unseen real long-horizon tasks <Cite id="gensim-2023" />. RoboTwin 2.0 repairs generated task code with simulation in the loop; a VLA trained on its data plus 10 real demonstrations improved 367% over the 10-demo baseline <Cite id="robotwin-2-2025" />.

<span id="holodeck-language-guided-environment-generation" />
## Holodeck builds rooms from prompts but does not test manipulation

Holodeck (CVPR 2024) uses GPT-4 to build AI2-THOR environments from text prompts. It selects Objaverse objects and proposes soft relational constraints, and a depth-first-search solver enforces collision and room boundaries while satisfying as many soft constraints as possible <Cite id="holodeck-2024" />.

Holodeck's evaluation, a navigation model pretrained on ProcTHOR-10K, fine-tuned in generated scenes and tested in artist-designed NoveltyTHOR rooms, does not test physically valid manipulation. The authors report trouble with complex layouts and with assets missing from Objaverse <Cite id="holodeck-2024" />.

Objaverse supplies 800K+ models without physical properties <Cite id="objaverse-2022" />. SceneSmith (ICML 2026) adds text-to-3D assets, articulated retrieval and physical-property estimation, with 3 to 6 times more objects than prior methods, under 2% collisions and 96% of objects stable <Cite id="scenesmith-2026" />.

<span id="robocasa" />
## More generated demonstrations raised RoboCasa success from 26.3% to 47.6%

RoboCasa's 120 kitchen scenes combine 10 floor plans with 12 styles from architectural references. It holds 2,509 objects and 153 categories, plus 100 tasks: 25 atomic tasks and 75 composite tasks; GPT-4 and Gemini 1.5 drafted the composite blueprints, with human filtering, editing and code. Objects come from Objaverse and Luma.ai text-to-3D, textures from MidJourney, and MimicGen adapts human demonstrations and keeps successful trajectories <Cite id="robocasa-2024" />. MimicGen itself turned about 200 human demonstrations into over 50K across 18 tasks <Cite id="mimicgen-2023" />.

<Image id="robocasa-kitchens-2024" />

The scaling study trains a language-conditioned BC-Transformer for a Franka Panda on an Omron mobile base on 24 atomic tasks, excluding navigation, with 100, 300, or 3,000 generated demonstrations per task using Objaverse objects and AI-generated textures. Tested with curated textures, unseen objects and 50 trials per task in five kitchens, two in unseen styles, success is 26.3%, 35.0%, and 47.6% respectively. Individual tasks do not improve monotonically <Cite id="robocasa-2024" />. RoboCasa365 extends the platform to 365 tasks in 2,500 kitchens, with 600+ hours of human and 1,600+ hours of synthetic demonstrations for multi-task, foundation-model and lifelong-learning benchmarks <Cite id="robocasa365-2026" />.

## Generators expand a few demonstrations or one image into training data

Isaac Lab Mimic splits human demonstrations into object-centric subtasks, transforms them and recombines them for new poses, from as little as one demonstration, across parallel environments. Results depend on SkillGen's trial counts, environment counts and planner settings, trading speed against success and motion quality <Cite id="isaac-lab-2025" />. SkillGen turned 60 human demonstrations into over 24K, and its agents were 24% more successful on average <Cite id="skillmimicgen-2024" />. NVIDIA generated 780,000 simulated trajectories for GR00T N1, equal to 6,500 hours of human demonstration, in 11 hours <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" />.

GRS starts from one real RGB-D image. SAM2 segments it, depth and a calibrated robot-frame transform give bounding boxes, and a vision-language model matches objects to supplied simulation-ready assets. A router revises generated task code and tests until an oracle policy completes the task. Experiments cap repair attempts, exclude runtime-error cases from the reported reward average and document failures that bypass intended behavior, and sim-to-real transfer is future work <Cite id="grs-2024" />.

<span id="the-push-test" />
## Generative simulation borrows its physics from the engine

The push figure separates three layers. Appearance renders the mug, as a reconstruction, texture or video frame might; a physics proxy adds collision geometry, mass and friction; and the simulator integrates the push, sliding the mug by $d = v^2/(2\mu g)$ every time. Cosmos-Transfer1 generates the first layer from segmentation, depth and edge inputs <Cite id="cosmos-transfer1-2025" />, and in the [taxonomy's](/world-models/taxonomy/) verdict on Gaussian Splatting twins, a real-time radiance-field renderer supplies only layer one <Cite id="3dgs-2023" />. Generative video tries to learn layer three, and generative simulation borrows layers two and three from an engine.

<span id="limitations" />
## Generated content still needs physical and human checks

1. Scenes are only as physically correct as their asset parameters: a text-to-3D mug's mass, friction and center of mass are estimates, and generated assets "often overlook rich and important physical properties" <Cite id="physx-3d-2025" />. Calibration is partly automated: Scalable Real2Sim identifies collision geometry and inertial parameters from joint-torque sensors and a camera <Cite id="scalable-real2sim-2025" />, and DrEureka constructs domain-randomization distributions <Cite id="dreureka-2024" />. Articulate-Anything raised articulation success to 75%, from 8.7% to 12.2% for prior work <Cite id="articulate-anything-2024" />. The 3D-generation survey still names "the gap between geometric quality and physical validity" a main bottleneck <Cite id="3d-gen-embodied-survey-2026" />.
2. Task fitness may miss human intent. Eureka notes that its task metric may not match the behavior a person wants, and its authors call a generated PushBlock reward "amenable to post-hoc human inspection and editing" without requiring such inspection or comparing reward hacking <Cite id="eureka-2024" />. Text2Reward's generated rewards matched or beat expert rewards on 13 of 17 manipulation tasks <Cite id="text2reward-2023" />. See [reward design](/rl-sim2real/reward-design-mpc/).
3. Scene diversity has specific design sources. Holodeck's authors document cultural biases from the LLM and asset retrieval, with only qualitative evidence that new prompts help <Cite id="holodeck-2024" />. RoboCasa's authors drew floor plans and styles from home-design and architecture magazines <Cite id="robocasa-2024" />.

<span id="where-this-leaves-the-field" />
## Policies trained on generated variations now transfer to real robots

Learned world models hold narrow roles in [evaluation](/world-models/evaluation/) and data generation ([generative video](/world-models/generative-video/)), while generative models author tasks, scenes, assets and supervision for physics engines <Cite id="robogen-2024" /> <Cite id="holodeck-2024" /> <Cite id="robocasa-2024" />. Policies trained on digital cousins reached 90% zero-shot sim-to-real success against 25% for exact digital twins <Cite id="digital-cousins-2024" />. In 2026, SimFoundry's object, scene and task cousins raised zero-shot real success by 17%, 21% and 40% <Cite id="simfoundry-2026" />.

Liqian Ma, Danfei Xu and colleagues at Georgia Tech (October 2026) train a rater network by meta-gradients to learn which simulator settings produce useful co-training data for a real task <Cite id="metagensim-2026" />. With 10 real demonstrations per task and Diffusion Policy on a Franka Panda, MetaGenSim data gave 58 full-task successes in 80 real trials over four tasks, against 23 for uniformly sampled simulation data and 26 for real data alone <Cite id="metagensim-2026" />. Data from settings an expert aligned by hand gave 57 <Cite id="metagensim-2026" />.

<span id="the-hybrid-boundary" />
## Generators propose content and the engine integrates contact

A generator proposes layouts, tasks, textures and initial states; an asset pipeline checks for interpenetrating parts and attaches physical properties, as Infinigen-Articulated does for 18 articulated categories <Cite id="infinigen-articulated-2025" />. Generated 3D worlds follow the same split: World Labs' Marble exports worlds as Gaussian splats and as meshes, including low-fidelity collider meshes for coarse physics simulation <Cite id="marble-2025" />. The engine integrates contacts, a supervisor labels outcomes, and the dataset records which component produced every field. Engines now model more of the contact: Newton 1.0 adds SDF-based collision and hydroelastic contact for tight-tolerance assembly such as connector insertion <Cite id="newton-manipulation-blog-2026" />.

Check asset scale and collision geometry before dynamics, static stability before rollouts and contact parameters against real measurements before claiming transfer. Steerable scene generation enforces feasibility "via projection and simulation" and releases over 44 million scenes <Cite id="steerable-scene-generation-2025" />. Keep failed scenes as adversarial tests; the [simulator comparison](/world-models/world-models-vs-simulators/) gives the selection rule.
