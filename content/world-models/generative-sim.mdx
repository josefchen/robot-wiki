Generative simulation uses AI models to write a simulator's tasks, scenes, objects and rewards, while a physics engine, itself a hand-built <Term id="world-model">world model</Term>, still computes how things move <Cite id="robogen-2024" />. A 2026 survey sees the field moving "from visual realism toward interaction readiness" <Cite id="3d-gen-embodied-survey-2026" />.

<Image id="robocasa-object-library-2024" />

Realism does not supply physics: on Physics-IQ, physical understanding in video generators was "unrelated to visual realism" <Cite id="physics-iq-2025" />.

<AppearancePhysicsPush className="my-6" />

<span id="generate-content-inside-a-physics-engine" />
## A physics engine keeps generated content physically consistent

A solver executes the generated task, layout, object or reward, so a mug with the wrong friction may slide too far but cannot fall upward. [Generative video](/world-models/generative-video/) learns physics from pixels instead, and a 2026 survey calls visual plausibility a weak proxy for control <Cite id="world-model-survey-2026" />. ManiSkill3 runs physics and rendering in parallel on the GPU <Cite id="maniskill3-2024" />, and Isaac Lab 3.0 runs one task interface across several physics back ends <Cite id="isaac-lab-3-0-2026" />.

<span id="robogen-propose-generate-learn" />
## RoboGen automates task proposal, but humans still inspect the results

Given a robot and a sampled object, GPT-4 in RoboGen proposes a task, writes the scene, splits the task into sub-tasks and supplies rewards. People still design the prompts and inspect the results <Cite id="robogen-2024" />.

| System | Method | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GenSim | GPT-4 grows a benchmark tenfold, to 100+ tasks | Beat baselines by 25% on unseen real long-horizon tasks <Cite id="gensim-2023" /> |
| RoboTwin 2.0 | Repairs task code with the simulator in the loop | 367% better than 10 real demonstrations alone <Cite id="robotwin-2-2025" /> |
| Code2Worlds-2 (Oct 2026) | A critic checks penetration, stability, forces and joint limits | Scenes exported to MuJoCo <Cite id="code2worlds-2-2026" /> |

<span id="holodeck-language-guided-environment-generation" />
## Holodeck builds rooms from prompts but does not test manipulation

Holodeck turns a text prompt into an AI2-THOR room of Objaverse objects, placed by a solver that respects collisions and room bounds. Its tests used navigation only, so it says nothing about valid manipulation <Cite id="holodeck-2024" />. ProcTHOR generates interactive houses for navigation, rearrangement and arm manipulation <Cite id="procthor-2022" />.

Objects are the weak link. Objaverse has over 800,000 3D models with no physical properties <Cite id="objaverse-2022" />. SceneSmith estimates those properties and places 3 to 6 times more objects than prior methods, under 2% colliding and 96% stable <Cite id="scenesmith-2026" />.

<span id="robocasa" />
## More generated demonstrations raised RoboCasa success from 26.3% to 47.6%

RoboCasa's 120 simulated kitchens hold 2,509 objects, and GPT-4 and Gemini 1.5 drafted 75 of its 100 tasks before people edited them. MimicGen multiplies human demonstrations by adapting them to new scenes and keeping successes <Cite id="robocasa-2024" />; alone it turned about 200 demonstrations into over 50,000 <Cite id="mimicgen-2023" />.

<Image id="robocasa-kitchens-2024" />

With 100, 300 or 3,000 generated demonstrations per task, a Franka Panda policy on 24 basic tasks succeeded 26.3%, 35.0% and 47.6% of the time on unseen objects <Cite id="robocasa-2024" />. RoboCasa365 scales to 365 tasks in 2,500 kitchens, with 600+ hours of human and 1,600 hours of synthetic demonstrations <Cite id="robocasa365-2026" />.

## Generators expand a few demonstrations or one image into training data

| System | Starts from | Produces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Isaac Lab Mimic | As little as one demonstration | Subtask segments recombined for new object poses <Cite id="isaac-lab-2025" /> |
| SkillGen | 60 human demonstrations | Over 24,000, with agents 24% more successful on average <Cite id="skillmimicgen-2024" /> |
| NVIDIA, for GR00T N1 | Human demonstrations | 780,000 simulated trajectories, equal to 6,500 hours of human demonstration, in 11 hours <Cite id="gr00t-n1-2025" /> |
| GRS | One real color-and-depth image | A matched simulation scene, with task code revised until a scripted policy succeeds <Cite id="grs-2024" /> |
| AutoRSR (Oct 2026) | A scanned real workspace | 1,000 episodes per task; 73.3% to 86.7% real success on three tasks, comparable to π0.5 on 150 teleoperated demonstrations <Cite id="autorsr-2026" /> |

<span id="the-push-test" />
## Generative simulation borrows its physics from the engine

A simulated push has three layers: appearance, a physics proxy of shape, mass and friction, and the integrator that always slides the mug $d = v^2/(2\mu g)$. Cosmos-Transfer1 generates only appearance, from segmentation, depth and edge maps <Cite id="cosmos-transfer1-2025" />, and so does a [Gaussian Splatting twin](/world-models/taxonomy/) <Cite id="3dgs-2023" />. Generative video tries to learn the third layer; generative simulation borrows the second and third from an engine.

RoboRender restyles simulated video while keeping geometry, robot motion and action labels <Cite id="roborender-2026" />. NVIDIA's November 2026 Cosmos framework renders from an external simulator's physical state, so the engine keeps the dynamics <Cite id="cosmos-interactive-sim-2026" />.

<span id="limitations" />
## Generated content still needs physical and human checks

A scene is only as physical as its objects. Generated assets "often overlook rich and important physical properties" <Cite id="physx-3d-2025" />, and generated geometry can look right while intersecting itself or sitting at the wrong scale <Cite id="worldlabs-taxonomy-2026" />.

| System | Automates | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scalable Real2Sim | Collision geometry and inertia | Identified from joint-torque sensors and a camera <Cite id="scalable-real2sim-2025" /> |
| DrEureka | Ranges for <Term id="domain-randomization">domain randomization</Term> | Built automatically <Cite id="dreureka-2024" /> |
| Articulate-Anything | Objects with moving parts | 75% success, against 8.7% to 12.2% for prior work <Cite id="articulate-anything-2024" /> |

Generated goals can miss what a person wants. Eureka's task metric may not match the intended behavior, and its authors do not require human inspection of generated rewards <Cite id="eureka-2024" />. Text2Reward's rewards matched or beat expert rewards on 13 of 17 manipulation tasks <Cite id="text2reward-2023" />; see [reward design](/rl-sim2real/reward-design-mpc/).

<span id="where-this-leaves-the-field" />
## Policies trained on generated variations now transfer to real robots

Variety beats exactness. Policies trained on digital cousins, similar but not identical scenes, reached 90% zero-shot sim-to-real success against 25% for exact digital twins <Cite id="digital-cousins-2024" />. SimFoundry's object, scene and task cousins raised zero-shot real success by 17%, 21% and 40% <Cite id="simfoundry-2026" />.

MetaGenSim learns which simulator settings make useful co-training data. With 10 real demonstrations per task, its data gave 58 successes in 80 real trials <Cite id="metagensim-2026" />. Real data alone gave 26, uniform simulation 23 and hand-tuned settings 57.

<span id="the-hybrid-boundary" />
## Generators propose content and the engine integrates contact

A generator proposes layouts, tasks and starting states, an asset pipeline attaches physical properties, and the engine integrates contacts. Infinigen-Articulated checks interpenetration for 18 categories of objects with moving parts <Cite id="infinigen-articulated-2025" />, and Newton 1.0 adds hydroelastic contact for tight assembly such as connector insertion <Cite id="newton-manipulation-blog-2026" />.

World Labs follows the same split. Marble exports worlds as Gaussian splats plus low-fidelity collision meshes <Cite id="marble-2025" />. Atlas turns images, camera poses and depth into splats or point clouds <Cite id="worldlabs-atlas-2026" />, and its November 12, 2026 release converts splats to meshes for simulation <Cite id="worldlabs-atlas-release-2026" />.

Check scale and collision geometry before dynamics, stability before rollouts, and contact against real measurements before claiming transfer. Steerable scene generation enforces feasibility "via projection and simulation" across 44 million released scenes <Cite id="steerable-scene-generation-2025" />. The [simulator comparison](/world-models/world-models-vs-simulators/) gives the selection rule.
