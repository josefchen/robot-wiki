A <Term id="world-model">world model</Term> is a model that predicts how an environment evolves in a form useful for a robot's decisions. Hou and colleagues' 2026 survey applies this functional definition to visual, <Term id="latent-dynamics">latent</Term>, structured and symbolic predictions. <span className="block" data-taxonomy-source-placement="line-start"><Cite id="world-model-survey-2026" /></span>

<span id="the-functional-cut" />
## A model counts only if it predicts the effect of actions

Hou and colleagues count a model only if it predicts how the future changes under robot-relevant actions and supports embodied decision-making. In their words, "a model does not qualify as a world model in our sense simply because it generates plausible future images or videos". The survey names weak action conditioning a technical bottleneck, since it allows futures with no causal tie to the pending action <Cite id="world-model-survey-2026" />. A second 2026 survey likewise defines a world model as "an action-conditioned predictive system" <Cite id="manip-wm-survey-2026" />.

## Six example groups

<WmTaxonomyTable className="my-6 wide-measure" />

### Latent dynamics: predict a compact state, dream in it

DreamerV3 uses a recurrent state-space model with stochastic latents and reconstructs inputs; its actor and critic learn from imagined trajectories, and the critic also receives a loss on replay-buffer trajectories. It ran more than 150 tasks in eight domains with fixed hyperparameters <Cite id="dreamerv3-2023" />. Hafner and colleagues' 2025 Nature article calls it, to their knowledge, the first algorithm to collect Minecraft diamonds from scratch without human data or adaptive curricula <Cite id="dreamerv3-nature-2025" />, in an environment with abstract crafting actions, accelerated block breaking and sparse intermediate rewards <Cite id="dreamerv3-2023" />. See the [latent-dynamics explanation](/world-models/latent-dynamics/).

Dreamer 4 draws most knowledge from unlabeled video, needing little action-labeled data <Cite id="dreamer4-2025" />. DayDreamer taught a Unitree A1 to roll off its back, stand and walk in about one hour of real training with no simulator or reset policy, though a PD controller and manual repositioning remained <Cite id="daydreamer-2022" />.

### Decoder-free latent: no image, just value

TD-MPC2 learns joint-embedding, reward and value predictions without reconstructing observations, and its MPPI planner scores sampled action sequences by latent rollouts plus a terminal value. Its 317M-parameter model trained offline on 545M transitions from 240 single-task agents, a run distinct from the 104-task online benchmark <Cite id="tdmpc2-2023" />.

### Generative video: predict the pixels

These models predict future pixels from frames plus an action or text condition; see [generative video](/world-models/generative-video/). NVIDIA presents Cosmos 3 as one Mixture-of-Transformers for understanding, generation, policy and forward and inverse dynamics <Cite id="cosmos-3-2026" />. DeepMind reports that Genie 3 renders text-prompted worlds navigable in real time at 24 fps and 720p, largely consistent for a few minutes, with constrained agent actions among its limits <span className="block" data-taxonomy-source-placement="line-start"><Cite id="genie-3-2025" />.</span>

### JEPA: predict the representation, never the pixels

V-JEPA trains "solely using a feature prediction objective" <Cite id="vjepa-2024" />. V-JEPA 2 pretrains action-free on over a million hours of video plus images. V-JEPA 2-AC freezes the encoder and trains an action-conditioned predictor on under 62 hours of filtered left-camera Droid video, which carries no reward, task-type or success labels but includes Franka Panda teleoperation <span className="block" data-taxonomy-source-placement="line-start"><Cite id="vjepa2-2025" />.</span>

The same weights and code ran zero-shot on Franka Panda arms in two labs absent from Droid, with hand-chosen cameras and, for pick-and-place, two subgoal images <span className="block" data-taxonomy-source-placement="line-start"><Cite id="vjepa2-2025" />.</span> See the [JEPA module](/world-models/jepa/).

### Unified world-action: one backbone, two heads

A world-action model (WAM) uses a pretrained video backbone to predict both future states and robot actions <Cite id="nvidia-wam-blog-2026" />; unlike a [VLA](/manipulation/vla-models/), it learns physical dynamics <Cite id="dreamzero-2026" />. WorldVLA interleaves action and image tokens and reports that the objectives improve each other <Cite id="worldvla-2025" />, and Cosmos Policy post-trains Cosmos-Predict2 into a policy with no architectural modifications <Cite id="cosmos-policy-2026" />. NVIDIA reports that DreamZero runs a 14B video-diffusion model as a 7 Hz closed-loop policy with over twice the generalization of state-of-the-art VLAs on real robots <Cite id="dreamzero-2026" />. Fast-WAM stays competitive while skipping test-time future generation, and removing video co-training hurts far more <Cite id="fast-wam-2026" />.

<Image id="cosmos-policy-aloha-2026" />

### Symbolic and structured: predict predicates

These models predict transitions over predicates, object relations or 3D occupancy; OccWorld, from autonomous driving, predicts how scene occupancy evolves and plans competitively without instance or map supervision <Cite id="occworld-2023" />. PointWorld predicts the 3D point flows that a sequence of robot actions produces, and at 0.1 s per inference it drives model-predictive control on a real Franka arm with no demonstrations <Cite id="pointworld-2026" />. Silver and colleagues treat learned operators as "a lossy abstraction of the transition model" <Cite id="symbolic-operators-2021" />, and VisualPredicator, which invents its predicates online, generalizes better out of distribution <Cite id="visualpredicator-2024" />.

## Two boundary cases

### A physics engine is a world model

MuJoCo is an explicit, hand-specified state-transition model: given a state and an action, it predicts the next state <Cite id="mujoco-2012" />. TD-MPC2 and classical <Term id="mpc">MPC</Term> therefore share one pattern, with learned or derived dynamics.

### A 3D Gaussian Splatting twin is not

A 3DGS reconstruction is a learned renderer that predicts a scene from a new camera pose <Cite id="3dgs-2023" />; its dynamics come from the physics engine underneath, so alone it fails the functional cut. PhysGaussian adds dynamics by evolving the Gaussians with a Material Point Method solver <Cite id="physgaussian-2023" />, and World Labs' Marble exports splats with collider meshes "intended for coarse physics simulation" <Cite id="marble-2025" />. See [generative simulation](/world-models/generative-sim/).

## Video models serve evaluation and data; latent models plan without images

Google DeepMind reports a Veo-based simulator predicted policies' relative performance, checked against 1,600+ real evaluations of eight Gemini Robotics checkpoints <Cite id="veo-world-simulator-2025" />. The Interactive World Simulator sustains over 10 minutes of interaction at 15 FPS on one RTX 4090, and policies trained on its data matched those trained on as much real data <Cite id="interactive-world-simulator-2026" />. Decoder-free latent and JEPA models plan without showing an image.

## Three tests before believing a world-model claim

The intervention test varies the action with history fixed, since "action-marginalized" hallucination is a measured failure mode <Cite id="wm-hallucination-2026" />. The ranking test asks whether the model orders policies as reality does. The transfer test repeats both across objects, contacts, viewpoints and horizons: in controlled scaling experiments, video models failed out of distribution and showed "case-based" generalization <Cite id="video-physical-law-2024" />. Visual quality counts last, since in six major video generators physical understanding was "severely limited, and unrelated to visual realism" <Cite id="physics-iq-2025" />.

Hou and colleagues separate learned simulation for reinforcement learning, rollout evaluation and video generation for supervision <Cite id="world-model-survey-2026" />. The [evaluation guide](/world-models/evaluation/), [model-based robot learning](/world-models/model-based-robot-learning/) and [world models versus simulators](/world-models/world-models-vs-simulators/) go further.
