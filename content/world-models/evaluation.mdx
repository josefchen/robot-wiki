<span id="start-with-the-intended-use" />
## Perception scores miss function, so each use needs its own test

The central failure is action indifference: realistic futures that ignore the action. Controllability beat visual quality for closed-loop task success <Cite id="world-in-world-2025" />, and a 2026 survey ranks it above plausibility <Cite id="world-model-survey-2026" />.

WorldArena scored 14 models as data engines, evaluators and planners and found a "significant perception-functionality gap" <Cite id="worldarena-2026" />. 1X saw unchanged policy weights degrade within days after subtle lighting changes <Cite id="1x-world-model-2024" />.

Also: DreamGen Bench, whose scores track policy success <Cite id="dreamgen-2025" />.

<span id="level-1-one-step-action-sensitivity" />
## Level 1: open models often fail to follow a changed action

Change one action dimension, hold the rest fixed, and check that the prediction changes. In six open models over 18,000+ cases, WorldSimProbe found actions systematically under-realized <Cite id="worldsimprobe-2026" />.

Also: WorldEval <Cite id="worldeval-2025" />, WorldEcho on actions outside the demonstrations <Cite id="worldecho-2026" />.

<span id="level-2-temporal-and-physical-consistency" />
## Level 2: pooled video metrics hide physical errors

Roll the model forward on its own predictions and score grasp and slip events per horizon. Fréchet Video Distance "increases only slightly with large temporal corruption" <Cite id="fvd-content-bias-2024" />.

| Test | Method | Finding |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WorldModelBench | 67,000 human labels | Catches objects resizing <Cite id="worldmodelbench-2025" /> |
| Physical-law study | Models trained on simple physics | Copy the closest training example out of distribution <Cite id="video-physical-law-2024" /> |
| WMEval (Oct 2026) | Same actions in Unity and the model | Error against true state <Cite id="wmeval-2026" /> |

<span id="level-3-task-progress-scoring" />
## Level 3: the progress scorer needs its own test

WorldGym pairs a video generator with a vision-language progress scorer; test each half separately <Cite id="worldgym-2025" />. GVL sharpens such scores by ordering shuffled frames, over 300+ real tasks <Cite id="gvl-2024" />. Runway and DreamDojo used human graders <Cite id="runway-gwm-robotics-eval-2026" /> <Cite id="dreamdojo-2026" />.

<span id="level-4-policy-ranking" />
## Level 4: the best simulators order policies as real trials do

Agreement is scored as Pearson r (1 is perfect) and Mean Maximum Rank Violation, MMRV (lower is better) <Cite id="runway-gwm-robotics-eval-2026" />. The STEP sequential test cuts the 10 to 50 real trials a comparison needs by up to 32% <Cite id="optimal-stopping-2025" />.

<Image id="roboarena-evaluation-2025" />

| Evaluator | Kind | Agreement with real trials |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Veo, 8 Gemini Robotics checkpoints | World model | MMRV 0.03 over 1,600+ real runs; underpredicts absolute success <Cite id="veo-world-simulator-2025" /> |
| WorldGym | World model | r = 0.78 <Cite id="worldgym-2025" /> |
| DreamDojo | World model | r = 0.995, NVIDIA's figure <Cite id="dreamdojo-2026" /> |
| Ctrl-World, tested by PolaRiS | World model | MMRV 0.22, "heavy hallucinations" in contact <Cite id="polaris-2025" /> |
| PhysCanvas, with touch | World model | r from 0.602 to 0.885 on 4 tasks after reward post-training <Cite id="physcanvas-2026" /> |
| SimFoundry, scenes rebuilt from video | Physics simulator | r = 0.911, MMRV 0.018, 7 tasks, 5 policy types <Cite id="simfoundry-2026" /> |
| PolaRiS | Physics simulator | r = 0.9 <Cite id="polaris-2025" /> |
| X2Real | Physics simulator | r = 0.84 <Cite id="x2real-2026" /> |

Physics simulation sets the bar but has artifacts: one collision constant ejected objects on RoboTwin; fixing it raised container-task success from 89% to 98% <Cite id="simuguard-2026" />. In WorldFailBench, seven world models largely failed to reproduce why and when policies fail <Cite id="worldfailbench-2026" />.

<span id="level-5-closed-loop-usefulness" />
## Level 5: planning and fine-tuning in a world model improved outcomes

The last test puts the model in the loop and measures the real robot.

| Use | Model | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Planning | IRASim | Push-T goal overlap from 0.637 to 0.961 <Cite id="irasim-2024" /> |
| Fine-tuning on imagined successes | Ctrl-World | Policy success up 44.7% <Cite id="ctrl-world-2025" /> |
| Action-observation post-training | World-in-World | Beat a stronger pretrained generator <Cite id="world-in-world-2025" /> |

Also: SIMPLER's sim-real correlation test <Cite id="simpler-2024" />, RoboArena's multi-lab real evaluation <Cite id="roboarena-2025" />.

<span id="control-the-evaluation-shifts" />
## Saturated benchmarks hide robustness gaps

Vary one factor at a time; one "unseen" split hides what breaks a policy. Nearly all policies PolaRiS tested scored 90% to 95% on LIBERO, poorly correlated with real results <Cite id="polaris-2025" />; LIBERO-Plus perturbations exposed the gaps <Cite id="libero-plus-2025" />.

<EvalShiftTable className="my-6" />

RoboLab-120 perturbs 120 tasks along visual, procedural and relational axes <Cite id="robolab-2026" />; its best world-action model succeeded in 46.2% of 1,200 episodes, per Runway <Cite id="runway-praxis-1-open-2026" />.

<span id="leakage-and-circularity" />
## Disjoint data and blind trials guard against leakage

World models may pretrain on benchmark video. Ctrl-World trains on DROID's 564 scenes <Cite id="ctrl-world-2025" /> <Cite id="droid-2024" />, and Runway's RoboArena ranking put a DROID-trained policy first <Cite id="runway-gwm-robotics-eval-2026" />. X2Real keeps "strictly disjoint training-evaluation pipelines" <Cite id="x2real-2026" />; TRI ran blind, randomized trials <Cite id="tri-lbm-2025" />.

<span id="the-minimum-credible-report" />
## A credible report pins the benchmark version and the real anchor

Report the use case, action format, horizon, real anchor and benchmark version. Physics-IQ Verified refined 57.6% of samples, and its rankings correlate only 0.46 with the original <Cite id="physics-iq-verified-2026" />. By 1X's arithmetic, a 70%-accurate world model picks the better of two policies 15% apart 90% of the time <Cite id="1x-world-model-2025" />.
