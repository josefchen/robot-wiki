Model-based robot learning is the family of methods that learn how the world responds to actions and use that model to plan, to train a policy in imagination, or both. Its two steps are learning a dynamics model and integrating planning with learning <Cite id="mbrl-survey-2020" />.

<Image id="daydreamer-a1-rollover-2022" />

The loop predicts futures under candidate actions, scores them, acts, observes the real transition and updates the model. Sutton proposed integrated architectures for learning, planning and reacting in 1990 <Cite id="dyna-1990" />.

<span id="the-model-based-decomposition" />
## Model-based means predicting the effect of an action

A 2026 survey defines a world model as "an action-conditioned predictive system" <Cite id="manip-wm-survey-2026" />. Classical model-predictive control (MPC) uses explicit dynamics, such as MuJoCo's rigid-body and contact model <Cite id="mujoco-2012" />; model-based learning fits some or all of that transition to data.

A <Term id="world-model">world model</Term> need not predict pixels: value-equivalent models predict only what planning needs, "without compromising performance" <Cite id="value-equivalence-2020" />.

<span id="two-ways-to-use-imagined-experience" />
## A model can plan each step or train a policy in imagination

A planner rolls sampled action sequences through the model, executes the best first action and replans, so a new goal or cost needs no retraining, at the price of many rollouts per real step ([reward design and MPC](/rl-sim2real/reward-design-mpc/)). In 2016 Finn and Levine combined action-conditioned video prediction with MPC so a real robot could push novel objects <Cite id="deep-visual-foresight-2016" />. TD-MPC2 trained one 317M-parameter agent on 80 tasks <Cite id="tdmpc2-2023" />, and PointWorld, trained on about 2 million trajectories, plans with MPC at 0.1 s per inference on a real Franka without demonstrations <Cite id="pointworld-2026" />.

Dreamer instead trains a policy on trajectories imagined in a learned latent space <Cite id="dreamer-2019" />, and DreamerV3 covers more than 150 tasks with one configuration <Cite id="dreamerv3-2023" />. Dreamer 4 learns purely offline, a setting its authors tie to robotics, where interaction "can be unsafe and slow" <Cite id="dreamer4-2025" />. [Latent dynamics](/world-models/latent-dynamics/) covers these agents.

<span id="uncertainty-aware-models" />
## Uncertainty estimates keep policies from exploiting model errors

A policy trained on a model "tends to exploit regions where insufficient data is available", which ME-TRPO counters with an ensemble <Cite id="me-trpo-2018" />. PETS, a probabilistic ensemble, matches model-free asymptotic performance with 8 and 125 times fewer samples than SAC and PPO on half-cheetah <Cite id="pets-2018" />. MBPO keeps rollouts short and "branched from real data" <Cite id="mbpo-2019" />. In [offline RL](/rl-sim2real/offline-rl/), MOPO penalizes reward by model uncertainty <Cite id="mopo-2020" />.

<span id="why-physical-robots-make-the-model-harder" />
## Robots see only part of the state, yet learn useful models in minutes to hours

A robot observation is partial: cameras miss forces, friction and compliance, and RGBD gave an 80% improvement in fabric-folding success over RGB <Cite id="visuospatial-foresight-2020" />. Data is narrow too: Hansen and Wang call world-model hallucination "inherently a data coverage issue" and adapt a model to unseen simulated environments with 50 trajectories <Cite id="wm-hallucination-2026" />.

Chenyuan Zhou, Yining Hong, Jiajun Wu and colleagues at Stanford and elsewhere (October 2026) condition a video world model on a 32-dimensional latent for hidden factors such as friction or mass, fitted at test time from one or two interactions while the model stays frozen <Cite id="t2z-2026" />. Averaged over four real-robot tasks, the adapted model's mean tracking error fell from 19.30 to 10.85 pixels against the same model without a latent <Cite id="t2z-2026" />. Actions it picked succeeded 70.80% of the time against 26.47% for the model without a latent, though the paper does not state trial counts <Cite id="t2z-2026" />.

PDDM learned contact-rich manipulation on a 24-DoF hand from 4 real hours <Cite id="pddm-2019" />. DayDreamer ran Dreamer online on 4 robots without simulators; a quadruped learned to stand up and walk in 1 hour <Cite id="daydreamer-2022" />. SSRL adds a learned force ensemble to known Lagrangian dynamics and learned Go1 gaits from a few minutes of data <Cite id="ssrl-2024" />.

In September 2026, Claudio Canales, Marco Hutter and colleagues learned a probabilistic dynamics ensemble from scratch on an 11.5-ton hydraulic excavator. After 20 minutes, their sampling-based MPC tracked comparably to learned controllers trained on 100 to 150 minutes; after 40 it held sub-centimeter mean path error at high speed <Cite id="excavator-mbrl-2026" />. Hansen, Fox and colleagues trained one TD-MPC2 model in simulation on up to 90 insertion tasks, with 56% zero-shot success on unseen objects against 7% model-free <Cite id="insertion-world-models-2026" />.

## Model error is task-weighted

A two-millimeter error is irrelevant in free space and decisive at a tight connector. One-step prediction likelihood "is not always correlated with control performance", and task-effective models "do not necessarily need to be globally accurate" <Cite id="objective-mismatch-2020" />. The 2026 robot world-model survey stresses action-conditioned consistency and functional-utility evaluation <Cite id="world-model-survey-2026" />; the [evaluation guide](/world-models/evaluation/) gives a protocol.

<span id="neural-simulators-and-hybrid-models" />
## Neural and hybrid simulators now train real-robot policies

Robotic World Model trains a neural-network simulator for policy optimization <Cite id="robotic-world-model-2025" />, and RWM-U adds epistemic uncertainty to train real quadruped and humanoid policies entirely offline <Cite id="rwm-u-2025" />. RISE improves a VLA inside a [video world model](/world-models/generative-video/) scored by a progress-value model, gaining 35 to 45 points on three real tasks <Cite id="rise-2026" />.

<Image id="roboworld-synthetic-scenes-2026" />

Hybrids keep known physics and learn what it misses: TossingBot learns residuals on a physics-based throw and reached 500+ mean picks per hour <Cite id="tossingbot-2019" />, and ASAP fits a delta action model to real Unitree G1 data <Cite id="asap-2025" />. [World models versus physics simulators](/world-models/world-models-vs-simulators/) compares the options.

<span id="when-to-choose-model-based-learning" />
## Model-based learning pays off when real interaction is expensive

It fits when interaction is expensive, goals change faster than dynamics and planning fits the latency budget; it fits less well when observations omit contact state or resets are cheap. Plan only once predicted action rankings track real ones: in a 2026 agile-driving study, SAC won in simulation, yet "only TD-MPC2 consistently outperforms the MPPI baseline on the physical platform" <Cite id="reset-free-driving-2026" />.
