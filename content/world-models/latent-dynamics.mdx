<Term id="latent-dynamics">Latent dynamics</Term> is the world-model paradigm that learns a compact recurrent state, predicts how it and the reward evolve under actions, and trains or plans inside that imagination <Cite id="dreamer-2019" />. It is the oldest of the six paradigms in the [taxonomy](/world-models/taxonomy/): Embed to Control learned locally linear latent dynamics from raw images in 2015 <Cite id="e2c-2015" />.

<span id="the-rssm-a-state-you-can-dream-in" />
## Agents trained inside a recurrent latent model reached human-level Atari

Ha and Schmidhuber trained an agent "entirely inside of its own hallucinated dream" and transferred it back to the real environment <Cite id="ha-world-models-2018" />. PlaNet planned from pixels with deterministic and stochastic latent transitions <Cite id="planet-2018" />. DreamerV3's Recurrent State-Space Model (RSSM) pairs a recurrent state that carries memory with a stochastic categorical representation, and learns to reconstruct inputs and predict reward and continuation <Cite id="dreamerv3-2023" />.

Dreamer's actor and critic are trained purely on imagined latent rollouts from real encoded states, backpropagating value gradients through the model <Cite id="dreamer-2019" />. DreamerV2, which added discrete latents, was the first agent to reach human-level performance on the 55-task Atari benchmark by learning inside a separately trained world model <Cite id="dreamerv2-2020" />.

## DreamerV3: one recipe across 150 tasks

DreamerV3 was evaluated on more than 150 tasks across eight domains with fixed hyperparameters. It uses symlog transforms for vector observations, free bits with KL balancing and percentile-based return normalization <Cite id="dreamerv3-2023" />. In the 2025 Nature article its authors call it, to their knowledge, the first algorithm to collect diamonds in Minecraft from scratch without human data or adaptive curricula <Cite id="dreamerv3-nature-2025" />. Its MineRL-based task used abstract crafting actions, accelerated block breaking and sparse intermediate rewards for 12 milestones from log to diamond <Cite id="dreamerv3-2023" />.

DreamerV3 learned online; in 2025 Dreamer 4 became "the first agent to obtain diamonds in Minecraft purely from offline data", with a world model that runs in real time on a single GPU <Cite id="dreamer4-2025" />.

<span id="td-mpc-and-td-mpc2-drop-the-decoder-plan-at-every-step" />
## TD-MPC2 drops the decoder and plans at every step

MuZero planned with a model predicting only "the reward, the action-selection policy, and the value function" <Cite id="muzero-2019" />, and value-equivalence theory holds that capacity is "better used to build models that are directly useful for value-based planning" <Cite id="value-equivalence-2020" />. TD-MPC trains reward, TD value and latent-state consistency predictions without reconstructing observations, and at each step runs short-horizon MPPI with a learned terminal value <Cite id="tdmpc-2022" />. TD-MPC2 keeps this planner, adds candidate sequences from a policy prior and warm-starts from the previous solution. <span data-latent-source-placement="below" className="block [&_[role=tooltip]]:top-full [&_[role=tooltip]]:bottom-auto [&_[role=tooltip]]:mt-1.5 [&_[role=tooltip]]:mb-0"><Cite id="tdmpc2-2023" /></span>

The multitask experiment trained one 317M-parameter agent on 545M transitions from 240 single-task agents. This offline 80-task experiment is separate from the 104-task online benchmark, and aggregate normalized scores rose across five model sizes <Cite id="tdmpc2-2023" />. MuDreamer replaced Dreamer's reconstruction with value and action prediction <Cite id="mudreamer-2024" />.

<span id="daydreamer-imagination-on-physical-hardware" />
## DayDreamer taught a real quadruped to walk in about an hour

DayDreamer builds on DreamerV2 and learns online without a simulator on four robots with shared hyperparameters. After about one hour of real training, a Unitree A1 learned to roll off its back, stand and walk, with manual repositioning at the area boundary that left its joints and orientation unchanged. SAC learned to roll over but neither stood nor walked within that budget <Cite id="daydreamer-2022" />.

<span id="the-horizon-problem" />
## Latent models imagine only 3 to 16 steps ahead

The 2023 DreamerV3 preprint lists imagination horizon H = 15 in a table and prediction horizon T = 16 in its actor-critic section, the 2024 revision keeps both labels, and the 2025 Nature article uses T = 16 <Cite id="dreamerv3-2023" /> <Cite id="dreamerv3-nature-2025" />. TD-MPC2 plans with H = 3 and bootstraps beyond it with a terminal value <Cite id="tdmpc2-2023" />. DayDreamer states H = 16 in its methods and H = 15 in its appendix <Cite id="daydreamer-2022" />.

MBPO keeps model rollouts short and "branched from real data" <Cite id="mbpo-2019" />, and STEVE varies the horizon per example to limit model error <Cite id="steve-2018" />. HWM, with world models at several time scales in one latent space, lifted real Franka pick-and-place from 0% to 70% <Cite id="hwm-2026" />. A 350M-parameter model trained on 427 hours across 210 tasks still showed perceptual, action-marginalized and scene-diverging hallucinations <Cite id="wm-hallucination-2026" />.

<span id="successors-and-open-limits" />
## Simulation-trained world models now transfer to real legged robots

Robotic World Model (RWM) predicts observations over long horizons under partial observability with a GRU that feeds its predictions back in. Policies trained with MBPO-PPO in simulation showed zero-shot transfer to ANYmal D and Unitree G1, including under external disturbances; safe online hardware learning remains open <Cite id="robotic-world-model-2025" />. RWM-U adds uncertainty and trains policies "entirely from offline datasets" for a real quadruped and humanoid <Cite id="rwm-u-2025" />. SimDist pretrains in simulation and on hardware updates only the latent dynamics; on contact-rich manipulation and quadruped locomotion it kept improving where prior adaptation methods stalled or degraded <Cite id="simdist-2026" />.

## Refinements to latent planning bring uneven gains

Dream-MPC refines policy-proposed action sequences by gradient ascent on predicted return, with an uncertainty penalty. Used in place of MPPI in pretrained TD-MPC2 and BMPC on 24 simulated tasks, over three seeds and ten episodes, the BMPC variant raises interquartile mean by 26.7% and mean by 20.5%. The TD-MPC2 variant beats its policy alone but does not consistently match MPPI <Cite id="dream-mpc-2026" />. BMPC learns its policy by imitating the MPC planner <Cite id="bmpc-2025" />, and HumanoidBench, one of the three task suites, poses whole-body humanoid tasks <Cite id="humanoidbench-2024" />.

TD-M(PC)² found that planner-generated data causes "persistent value overestimation" and fixed it with a policy constraint, gaining most on 61-DoF humanoids <Cite id="td-m-pc2-2025" />. Fast-WAM, a video world-action model, keeps video co-training but encodes the current frame once and runs action denoising on a chunk, with success similar to its imagine-then-execute variants on LIBERO and RoboTwin 2.0. On Galaxea R1 Lite towel folding it reports 190 ms inference latency against 810 ms for Fast-WAM-IDM on one RTX 5090D V2 32GB GPU <Cite id="fast-wam-2026" />.

## TD-MPC2's authors expect broad generalization to need far more tasks

TD-MPC2's authors expect broad generalization, such as transfer to new embodiments, "will likely require several orders of magnitude more tasks" <Cite id="tdmpc2-2023" />. In November 2025 the same group's Newt trained one language-conditioned world model jointly across 200 tasks <Cite id="newt-2025" />. In simulation, one world model trained on up to 90 insertion tasks reached 56% zero-shot success on unseen parts, against 7% for a model-free baseline <Cite id="insertion-world-models-2026" />. DayDreamer's authors report that long hardware training causes wear needing human intervention or repair <Cite id="daydreamer-2022" />.

<span id="pick-the-models-role-before-its-architecture" />
## Imagination tolerates slow models; online planning needs predictable latency

Imagination-only training, as in Dreamer, tolerates slow generation but can bias the policy silently. Online planning, as in TD-MPC2, needs predictable latency and a fallback, and a planner that never changes its policy prior's proposal pays model cost for nothing. A latent model can also supply a representation: a simulation-trained world model predicted real legged-locomotion trajectories well enough to inform a separate controller <Cite id="wmp-2024" />.
