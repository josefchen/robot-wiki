## A wrong link anywhere moves the grasp

Between the pixel where a policy sees a mug and the gripper reaching for it sit <Term id="camera-intrinsics">camera intrinsics</Term>, depth, the camera mount, joint zeroes, tool dimensions, timing and actuator response. A wrong camera transform misses even with FoundationPose tracking the object <Cite id="foundationpose-2024" />.

Also: [visual servoing](/classical/perception) <Cite id="espiau-1992" />.

## The camera model must match the lens and the depth sensor

Intrinsics map a pixel to a ray. Zhang's method recovers them from a flat pattern at a few angles <Cite id="zhang-2000-calibration" />. Refit after any focus or crop change, judged by held-out reprojection error <Cite id="reproducible-intrinsics-2026" />.

Stereo depth error grows with the square of distance <Cite id="realsense-tuning-2026" />, and shiny surfaces saturate the image <Cite id="realsense-d400-datasheet-2026" />. Kinect's error reaches about 4 cm at full range <Cite id="khoshelham-kinect-2012" />.

Also: fisheye lenses <Cite id="kannala-brandt-2006" />, single-image AnyCalib <Cite id="anycalib-2025" />, Kinect depth distortion <Cite id="herrera-depth-color-2012" />.

## Hand-eye calibration solves AX = XB

Hand-eye calibration locates the camera on the gripper. Shiu and Ahmad cast it as $AX = XB$, with arm motions $A$, camera motions $B$ and the unknown mount $X$ <Cite id="shiu-ahmad-1989" />. Tsai and Lenz solved it in closed form <Cite id="tsai-lenz-1989" />.

<Image id="baxter-gripper-wrist-camera" />

It needs at least three poses about non-parallel axes <Cite id="opencv-hand-eye-docs-2026" />; accuracy levels off near 12 to 15 <Cite id="moveit-hand-eye-tutorial-2026" />.

## Real arms drift from their nominal kinematics

Manufacturing errors and joint flex shift real arms from nominal [kinematics](/classical/kinematics) <Cite id="modern-robotics-2017" />. Calibrating a humanoid from 31 postures cut its RMS error 2.3-fold <Cite id="humanoid-geometric-calibration-2025" />, and OmniCalib found CAD camera values off by up to 10.56 mm <Cite id="omnicalib-2026" />.

Also: humanoid joint offsets <Cite id="humanoid-joint-offset-2026" />.

## A late frame misses by speed times delay

A frame 50 ms late shows the gripper where it was, so at 10 cm a second the grasp lands 5 mm off. A RealSense rig measured 55 ms of camera delay <Cite id="realtime-vla-v2-2026" />, and compensating such delay raised policy success by 8.1 points <Cite id="synchec-2026" />.

## Actuator response must be measured

Without a measured actuator response, policy error and tracking error look the same. Hwangbo and colleagues learned ANYmal's actuator dynamics from measurements <Cite id="hwangbo-2019" />. SPI-Active, choosing that data actively, beat baselines by 42 to 63% in locomotion transfer <Cite id="spi-active-2025" />.

Also: identified inertias <Cite id="atkeson-an-hollerbach-1986" />, Franka Panda dynamics <Cite id="gaz-panda-dynamics-2019" />, periodic excitation <Cite id="swevers-excitation-1997" />.

## Calibrations drift, so store them with their evidence

Most public datasets lack extrinsics; CalibAll recovered them for about 97,000 episodes <Cite id="calib-all-2025" />. Store each calibration with serials, settings, software version and residuals; recalibrate after a remount, collision or tool change.

Also: DROID's per-session checkerboards <Cite id="droid-2024" />.
