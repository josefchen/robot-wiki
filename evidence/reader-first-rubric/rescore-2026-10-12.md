## How the terms were scored

A term whose quoted words are exactly exempt words or counters, such as
"Adjust more", "Play button" or "Round 1 of 3", was matched by
`lib/reader-first-rubric.ts`, the same code `check:reader-first` uses.

A mark, kicker, self-label or credit was exempted only where the stored answer
shows it is an item the rubric names. Examples: "I needed the key to learn they
are joint motors" (a legend-keyed mark), or a question about nothing but the
colour of a stage annotation. At the records' commits the annotations were
drawn in the highlight role, `#507C00`, the dark tone of the former lime
highlight; the rubric reads that highlight as today's highlight mark. The
kicker and the self-label were checked against the frame's source at the
record's commit.

Everything else still counts: technical words, labels written for one figure,
marks that no label or key explains, legend entries that do not match the
drawing, and marks whose shape or direction the reader could not read. Where a
term was close to an exemption, the JSON gives the reason it still counts.

## What the check reports now

`check:reader-first` still fails all 19 records on their stored verdicts. It
also reports each one as reviewed before the fixed rubric, because a record
now stores the digest of the rubric it was reviewed under. Every figure
therefore needs a new review under the rubric in the convergence round.
