One Kalman filter, a whole run: illustrative clip, 16 seconds.
The filter mixes a motion prediction with sensor readings to track the object. When readings stop, its widening band shows growing uncertainty.
Transcript:
- An object moves back and forth as time runs left to right, and the dashed line marks its true position.
- A noisy sensor reports the object's position as dots, and about one time in five it reports nothing.
- The filter's estimate, the solid line, stays close to the true position, and the shaded band shows how unsure it is.
- While readings are missing the filter grows less sure and its band widens, which tells a robot to trust the estimate less.
Illustrative: a simulated run, not measured on hardware.
